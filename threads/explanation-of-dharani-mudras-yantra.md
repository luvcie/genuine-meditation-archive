# Explanation of Dharani, Mudras, & Yantra

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 1 year ago

Here I will reveal the secret of the three.

Dharani is the mystery of words, Mudra is the mystery of body, and Yantra is the mystery of mind. As such, this is the secret of body, speech and mind.

Dharani

There are 4 types of Dharani:

Literature Dharani: Wherever one goes, the Buddha's Dharma will always be remembered.

Meaning Dharani: Wherever one goes, the Buddha's logical meaning will be remembered.

Mantra Dharani: Recital eliminates wicked actions, sorrows and obstacles, wiping them from the mind.

Essence Dharani: One embodies the true nature of virtues, and Dharani has become Nibbana.

Dharani originated in India and was handed down in Sanskrit for thousands of years, for fear of losing its power in translation. Master over the years, being proficient, re-arrange these energies and pass them to their disciples.

Therefore, reciting a Dharani is one of the fastest ways to see the supernatural force, for its power is huge.

Mudras

The energetic body is full of channels, each with varying manifestations of movements of chi. Depending on its manifestation, one might call it fire, water, air, earth or akasa.

Now the fingers have channels that leak chi at its tips and the palms. Each has a representation:

Thumb is Akasa and represents the Vairocana Tathagata. Left thumb refers to the world of Man. Right thumb refers to the world of Buddhas.

Index finger is Air and represents the Buddha of the East, Akshobhya. Left index finger refers to the world of Spirits. Right index finger refers to the world of the Bodhisattvas.

Middle finger is Fire and represents the Buddha of the South, Ratnasambhava. Left middle finger refers to the world of Animals. Right middle finger refers to the world of the solitary hermits.

Ring finger is Water and represents the Buddha of the West, Amitabha. Left ring finger refers to the world of Hungry Ghosts/Pretas. Right ring finger refers to the world of the Buddha's disciples.

Pinky finger is Earth and represents the Buddha of the North, Sakyamuni. Left pinky refers to Hell, Right pinky refers to the world of the Gods.

When chanting a dharani or mudra, or meditating, one is actually using the fingers to form the seal and establishing contact with the divinities and the Buddhas.

Connecting the tips of fingers in various ways hence form different seals that lead to different effects.

Yantra

Yantras are made when one understands the laws of the universe through meditation insight and organize them to fit with the natural forces.

To one with faith, having seen its power, the Yantra can represent the microcosm to which the Vairocana Buddha appears in the center, surrounded by Gods and Saints.

One should hold the triple Gem in mind - Buddha, Dharma and Sangha. The power of the Buddhas will hence manifest through the person because of one following the virtue path of the Noble Eightfold Path. Dharma protectors will rise and protect this rare individual, and whatever one wishes can turn into fruition.

This is the Yoga Samadhi of Secret Buddhism. The practitioner devotes huge effort to researching this subject in meditation - and this magic will be used to preserve the dharma teachings and stop the false ones.

How does one start?

One of the most infamous dharanis is known as the Cundi Dharani as spoken by the Mother of Seven Kotis Buddhas. Simply sit and recite the Dharani with the following mudra:

http://www.shenlun.org/more/mudra/mudra_cundi.jpg

Here is the Dharani.

Here is a magic that can be accomplished by the Cundi dharani, to illustrate its power:

If one goes to the top of a mountain and recites this Dharani a hundred billion times, Vajrapani Bodhisattva will appear and take this individual, along with 560 people to the Asura palace where the mystic lives an entire lifecycle. He will then see Maitreya Bodhisattva and hear his lecture on the full, right doctrine, and allow one to attain to the position of an unregressing Bodhisattva.

Just one recital of this Dharani, too, will fasten the seed of awakening within an individual.

Why is the Cundi Dharani infamous?

There are many famous methods that propagate faster than other methods in Buddhism, such as Anapanasati. The reason is because of the successes that people get when doing them.

As for the Cundi Dharani, it has been used particularly in East Asian Buddhism in Vietnam, Japan and China, and the reason for its huge proliferation is its ability in changing the fortune of the practitioner, and guiding them towards the path of Dharma. In fact, this Dharani is so powerful that it has been used in Mind-only schools such as Zen.

Various other powerful Dharanis are the infamous "Om mani padme hum". I will do further posts on them, as I wish to shed light on the branch of esoteric Buddhism (outside of Vajrayana) and cut out the wrong understandings.

The problem with most esoteric Buddhism is that one has to master them correctly. Otherwise, it can become a side-path. This is why the Gelug school of Tibetan Buddhism is correct - one has to learn all the teachings of the Buddha, follow the footsteps of the Buddha of sila - samadhi - prajna, then only then are they qualified to receive transmissions.

Could you tell us a bit about Cundi herself?

Cundi is often seen with 18 arms, each arm representing the eighteen merits of Buddhahood. For example, the axe she wields represents the cutting away of evil. She is often seen in China as a manifestation of Avalokitesvara.

The best thing about Cundi's dharani is that one does not need any initiation, but yet allows one to connect with her compassionate wisdom.

She is often seen participating in various discourses by the Buddha Sakyamuni, such as during the expounding of the Dharani of Great Satisfaction and the Great Compassion Mantra. Her appearance can scare some people, since it would be the first time anyone has seen more than 2 arms on a human-like figure.

And finally, how would one go about repeating it a hundred billion times? It seems impossible without some kind of siddhi or living many thousands of years, I really would like to know the method, there's no price that seems too big to pay for that kind of result.

After 1 million repetitions, one can move to the short form of the Cundi mantra, or if given permission by Cundi herself.

If however, Cundi appears, one should chant the following 7 times "Om Lah". This will ensure that it is not the impersonation of a Demon King, for all demonic forces are crushed in the chanting of that powerful mantra.

This is the short form mantra:

Om Jerlee Jewlee Juntee Soha, Om Bo-lin.

This is huge, huge, huge merit. Knowing all of this, the only way you can repay all of this is to become enlightened yourself.

What is so good about the Cundi dharani? It does not matter if you are monastic, lay, whether the place is dirty or clean. There is no need for any altar. It is so wondrously convenient. Some Dharanis lose their power when the practitioner does taboo, such as eating a mouthful of garlic. The Cundi dharani does not lose its effect in any of that.

Some forms of black magic use dog-blood, pig-blood, and all kinds of taboo to break the power of some tantric methods. What is the reason? This is the reason. However, the Cundi Dharani does not suffer in effect in anyway. There is another that does not fail, known as Subduing magic, but that is not relevant here.

The Cundi dharani is impenetrable and unbreakable by any spell or form. It can turn any form of evil into huge wisdom. Such is this power of the dharani.

There is another work known as the Xian Mi Yuan Tong Cheng Fo-Xin Yao Ji, which is unfortunately unavailable to the English public. Its Chinese name is 显密圆通成佛心要, so if there is a chance for understanding this, it will become easier to become a Buddha. Just as good, is doing the practicing of Zen Buddhism along with the Cundi mantra - this will bring results without fail.

I will do another post on many other various mantras in detail in future.

One can certainly keep a few practices. However, just one should be the main practice, and one should consistently practice that one method until stream-entry. Otherwise, it is also possible to just use one and nothing else, but for most people, a mixture of practices can help speed up progress.
