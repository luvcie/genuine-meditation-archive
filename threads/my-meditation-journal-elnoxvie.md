# My Meditation Journal: Elnoxvie

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **elnoxvie** · *(OCR)* · `Journal` · 5 points · 2 years ago

I feel that it would be beneficial to post my meditation journal here to get helped / advice from fellow dhamma followers. I also hope by posting here, the post can serve as a medium for comparing our notes.

To start with, I will give a little highlight on my journey and practice so far.

Topic Highlight

Meditation Journey start Jan 2012

Meditation 7AM - 8AM Morning, 12.30 AM onwards (No time limit). schedule

Meditation Type Breath Meditation , Walking meditation.

So far only some readings on Books from Ayya khema, Ajahn brahm and Ajahn Chah. Some Teacher advise given my uncle who is also on the path

Goal Stream Winner in this life

My Current routine:

1. Recite: Namo tassa bhagavato arahato samma sambuddhassa x 3 2. Follow by: Namo amitabha x 3 3. Body scan from head to toe and toe to head, paying attention to every parts of the body. Feeling the hair, the nose and so on. 4. Then, i will picture my self alone in the room and put my focus on feeling my body's present. 5. After i feel sufficiently focus, i will start to listen to my breath, focusing in breath, focusing out breath while incorporating the 4 frames of mind. 6. When I feel significantly stable. I will turn to follow my breathing closely. Noticing the inhaling starts, inhaling stops, pause, exhaling starts, exhaling ends, pause and repeat again. 7. If the meditation is smooth, after sometime my breathing will turn shallow or hard to define. At this time, either Nimitta or some tingling sensation will start to appear. I will then shift my focus onto Nimitta. 8. At this time, the Nimitta will expand( I will usually encounter two types: Tactile or the visual Nimitta). 9. When i reached here, i stop doing anything. Just absorbed into the state. This is the current routine that I have.

*Please feel free to comment and advise. Will be appreciated. * &amp;amp;amp;amp;nbsp;

My Meditation Journal:

28/1/2015: Started my meditation as usual. Incorporating the 4 frames of mind and not long after, I noticed a high pitched sound. Feel that this could be the Nimitta that refugeinbuddha mentioned. I then switched my anchor to the sound. The sound then seemed to expand, it's like getting closer, louder. Breathing was still not shallow at this stage so I kept my anchor on both sound and breathing. After a while, the breathing started to become shallow. Here on, tactile Nimitta seemed to appear. Again I was switching my anchor to the sensation. A shimmering light appeared. Again I was switching my focus to the light. Feeling that it expands, the sensation filling the whole body (tingling). After sometimes, the sensation seemed to fade. There are some remnants of thoughts, I feel that the state of mind is still not at top condition but then this is the first time, I noticed the Sound Nimmita and multiple Nimitta at the same time. Near the ending, there was this sudden white bright light shifted. Not sure what is that though.

30/1/2015: Reflecting 4 frames of mind while inhale and exhale seemed to work better than just watching my breath. With each breath in and out, i calm myself. With each breath in and out, i calm my mind. Just like this. it seems to work better for me.

7/2/2015: Doing an open eye meditation on bed, Evening time. Was having some thoughts on the relationship between relaxation and jhana so I picture myself sitting on top of a mountain with a spectacular scene. This somehow made me feel very relax. Then followed by mindfulness on the breathing. After a while body is still, tacticle sensation arise and it seems to be quite easy to get into jhanas that way. Still not progressing too far off from 2nd jhana.

11/2/2015: Today's meditation was pretty interesting. I thought I only have 30 mins to meditate but to my surprise. The meditation went pretty well. I was on the first jhana in minutes. Mind was stable, thoughts were halted / caught before they appeared. Was on the 3rd when my alarm rang. Relaxation and finding a closely easy to follow posture on breathing was probably the key

15/2/2015: I was doing the whole day meditation for multiple sessions ( 5 - 7 sessions). I was able to get to Jhanas almost every session that lasts around 1 hours - 1.30 hours. Few things i noted:

1. Before moving to the 3rd Jhanas, the rapture needs to be strong as to calm the pyshical pain due to bad posture. Leaving the 2nd jhana, the rapture is still there to calm the body but we are just moving to the next level of consciousness.

2. The sound nimitta can appear even before the mind has calmed. Focusing on them won't fill the body with rapture. Only when the mind is calmed, focusing on nimitta will fill the mind with rapture. 3. The sitting posture needs to be correct, otherwise it will interfere with Jhanas or when rapture is not strong enough. 4. Switching awareness to tip of the nose allow the breathing to be much more noticable. 5. One can switch awareness to different types of nimitta when they appear. For visual nimitta, one has to focus on center of the face otherwise, sometimes, it's hard to notice them. 6. To increase one rapture, one has just to keep focusing.

Still couldn't pass the 3rd Jhanas / i am not sure if i am on 4th. Trying to recall my past lifes but nothing appears. Maybe i was just too rush.

23/2/2015: Meditating on the Bliss. Strange and Powerful, within minutes it brought me to 2nd jhana. I have encountered another strange phenomena. A very strong tactile sensation, almost exploding then void. Feeling the body was sucked out, breathless (as if a strong wind passed by) and than it stops again. The Tactile sensation is almost doubled. Think i have reached 4th jhana and on the transition to 5th but was falling down again to lower jhanas. The experience was pure bliss. After finishing the meditation, the mind is not dull but bright, energized.

25/2/2015: Meditating in the evening for 1.5 hours, followed by meditating lying down for 2 - 3 hours. The bliss was strong, i still have hard time transitioning from 2nd to 3rd jhana but overall the path to jhana is pretty much secured. There was a strange phenomena during the lying down meditation, I heard sounds where many were talking (this is probably at 1 - 2 am). I am not sure what they were saying but it felt familiars.

26/2/2015: Doing sitting meditation without much success and turned into lying down meditation instead. I was able to get into 2nd and 3rd (feels like) jhana. I have another strange phenomena occurring. I smelt a few different types of smells which i am not sure what those were. Maybe just something in the rooms. Trying to sleep in 2nd jhana but proved to be difficult due to tingling intensity.

10/3/2015: Meditating on the tingling inside the hands. Was able to get into 2nd Jhanas quite smoothly. Bliss was strong. At some point, it feels like another blissful sensation passing through around the heart areas. I was then get into very deep in meditation, feels like entering some void but just for a while.
