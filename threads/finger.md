# Finger

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 9 points · 2016-06-18

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/4oncn0/finger/>

You, there is no you.

Me, there is no me.

Us, there is no us.

Self, there is no self.

No self, there is no no-self.

Sight, there is no sight.

Taste, there is no taste.

Touch, there is no touch.

Smell, there is no smell.

Hearing, there is no hearing.

Thought, there is no thought.

Buddha, there is no Buddha.

Dharma, there is no Dharma.

Sangha, there is no Sangha.

All there is is all there is.

All there isn't is all there isn't.

As things arise, they arise.

As things fall away, they fall away.

More of something leads you away.

Less of something leads you away.

Just realize right now

Right here in this moment

Where future and past does not exist

Where future and past are illusions projected

Where no thought exists

Stop moving

Just dwell as such

Stop doing

Just dwell as such

Stop thinking

Just dwell as such

Stop grasping

Just dwell as such

Stop seeking answers

Just dwell as such

Stop theorizing

Stop projecting

Stop evading

Stop letting go

Right here

Right now

If you see this

You understand that

The Tao that can be spoken of

Cannot be the Tao.

You were never any of the things that

Rise and fall away.

However, you were also never not them.

The moment your mind stirs

Thinking "this is me"

or thinking "this is not me"

You lose it

It slithers between your fingers like an eel.

The moment your mind stirs

Thinking "I am a higher self"

or thinking "I am not a higher self"

You lose it

It slithers again between your fingers.

Awaken, there is no awakening.

You are already awake

You constantly move your mind,

You try to awaken,

But you are already awake.

Any idea

Whether it be -

That of stillness

That of emptiness

That of union

That of an impermanent Self

That of an eternal Self

That of God

That of Atman

That of Brahma

That of Buddha

That of Dharma

That of Sangha

Relinquish them all

And reside like a baby

A baby who knows nothing.

Like the great oak tree

its roots search deep into the ground

its branches sway gently in the wind.

It does not think "this is me"

It does not think "this is not me"

It does not think "I should think"

It does not think "I should not think"

There is nothing to retreat to,

But yet this "nothing" is not

a thought of nothing.

Can anything exist on its own?

Without previous conditions, it cannot.

If you cut a table in half,

Where is the table now?

All names are thoughts.

All concepts are fiction.

The "I" is fiction.

The inner "I" is fiction.

The endless-inner "I"s are all fictions.

If you preach "no self"

it is the same as preaching "self"

If you preach "Atman"

it is the same as preaching "Anatman"

Stop wrestling with the mind

Stay still

Lie down

Strike yourself with a thunderbolt.

Cut all your false thoughts.

Can a mirror contain its reflections?

Can a reflection find the mirror?
