# Dealing with fear

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **primatelife** · *(OCR)* · `Question` · 5 points · 10 months ago

First of all, I want to thank this subreddit and especially refugeinbuddha for the guidance in my path. This subreddit has brought me intense clarity about my practice.

I have been meditating sporadically from since 2014 with periods of disciplined meditation practice, but with also periods of complete lack of practice. Now having the right instructions and a virtual shanga that motivates me every day, I've been able to deepen my practice.

I had an unusual experience yesterday while sitting that frightened me a bit.

Background:

o 2 weeks ago I moved in to a new country e For the past week I've been feeling intense emotions of anger, anxiety and sadness rising from almost out of nowhere. They seem to accumulate up in the area of the third eye causing headaches. When the emotions get intense, I've been doing stream of consciousness writing which helps to alleviate the pressure to some degree. By writing down the thoughts I've been surprised by the utter extremeness of this anger and hostility that seem to lurk in the thoughts - going unnoticed most of the time. I've been wondering if this is a cycle of spiritual crisis, but not having reached profound states or even having a prolonged periods of practice, I'm not assured.

The frightening experience:

I was meditating yesterday in the evening doing Anapanasati reflecting. I haven't been paying much attention to my posture previously, so I've always kin of ended up in a careless half-lotus position with my back against a cushioning. This time, I felt my leg going numb so I shifted to a sitting position without any back support with an erect spine. After shifting to this position I felt an immediate change in the meditation, It felt like the energy could move more freely and the meditation started getting calmer and deeper. I was about 30-40 minutes in the meditation reflecting on the short/long breath and was getting deeper and more concentrated. I felt my mind wondering a few times, but brought it back to breath and the breath itself felt wonderfully peaceful and meaningful. I made a mental note that “I'm going deep" and I felt the contrast with the state I was in to the state of my normal waking awareness. This happens to me often in meditation after +30 minutes, when some part of me realizes that I'm in very different state. I feel like the the walls of my awareness start closing in and it gets darker and darker, and I get anxiety. Many times I've had to open my eyes because I've felt I'm "In too deep”. It feels like I don't have enough good impressions in my mind to go that deep. It feels like I'm not ready for it yet. I don't have problems sitting in meditation for prolonged times, I have troubles staying there because of the fear.

So now I got this same feeling, but I just ignored it and returned to the breath. I was feeling the anxiety but I got back to the breath and once I got the breath again, I started hearing a high pitched sound, like the "sound of silence" but it was more intense and louder. I switched from the breath to the sound and almost immediately as I did that, the sound got exponentially louder and a huge jolt of energy washed over my body. My whole body got immersed with the energy in a split second. I opened my eyes immediately and the energy continued, my heart started racing and it was accompanied with a huge anxiety and fear. These thoughts were along lines; "have I done some permanent damage to my psyche?". I don't think I've ever felt my heart racing so fast. I did the only thing I know and just intuitively raised my hands in a receiving way and surrendered to the fear and let it pass me. The energy eventually subsided and I returned back to my normal state. I was feeling uncomfortable and anxious about the experience, it felt too strong for me to handle. Anyways, the high pitched ringing continued faintly until I went to sleep and every time I concentrated I could hear it getting a bit louder upon listening. The next day, the same irrational emotions of anger and anxiety are accumulating in the third eye region and I feel uneasy about meditation. I wonder if these are just normal psycho-biological symptoms or something related to my spiritual practice.

What was this phenomena that happened? I doubt it was an Jhana-Nimitta with the amount of cultivation I have with this practice.

Should one cultivate good impressions (metta, equanimity) before entering deeper states in meditation? How does one cultivate those impressions?

How does one deal with fear in meditation?

My experience is anecdotal but I hope the answers benefit also others on the path experiencing similar events
