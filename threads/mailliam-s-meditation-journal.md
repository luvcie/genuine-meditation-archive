  > Does anyone have any tips on maintaining mindfulness throughout the day?

  For a start, watch your thoughts, sensations and perceptions. In order to function in life, the consciousness works like a stream. However, you are not this consciousness, because if you are, then you cannot possibly witness this stream.

  The best way would be to go into jhana and remain in jhana. Of course, the second best would be to be mindful and adhere to the Noble Eightfold Path. Be mindful of speech, thought and actions.

  Thought-wise, one has to always be careful of unwholesome states and unwholesome thoughts. Shift the polarity to wholesomeness. This goes as well for speech and actions. Mindfulness is a habit. It gets better when you incorporate it into your lifestyle.
