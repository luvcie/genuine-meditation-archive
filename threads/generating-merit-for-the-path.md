# Generating Merit for the Path

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 2 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2zkadr/generating_merit_for_the_path/>

Without merit, it is impossible to succeed on the Path. But abandon those notions of merit that you have in your head right now. The word "merit" has been too loaded with social conditioning. This merit is far more incomprehensible than you can imagine.

As a general rule, one should always do what is undone that you know should be done. Support the virtuous deeds that are ongoing and being carried out. Many people are passive - But in order to have true merit, one has to give aid and support these notions in order to allow society to flourish with virtuosity and goodness.

Secondly, when you recognize evil deeds and thoughts being done by yourself or those within your influence, you must immediately try to cut it. Cut it before it becomes too big a problem to handle. When one goes too far, it often feels as if the problem spirals out of control. How do you prevent this? Mindfulness - Mental introspection is the key. Watch your thoughts at all times and be aware of when unwholesome states or evil thoughts come up. Do not live like a zombie.

Thirdly, arrange circumstances so that errant ways can never be born. Prevention is always far better than cure. Governments have checks and balances to prevent power corruption. Likewise, you must have a way of checks and balances to prevent errant ways from rising. This initially comes from sila practice, and sometimes from a teacher.

To not prevent evil from gaining sway in societies, is often a big problem by good people. They want to be left alone and not be bothered, and this causes evil to grow. Remember - To understand cause and effect is wisdom. The Dharma is present everywhere, not just in meditation work.

To lack the nerve to do virtuous work is a great disservice. Individuals who devote themselves to noble efforts perform tremendous good, and they will only reap tremendous karmic returns.

Investigation of the mind is one of the seven factors of enlightenment. You should never get rid of logic and analysis, nor try to destroy your mind's capabilities. Instead, you want control over them. That is not the same as suppressing and ignoring them.

Why is merit so important? If you do not have merit, you are not able to reach the deepest levels of meditation. That is how important it can be.

What is the highest form of merit? To achieve a clean and pure mind, through cultivation of meditation. The more you achieve this, the more merit you gain, and naturally, the deeper you will be able to go.

Now monetary merit, such as giving gifts or time to organizations like the Salvation Army, Doctors Without Borders, etc, is merit. However, what is most important is not this.

The act of giving is most important. If you are able to forget yourself completely, to the point where you act on behalf of other with compassion and selflessness, that is cultivation practice in itself.

Practicing right speech, thoughts and action are all forms of merit. Smiling to a stranger, talking to strangers with a friendly disposition, inspiring others and giving hope, spreading dharma teachings, etc.

But merit is not that simple. One also has to have the wisdom to know if it is merit. For example, if you support a bad organization, it can result in a bad result. Logic, reasoning and deep understanding itself is important for worldly affairs! If you lack the ability to think critically in the real-world, what makes you think you can attain supramundane wisdom?

You should never have any expectation or return or reward when working for others. Even to think that you would receive good karma in return for helping others is still not it. It should be just natural, just habitual, just something that you do.

Even if people do not appreciate what you do, you will not mind it at all. You can sacrifice everything that you have as an offering, and even if nobody appreciates it, if you have, through wisdom, done the best you can, that is merit.

Merit is not power or money. Even saying a word of prayer, offering the little things to people, giving change to the homeless, etc, can all be acts of merit. Wisdom of course, must still be exercised.

The Hungry Ghost Exercise
This is a method that one of my teachers taught.

Hungry ghosts are beings who were filled with greed and selfishness in a past-life. Having to suffer their karma, they are deformed bodies that cannot take in food. Hence, they always suffer from a state of lack.

It can be a few grains of rice, or a small glass of water. It should be vegetarian. Once you have them, say a few prayers of goodwill to bless these food, then place them outside as an offering to these suffering beings.

Now this is advice given from an enlightened master. Through the blessing, it makes the food so purified that they are able to intake the food. Never offer meat, since it can drive them into the animal realm or increase their greed for murder and meat.

Praying for the Deceased

Another one that helps to exercise merit, is to pray for the departed. It does not matter exactly how you pray, for any religion.

Sometimes, the departed may enter your sleep or a relative's, requesting for a specific type of prayer. Don't be ignorant and think that it is of a different religion. All religions are but the same cosmic truth, just varying degrees of them. Doing it allows them to achieve an empty state of mind.

They really do help people. If you are able to do this for the departed, such as reciting the Uṣṇīṣa Vijaya Dhāraṇī Sūtra a few thousand times, it can really free these beings.

No matter how bad a person is, no matter how evil one is, no matter how many evil deeds, how henious, how incorruptible one may seem to be, if one has the true drive to want to change - enlightened beings will be compassionate and willing to help people. If a compassionate one does not help this person, who else will?

Reading the Diamond Sutra

As stated earlier, keeping a pure and clear mind is one of the highest forms of merit. However, in order to get to this point, one has to hear the words of wisdom from an enlightened person.

The Buddha has given this discourse directly to one of his great ten disciples, known by name as Subhuti. This discourse talks about the stream-entry of emptiness. Get the Red Pine translation[1] and you can see the relevant commentaries as well.

By reading this sutra, you start to understand what it means. The more you read it, the more you understand it. My teacher once asked a student to recite the sutra a thousand times. This is how effective this can be in generating merit.

The highest merit is only reachable by active practice of the Noble Eightfold Path. Strive well my friends.

      Merit, is in a simplified way of putting it, the reversal of karma. The highest merit that one can achieve is to surrender one's ego completely and merge with the infinite in Nibbana.

      The lifestyle changes make sure of one thing: That when you sit down, it can very well be your last - You do not regret anything, you do not have anything pending left to do and you have nothing as a fetter to hold you back from enlightenment.
