# Opening the Third Eye (For fourth jhana only)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 5 hours ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3ofi8n/opening_the_third_eye_for_fourth_jhana_only/>

There is a reason why I restrict this practice to the fourth jhana only. I will not put the practice here, instead, if you feel that you have already mastered the fourth and are ready to move into awakening the spiritual eye, then send a message to me describing your attainment.

The prerequisite for this opening is the ability to sustain your focus to the point of non-duality, such that one moves past the tranquility of the mind into the non-dualistic experience of equanimity.

Secondly, the reason for acquiring a trained third-eye should be based off an expression of deep compassion, not a selfish attainment or based off mere curiosity.

It can seem interesting since it is novel, but you must realize that this is a path that cannot be reversed once you tread in it. There is no going back once the eye is opened. If you have your mind set on enlightenment, then this is the path to tread.

At the very start, there is Nibbana - Tao - God - YHVH. The Monad/One manifested from the Tao and this is pure, undifferentiated energy and information. From this, energy polarized into two poles - Light and dark, Hot and cold, etc. Then from this polarity comes the advent of Universal Mind which organizes the "creation" of all matter.

This polarity is known as Yin and Yang. What generates the different organizations is the Mind. Without the Universal Mind/consciousness, then nothing can take shape or form. As individual beings, we tap into this universal consciousness through individual consciousnesses. The yoga sutras call this the cloud of truth/light while Kabbalah calls this the many fragments of the Original Man.

Many physical appearances are merely a representation of energy. If there is imbalance in the body, for example, it can result in health problems such as tumors.

It is not only physical - All forms of decisions like your speech, thoughts and actions are part of your consciousness and affects energy. Therefore, this is why the Buddha always stressed one to have good discipline over one's thoughts, speech and actions.

The Buddha also talked about discipline of emotions from sensory feelings and perceptions. Anger affects the liver; Fear attacks the kidneys; Depression influences the chest organs like lungs; Over-excitement stresses the heart. All are vibrations and caused by your consciousness. Therefore, be mindful of your thoughts.

It is not simply sitting on the mat. Your consciousness works for all 24 hours of a day without rest, even in your sleep. As long as there is consciousness, one must relentlessly work on mindfulness.

Love and open up the heart. Be as flexible as a young willow plant, not as rigid as an old oak tree. Be as innocent as a child, not as hardened as an adult. Follow the example of the sages like the Buddha, or of other religion's representatives. Be generous and giving.

Don't base your actions off emotions for they are mere sensations. Instead, base them off an unconditional giving and surrender. Letting go, go into the emptiness and let the universal energy flow through your actions. Let your energetic vibration reach that of the cosmos - and merge as one.

The third-eye can be accessed by one who has mastered this degree of emptiness. Hence, I use this as a form of motivation - Practice well, strive well and once you attain the fruit, more will be revealed to you.

AN 11.16 PTS: A v 342 BJT calls this the Mettanisamsa Sutta; Thai, Burmese, and PTS call it Metta Sutta.

Metta (Mettanisamsa) Sutta: Discourse on Advantages of Loving-kindness

translated from the Pali by
Piyadassi Thera
© 2005
Alternate translation: Thanissaro

Thus have I heard:

On one occasion the Blessed One was living near Savatthi at Jetavana at Anathapindika's monastery. Then he addressed the monks saying, "Monks." — "Venerable Sir," said the monks, by way of reply. The Blessed One then spoke as follows:

"Monks, eleven advantages are to be expected from the release (deliverance) of heart by familiarizing oneself with thoughts of loving-kindness (metta), by the cultivation of loving-kindness, by constantly increasing these thoughts, by regarding loving-kindness as a vehicle (of expression), and also as something to be treasured, by living in conformity with these thoughts, by putting these ideas into practice, and by establishing them. What are the eleven?

1. "He sleeps in comfort. 2. He awakes in comfort. 3. He sees no evil dreams. 4. He is dear to human beings. 5. He is dear to non-human beings. 6. Devas (gods) protect him. 7. Fire, poison, and sword cannot touch him. 8. His mind can concentrate quickly. 9. His countenance is serene. 10. He dies without being confused in mind. 11. If he fails to attain arahantship (the highest sanctity) here and now, he will be reborn in the brahma-world.

"These eleven advantages, monks, are to be expected from the release of heart by familiarizing oneself with thoughts of loving-kindness, by cultivation of loving-kindness, by constantly increasing these thoughts, by regarding loving-kindness as a vehicle (of expression), and also as something to be treasured, by living in conformity with these thoughts, by putting these ideas into practice and by establishing them."

So said the Blessed One. Those monks rejoiced at the words of the Blessed One.
