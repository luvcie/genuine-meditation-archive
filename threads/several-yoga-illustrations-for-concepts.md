# Several Yoga Illustrations for Concepts

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 4 points · 2 years ago

Although we seek the Buddhist way, you must also understand that there are many other methods of cultivation out there. Taoism's Tao, Yoga, Relationship with God, Enlightenment, all of these are fingers pointing to the same nature. Perhaps their literature might suffer a little, as well as their degree of perception, but most of them are in the right

way.

I wanted to find a good translation of the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali to share with you friends but came across these rather aptly made graphics that I must say illustrate these concepts rather well.

The Filtering Process of Meditation

What is the True Self?

The Ego and Consciousness

Why a Zen master called the body a donkey

Five Types of Thoughts in Yoga - Of course, Buddhism talks about a few more.

The Paradox of Desiring Progress and Letting Go

From material objects to immaterial concepts

How the self is a microcosm of true nature

Yoga's equivalent to Eightfold Path

Why do people not naturally reach enlightenment?

The Deep Meaning of the Breath

Excellent Diagram of how the true nature is filtered to form sensory experience

They say one picture speaks a thousand words. I hope this helps you with any confusion and doubt in the path.

However, do not forget that these are analogies to help a person grasp the "simple" concept behind it. The actual experience of this knowledge is unimaginable and indescribable. Always use these things as a guideline, then drop it to practice and acquire the real wisdom.

When you close your eyes, do you see complete darkness? This is what a Zen master would call "black lacquer”. Strive well, and when you close your eyes and see light, you will know that you are on the path to enlightenment.
