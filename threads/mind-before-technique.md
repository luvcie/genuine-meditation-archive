# Mind before technique

**r/Meditation** · by **blissfulencounters** · *(OCR)* · 1 point · 10 months ago

Technique matters. It is like getting a horse on a bridle so you can control where the horse goes...

I learnt this from a really deep meditator (/u/refugeinbuddha)... very indebted to him... just to be clear, he refuses me as a student but i do get advice from him...

He teaches that all these techniques, what they do is simply to help do certain things to the mind...

So if you want to get into deep states, what gets into a deep state? Well... the mind goes into a deep state...

So if you focus on breath, the only thing this mental exercise is doing is to train your concentration.... or placing your attention in one single place...

what happens to mind when you do this? well... there are fewer discursive thoughts... monkey mind becomes more tamed...

that's the purpose of training with the breath...

then he says this further...

mind and breath are linked, right? so obviously when your mind 'stops' with busy activity, your breath is also going to react... it's going to stop as well... before it stops it normally goes super faint and imperceptible...

when it really stops, then you suddenly get filled with immense energy that burns through obstacles in your body... so you see all sorts of lights, sounds, feelings like burning, movements, blah blah blah...

BUT...

when your mind becomes distracted again... then you start breathing again... that's a sure sign your mind is super distracted. so you start using the technique of watching to get the monkey mind tame again...

but yeah... I noticed this about a lot of people in the sub who think you should stick to the technique throughout and forever... i notice they might get occasional results... but no, real meditation, or at least what i learnt from /u/refugeinbuddha, is when you really do get progress, insights, freeing of blockages, burning away of afflictions, neuroses etc

technique is a training wheel... it gets you to the right state, then the body knows what to do... other techniques also can help you burn away things faster, like certain mantras and what not...

yup... but the end point of going through all those states is what we call clearing away the body karma junk...

then when you go higher up into deeper states after the body is no longer a problem... we go deal with all the layers of the mental karma...

then after all that... we finally deal with reality and grasping reality... insights into impermanence and all of that kind of stuff... i'm definitely not there yet, but i can safely say that this is really the way... believe me if you wish... i cant vouch for it lol

but yeah...

mind first.

what the technique does to your mind is more important than the technique. but of course you got to do the technique right, but you gotta understand what the technique is trying to do! right??

alright peace with you mates. enjoy meditating.
