(14) Observe Dispassion

The Desire Realm is all oneself’s greed, anger, ignorance and longing. In this, you jump out, and everything is released. You see impermanence, you see release and you see dispassion.

This is not the kind of lust between males and females any longer. Any kind of attraction of the world can no longer hold one back, this is real dispassion.

In this world, you earn what you should earn, and this earning is yours. However, if you earn what you don’t have to earn, earning over a few million, what is the purpose of this? In the end, it is still another person’s. You just need to do what you need to do. Anything else is out of the question.
