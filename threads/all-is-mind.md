# All is mind

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 9 points · 1 year ago

All is mind. But it is easily to get confused at what the word 'mind' means.

There is a difference between cognition and mind. There are many cognitive functions that happen. The discriminating function, the memory function, and so on. There is the discriminating mind (6th ), ego (7th) and alaya-vijnana (8th) apart from the 5 senses.

But these aren't the "mind" that is being referred to.

Therefore, when you are in meditation, feel it. Don't think about it or put a label on it. Many beginners like to "note" things, saying in their minds, "Oh, this is happening. That is happening.” These people are too used to using their cognition, probably because of education.

Let go of concepts and feel. Or rather, know.

There is nothing you can hold onto and group into a label in non-duality. Even deeper than non-duality, the boundaries of concepts fade.

When your knowing, or feeling is right, you don't need to worry about meditation techniques. Everyone has a different set of perception. But the knowing is always the same.

Feel connected to the divine. Feel connected to the many masters of the past. Feel connected to all living beings. Feel connected to the unconditional love of the Earth that continues to shield us from the UV-rays of the Sun. Feel connected to the brilliant stars that feed each system their loving energy. Feel the beauty of the universe. Feel.

Let go of doing. Let go of trying to grasp onto a concept. Do not be fearful of letting go of concepts. They are illusory. Nothing is ever grouped in space, because one thing is always interconnected with another. Nothing is ever grouped in time, because all things are impermanent as a chain of endless causes and effects.

When you sit in meditation, you are just sitting. You are not 'meditating’. You are sitting, that is a condition. When you focus on the breath, that is another condition. Let go of the concepts and simply be.

Meditation is not just on the mat. It is unhindered, moment-to-moment sati (mindfulness), taking into account the body, the sensations, thoughts and mental qualities. Having developed powerful mindfulness, one then is able to develop deep insight.
