# Alternative hand position

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Question` · 4 points · 1 year ago

This was requested by a sangha member, saying: "Is there another method other than the samadhi mudra?”

This is especially for people who have a difficult time quietening the mind. Utilize this mudra:

Place your hands on your knees, and touch the thumb with the ring finger. Make sure that the entire body is relaxed. This allows the shoulders to be balanced during meditation.
