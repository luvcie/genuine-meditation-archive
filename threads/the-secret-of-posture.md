# The Secret of Posture

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 2015-11-30

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3uv385/the_secret_of_posture/>

If you are able to align your spine in the right manner, you can naturally enter a meditative state. This is one of the secrets.

How does one do this?

Firstly, sit in your position - be it a chair, Seiza, half-lotus or full-lotus. If you are a beginner, a larger cushion is better to elevate your hips above the knees. The more advanced you are, the thinner the cushion should be. All energy masters must be able to sit with a very thin cushion or completely flat on the ground just as the Buddha did under the Bodhi tree.

When your hips are aligned properly, the pelvis tilts so that your buttocks stick out a little, while maintaining a very slight arch in the lumbar spine. In half-lotus, one heel must be tucked into the perineum. Ultimately, the full-lotus is the most powerful posture.

To align the spine properly, one must firstly imagine a stream of energy moving from the center of the Earth upwards to the center of the universe.

Rock your spine forwards and backwards by very small degrees. At one point, you will become more sensitive to a jolt of energy, like electricity or heat, at the base of your spine. This indicates a straightening of your spine. Then rock your spine sideways. Find the sweet spot. Then do the same thing to your head.

The heart area is pulled outwards and upwards while the shoulders are relaxed and pulled backwards. It is a proud posture that requires strengthening of your muscles. The energy current should flow through the back of the head and heart area. There is belly breathing naturally. When you find the right center, slowly, the breathing changes and the energy flow becomes distinct.

If you do it correctly, you will find that the meditative state comes naturally. Try this and see the improvement in your meditation.
