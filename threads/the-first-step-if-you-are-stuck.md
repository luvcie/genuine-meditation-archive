# The first step if you are "stuck"

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3cc4ar/the_first_step_if_you_are_stuck/>

There is a difference between what you directly perceive and what you read/learn from others.

• Direct perception : Real knowledge
• Indirect, transferred perception: Imagination

If you find that you are having problems advancing in your meditation, or having problems doing this or that, always come back to this.

• What do you know?

• What do you not know?

By knowing, I mean, what have you directly perceived? That is what you really know.

When you have never seen a strawberry in your life, and I describe how a strawberry tastes, you have an imagination in your mind of what the strawberry is. The strawberry is red, triangular, sweet, sour at points, etc. The descriptions can be numerous. Yet, if you have never tasted a strawberry, it is difficult to understand the actual taste of it without tasting it.

Hence, if you are stuck, drop it all. What you have heard from others, even me, even enlightened beings, even the Buddha, just drop it all. What do you experience right now in your reality? Work from there.

Use the navigational signs as guidances to your own experience of reality. The four noble truths. Then pick a way of navigating - the five skandhas, the four frames of perception, anatta, suffering, etc. What works for you, use that.

What you do not perceive, you do not know. Do not try to look for them in places that you think they are in. Do not try to think of a concept and try to fit them in. It is like making a picture-frame before you know the actual dimensions of the photo.

It is important, perhaps even more important than knowing, to know that you do not know things. That is because when you know that you do not know, the space for possibility is there.

If you flood your mind with pre-conceived notions, it is like watching a movie with spoilers. You cannot experience it anymore, because you are constantly pre-empting the experience. You are square-boxing it. Instead of experiencing and discerning, you are holding onto a concept.

I hope this helps you if you find yourself stuck in a cycle of knowledge-seeking and if your practice is still not helping you.

Strive well for enlightenment.
