This group has given me much, although I happened on it quite randomly it seems. Thank you all for all the contributions on the articles one can read, I have yet to read many of them. Thank you Paramananda for your words of advice. Many of them have sounded true within me.

  > Furthermore I have decided that I will from now on sleep in the sitting position and no more lie down until I have attained stream entry.

  To this, I quote the AN 4.12 sutta:

  > Controlled in walking, controlled in standing, controlled in sitting and in lying down; controlled, a bhikkhu draws in the limbs, and controlled, he stretches them out. Above, across, and below, as far as the world extends, he is one who scrutinizes the arising and vanishing of such phenomena as the aggregates. Training in what is conducive to serenity of mind, always mindful, they call such a bhikkhu one constantly resolute.

  By forcing the body into ascetic conditions, you will only torture the body and it can serve as a hindrance to your cultivation.

  When walking, walk skilfully. When standing, stand skilfully. When lying-down, lie skilfully. When sitting, sit skilfully.

  What is skilful? It is withdrawing from sensual pleasure & withdrawing from unwholesome states, non-dwelling, arousing energy, entering the first jhana, second jhana, third jhana, and fourth jhana. Abiding in right-samadhi, this is the posture of the Tathagata.

          > Though I am a bit confused as why you would discourage this practice, as it is a practice buddhist monks in some monasteries engage in, and also a practice Dilgo Khyentse engaged in for all of his adult life as far as I know.

          Dilgo Khyentse Rinpoche is a highly-accomplished master, it is far easier for him to enter Samadhi in his sleep. He does not deplete as much energy as a beginner does. As for many other practitioners, particularly people who are in the pre-yoga phase, doing such a practice will cause muscular strain and depletion in bodily strength, which can cause drowsiness and dullness in formal meditative practices.
