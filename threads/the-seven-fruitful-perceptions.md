# The Seven Fruitful Perceptions

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Sutra` · 3 months ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3c9xzr/the_seven_fruitful_perceptions/>

Please refer to the following sutra[1] for more details.

The seven perceptions are listed as follows:

1. Perception of the unattractive/foulness
2. Perception of death
3. Perception of loathsomeness in food

4. Perception of distaste for every world, for every worldly embllishment

5. Perception of inconstancy
6. Perception of suffering in impermanence

7. Perception of not-self (anatta) in what brings suffering - This is about stopping all "I am X" identification with your body, thoughts, emotions, etc.

This sutra is very powerful, do take the time to study and apply it to your practices. Remember... apply. You must apply or it will not be your insight.
