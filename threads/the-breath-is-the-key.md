# The Breath Is the Key

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 11 points · 1 year ago

How do you see the breath as the key to life?

Without the breath, you will not receive oxygen for your body. Therefore, the body dies, especially if one does not turn to "internal breathing".

Have you ever seen a bellow used to keep fire burning strong? Everytime the bellow blows, oxygen gets pumped into the fire, and the fire roars to life. When the bellow does not blow, the fire starts to dull and may even extinguish.

Your body works like this. Without the air entering your nose or mouth, feeding the rest of the body through the bloodstream, then it will die. When the "bellow" blows, your cells receive oxygen and are rejuvenated. When the "bellow" relaxes, your cells push out carbon dioxide.

When you exhale, if you no longer inhale, you are dead. It is like a mini-death, is it not?

The entire universe is just like this. These two movements: Ana (outwards) and pana (inwards). Expansion and contraction, expansion and contraction.

Beings come, beings go. Suns, planets, eco-systems, humans, cells - All kinds of forms are subject to this everchanging phenomena. So watch this inconstancy. What is the nature of impermanence? You must examine this closely.

Why did the Buddha teach the anapana (expanding-contracting) meditation as one of the most direct observations towards release?

I just understood what this means... i get it! My body is a piece of nature. When the wind comes in, it rustles the leaves of this "tree". When it doesnt rustle, it rests. Like a fire, we're kept alive by the breath of life... though it seems automatic, it isnt! There's a certain unseen force pushing us to continue.

Just now I sat in meditation and using this insight my breath calmed down nearly immediately... then poof, in breath cessation! So quickly. It's as if i was watching the trees in nature, with the wind caressing the leaves... the same as my body... the wind caressing me with the gift of life... i felt so grateful that this joy kept me awake throughout. I just sat for 2 hours straight without rousing once!!

My gosh i never realised that each breath itself was a gift of life. I always took every breath for granted.... And just letting go of it took me into the internal breath and ecstasy.

      This is wonderful. If you continue with this insight, it will surely take you deep into the jhanas.

      Now extend your insight and investigation. What do you perceive as yours? Simply this: Anything within your sensory boundaries. Anything within the reach of your six senses, you claim as you, yours, your identity, associated with you.

      As you take in that breath, you also notice that this very same air is seamless with the breath of another being, or that of the trees. The air pressure in the entire world, the hurricanes that come and go, the chilly air in the Himalayas, etc.

      Now, you feel your sensory "body" expand in this manner. When you merge with the entirety of space, we say that you have accomplished yoga with "Infinite space". Dissolve your sense of 'small self' that is constrained to your body. Can you not see the same gift of life entering and leaving all sentient beings in the universe?
