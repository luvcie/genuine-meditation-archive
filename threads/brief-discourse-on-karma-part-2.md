# Brief Discourse on Karma - Part 2

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 7 points · 2018-01-14

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/7qalzn/brief_discourse_on_karma_part_2/>

This is a continuation of the [previous part 1](https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/7pvad1/brief_discourse_on_karma/).

As discussed previously to crystalize this understanding, the binding force that holds sentient beings to the six-realms (samsara) is Karma. Karma is caused by upakleshas, afflictions. And these upakleshas can only arise because of the self-view.

We examined why this seemingly-continuous self-view arises. Firstly, the 8th Alaya consciousness has a perceiving function that is called the Darshana-bhaga. Secondly, the 7th consciousness then **mistakenly** grasps onto this perceiving function and thinks of it as a "self".

Next, we examined what this Darshana-bhaga is. It is the Subject, which can only exist in opposite to Object, creating a Subject-Object duality. In other words, it is also called a Cause-Caused duality.

Now we shall investigate further.

The 8th consciousness, as discussed, has a perceiving-discrimination function. The 7th consciousness hijacks this function and therefore there is always a "self" that sees, hears, smells, tastes and feels.

In the Southern schools of Chan, they do exactly this - training themselves to not have a "self" that sees, hears, smells, tastes or feels. It is just seeing, hearing, smelling, tasting and feeling, without adding any extra notion of "self" on top of it.

If this self-view, this additional labelling on top of experience, is completely eradicated, whether it is using any method from any school, then there will not be any rebirth. Why? Because rebirth is caused by this "self", this mistaken grasping of the 7th-consciousness!

What again is this "self"? It is imagined, there is no actual object that is a self, there has never been such a thing. This "self" is the grasping function of the 7th consciousness, and nothing else.

When there is pure seeing, pure listening without a "self" - there are no good or bad sights or sounds. When this happens, this is pure Presence without any mental-discrimination from the 6th consciousness.

**This is what happens.** When there is experience of the five senses, they firstly become mentally-discriminated by the 6th sense, being classified into whether it is pleasurable or painful, liked or disliked.

After this, the 7th consciousness then adds onto it the notion of a "self" and it changes into whether the "self" feels that it is pleasurable or painful, liked or dislike.

If one can understand this principle, your cultivation will be extremely fast. One will easily be able to enter jhanas and move through them. Seeing is just seeing, there is no "self" that is attached to the seeing, no "self" that is doing the seeing.

Now let us move on to a deeper understanding.

# Alaya, The 8th Consciousness

The alaya mind-ground stores karmic-seeds of infinite kalpas. When they fruition, they bring about repeated rebirths. All of these karmic-seeds were done by oneself due to repeated self-grasping in inconceivably-numerous past-lives.

Therefore, whatever one sees, is in fact the blossoming of karmic-seeds from these numerous past-lives.

One might think that it is unfair. However, for numerous lives, one has continued to grasp onto the "self", and it is because of this that the karmic-seeds have been planted. When one is rebirthed again and again, it is the 8th-consciousness is always there. It always remembers, it does not have any grudge against you, it is simply how the laws of nature work!

What one can do is to **now** remove the reinforcement of any seeds that surface. Remove the "self" in any seeing and there will not be any afflictions that arise which will perfume into the Alaya consciousness. When all of these karmic-seeds are resolved and purified, then one becomes a fully-enlightened Buddha.

So what is one doing when doing cultivation? **Cultivation is simply cleaning up the seeds of the Alaya.**

But how do you clean them up?

This depends on what you do right now. If one is constantly unmindful, remaining inside desire, anger, ignorance, conceit, doubt, big or small afflictions, grasping onto this or that, then all of these will certainly perpetuate more seeds in the Alaya. If one is able to be very mindful, very clear, such that every single seed is planted towards liberation, then this is cleaning!

We know as discussed previously, that the "self grasping" comes from the 7th-consciousness. What it is grasping onto and taking as the "self" is actually the perception-function of the Alaya.

Now why is it that we have our bodies, our minds, and even the external phenomena around us? These are all due to the function of the Alaya, just like the "self", they do not actually exist!

From the very moment one is born inside the womb, it had already been decided that one is in the Human Path.

What exactly is awakening? What does one realize?

All the phenomenas of the world originally do not have a "self", they are all manifested by oneself. They are all impermanent, produced by cause and condition of karma! Due to the constant birth and death, one can never find a permanent realm in body or mind.

However, the speed of birth and death is so quick, so fast, that it seems as if all phenomena are continuous. A table appears solid, monasteries appear solid, whatever you are reading right now appear solid - but they are just a continuous movement of birth and death, moving so quickly that you think that there are no breaks in between.

# The Reason for Self-Grasping

Now that we have understood very clearly how this "self" appears, which is the 7th consciousness grasping onto the perceiving-function of the Alaya as a "self", we must now explore why this occurs.

Why must this happen? Why does this grasping happen?

**This is what is called as Ignorance or Avijja.**

Ignorance simply means that one does not understand, one does not realize. Ignorance is not understanding that there is no "self" in every appearance. Because of this, it has caused the duality of perceiver and the perceived.

Ignorance is not understanding the true appearance of all phenomena. What is the true appearance? The birth and death of causes and conditions, emptiness without "self"! If one understands this, and attains to this, this is Vijja, Understanding.

When something is impermanent, it means that this takes birth and dies, it arises and fades, it appears and disappears. It is impossible to say that there is ever a "self" at all.

If you really understand this, you are truly, truly unable to find a "self" no matter where you look, then you have reached it, the ten directions will be emptied out.

Why? Because all of these phenomena are all manifested from the Alaya consciousness.

Now our common-karma is that we are all in the human path. Therefore, the scenery and phenomena that are manifested are all of the human path. Above that, they are all individualized. I have no ability of changing your manifested world of mind and body; Neither do you have the ability of changing my manifested world of mind and body.

The reason is because you and I have common-karma. You and I are both on the same subreddit. Offline, you and I would be in the same meditation hall.

For example, it is like a room with many lamps. This lamp shines onto the other, that lamp shines here. Every being shines their own light, just like how every being has their own karma, producing their own realm of mind and body. We are all interconnected by common-karma. Actually, in absolute reality, there is no "you" or "me". It is simply karma acting!

The objective of cultivation is to shatter Ignorance.

# Ignorance Is Originally Non-Existent

In our original Self-nature which is pure, is there ignorance originally? Of course not. If every single thought is understood to not have any "self" there, then there will not be ignorance.

Let me then ask you. Are Ignorance and Understanding two different things or one thing? Are Day and Night two things or one thing?

Some say two, some say one.

Now how does the day become the night? Slowly the sun sets and the brightness becomes dimmer and dimmer, until there is no more light, that is why we call it Night. When the sun rises, it becomes brighter and brighter, we then call it the day. So are they two or one? One!

Similarly, knowledge and ignorance are on a spectrum. The more Vijja, knowledge, arises, then the lesser the Avijja, ignorance.

But once one understands this principle, time passes and suddenly one forgets, isn't that right? This is because the karma is too heavy, immediately the ignorance returns, the self-grasping and the afflictions associated with the "self" arises. Therefore, it is not a one-off case of suddenly understanding this principle and breaking it off.

When this "self" arises, then the Sakkaya-ditthi (personality view) arises. There are in fact four other wrong views. What are the five wrong views?

1. Sakkaya-ditthi - Personality-view - there is not a single thought without a "self" present. This is why you turn around immediately when someone calls your name or why the "self" is even present in dreams.

2. Anta-ditthi - One-sided opinion

3. Ditthevisuddhi-ditthi - Attachment to opinions.

4. Miccha-ditthi - Deviant opinion

5. Sila-bbata-paramasa - Attachment to rites, rituals and ceremonies

Firstly, one has a Personality-view, steadying one's standpoint. After steadying one's standpoint, one tries to establish who is right or wrong - this is Anta-ditthi, one-sided opinion. Next one immediately falls into Ditthevisuddhi-ditthi, being attached to the strengthening of their view.

These are only breakable in the first fruit of Arahantship. Therefore, in the common folk, everyone has them, it is just that they are not aware that they are increasing their Ignorance.

# The Need for Awareness

This awareness, this knowing-nature, is therefore extremely important. It is originally there, you do not need to try to find this knowing-nature. What you need to do right now is to hold steady and maintain this knowing-nature, and at that moment it is Buddha.

If you do not ever lose this moment, never dropping into Ignorance, then this is Buddha. Realization at every thought is Buddha; Unawareness at every thought is a sentient being.

Therefore this principle is not that difficult. What is difficult is steadying this gong-fu.

In the next-part, we will again, delve deeply into what the Knowing Nature is.
