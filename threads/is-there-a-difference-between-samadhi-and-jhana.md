- **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 1 point · 2 years ago

  They are often used interchangeably.

  Samadhi generally means any state of meditative absorption. It is often used in yoga sutras to denote various types of samadhi, such as the material absorptions (savikalpa-samadhi) or immaterial absorptions (asamprajata-samadhi).

  While jhana can also be classified as material or immaterial, it is generally the first four material stages that are referred to as jhana (rupa-jhana). Beyond the first four, they are typically described as 'domains' (ayatanas), which are more accurate than affixing a jhana label to it.

  It is however, generally the case where one proceeds from the 1st to the 9th samadhi (9th being liberation/yoga/nibbana).
