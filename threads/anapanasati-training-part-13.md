# Anapanasati Training (Part 13)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 1 month ago

Commentary

Although the 16 steps may seem simple, you have to take note that when cultivating in reality, there are many “mara states”. This is when you should examine the Surangama Sutra for liberating oneself from the mara states.

It can be possible that you might suddenly have magical abilities, you might suddenly know everything, or some kind of special skill. The truth is that you have already gone into the 5 realms of Mara (demons). In these 5 realms, there are a total of 50 types of demonic states. There can be times where you are dragged away by the realm without you knowing, and you might think that you have magical powers.

That is why you should never “be” a sage. Cultivation is the most important, you must remember what the Diamond Sutra said: “Whatever that has appearance, is fabrication/illusion, if you can see appearance is not appearance, you see the Buddha.”

What is the use of special powers? Do not even care about them. The only thing you should care about is whether you can realize the Tao.

You must absolutely remember this. Some people cultivate until they have psychosis, they say that when they sit they see light, they see Buddhas, they see demons, they see gods – They do not know that these are just the interactions of physicality and the mind.

Many people cultivate, but yet they start losing progress and decreasing their practice over time. In the end, they either stay there or degrade their progress. If you think you are cultivating hard, you are actually losing progress. If you do not improve, then you are losing progress. You must continuously rise, do not stop because you will lose progress.

Why do you have to cultivate these 16 Extraordinary Victories? No matter what kind of cultivation you are doing, all cultivation practices are in this triad: A view (Understanding), a skill (Samadhi), and actions and intentions (conduct). Cultivation practices must have this triad of “realm, conduct and fruit”. What is a realm? When you are practicing Anapanasati, knowing the breath is going in or out is a realm in the present. When you feel pain in your legs in lotus, this is your realm – your reality. Or when you feel comfortable in your body, this comfort is yet another realm. Pain is a realm, achiness is a realm, numbness, itchiness, etc are all realms of feeling. What is conduct? When feeling or learning knowledge, this is conduct. When you have the right kind of conduct, you will then bear fruit.

If there wasn’t a realm, then why would we need to practice? It is very important to know that in cultivation, we will always have a realm. With the realm, if we have the right conduct or action, we will lead to fruit. This is the basis – The fruit of the Arahant, or the fruit of the Bodhisattva, or the Buddha.

But what is the fundamental reason we cultivate? It is to find the Mind. Mind is the Buddha, Mind is the Tao. But what is Mind? It is not your thinking, feelings or mental qualities/mood. These are just functions that come from the Mind. What we are after is the fundamental Mind, so that you can see your true nature. The Buddha taught that these 16 Extraordinary Victories, as taught as 16 steps of Anapanasati, will lead one to enlightenment.

Lastly, do not think that cultivation is easy. I will directly just say it here - the steps are simple, but the path requires a lot of patience, diligence and perseverance.

There are many people who think they have attained the Tao, when they clearly have not. There are others who think they have gained 'progress', when it is simply their bodies becoming healthier and resetting to the normal state. There are many who go into delusional paths, thinking that they can perform miracles, see the future, etc - When in fact, they have no real attainment.

The real attainments are the four jhanas. When one enters the last jhana, as well as the eight samadhis, one transcends this world. But let me ask you this:

Do you think there are Buddhas surfacing everywhere? It is in fact very rare - exceedingly rare. As one Buddhist teacher described, it is as if a needle fell from the sky and just happened to go through the eye of another needle floating across the ocean. The possibility of hearing the Dharma, and having a human existence, and then even having any attainments - it is ineffably precious.

Do not squander your chance here as a human being, indulging in sensory pleasures and lacking discipline, even after hearing all of these things. The steps are all laid out, there is nothing held back, there is nothing false - but it is all up to one's own personal investigation to be able to verify it for themselves.

Time is short. There are very few masters left in the world. Even my own life is limited, so whatever I have passed here, is everything that I can pass to you. The only thing is your own diligence. Work hard, strive hard, and remember that you can only bear fruit when you work on your cultivation.
