        - **orihh** · 2 points · 1 year ago

          Yes, detachment at times can be difficult to grasp, especially when it comes to detachment from very subtle things. You might find it easier to start with not-so-subtle things such as the simple feeling of heat or cold.

          This past winter, I made an effort to dress a bit less than usual to train this skill of discernment. Whenever I found the thought arising "My God it's so cold!", I would immediately remind myself that I was not the one feeling cold. As you've mentioned, this effectively pushed away a figurative layer of identity so that my body remained feeling cold, but I did not. Eventually I could walk around wearing t-shirts and shorts in negative degree weather without suffering the cold.

          This practice of discernment can be done with other feelings such as light pain, as well as emotions and desires. Ultimately however, you will come to a point where finding a distinction between certain perceived things and yourself to be very slippery. It is then that you will need a profound awakening to transcend the superficial understanding that you are not anything you perceive. I don't mean to discourage you however - an awakening is a very simple and effortless thing. You can do it here, and right now. You don't need years of training or immense amounts of effort or suffering. I encourage you to take a broom and sweep up every thing you know, and at the end, to throw away the broom as well!
