# I am making progress

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **Sylamatek** · *(OCR)* · `Discussion` · 2 points · 1 year ago

After returning from a trip to Japan+Singap0re in January, I decided to restart my meditation practice in earnest, as well as applying some Buddhist literature I received in Asian temples to my everyday life.

Initially I was able to move up to 40 minute sessions of meditating twice a day quickly. Lately, I sometimes feel “done" in 20 or 30 minutes. I never feel bad for not doing a full session, especially since I have been in a new environment lately (parent's house for Spring Break). I also think I am getting more out of these sessions because I am applying sutras and learning to maintain mindfulness throughout my day. I am also able to enter a fairly deep state of concentration rather quickly anymore in my sessions.

I feel like I am slowly preparing myself to commit to Buddhism as a religion, something that has not been a part of my life for years. I feel like "converting" is inevitable at this point, but I still believe that it is important to educate myself and slowly open up more to ideas and concepts within Buddhism. Approaching things like joy from attachments and seeing its impermanence as an atheist almost feels like proving scientific hypotheses, moving me more towards the right direction. I feel otherwise that telling everyone I'm beginning the n8p will have no meaning, as simple as taking refuge in the three jewels may be on a conceptual scale.
