# The use of a virtuous lifestyle

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 4 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3b3nkd/the_use_of_a_virtuous_lifestyle/>

What is the value of practising virtue? The value of doing merit is far greater than one would ever imagine.

Let us start with an example.

Have you ever noticed that your own thoughts, speech and actions affect your mind in deep levels? For example, if you constantly think in hateful ways, your mind tends to be filled with hatred-fueled actions, speech and thoughts. This is simple and I am sure everyone understands this as quoted from the Dhammapada:

"If a man speaks or acts with an evil thought, pain follows him, as the wheel follows the foot of the ox that draws the carriage...

If a man speaks or acts with a pure thought, happiness follows him, like a shadow that never leaves him."

What is the real meaning behind these words? Virtue is not simply because of religion or to allow order in society. It goes far, far deeper.

When a person dies, we call this the breaking or dissolution of the physical body. What happens here, is that the "subtler body" then separates from the physical body.

What is the difference here?

In the physical body you have the ability to discern your thoughts. For example, if you are craving for sugary food, you can stop yourself and think, "I do not want to entertain that craving."

But if you are no longer connected to the physical body, you are no longer capable of doing that. Whatever tendencies come up, you immediately follow, like a ghost.

If one lives a lifestyle full of greed and selfishness, clinging tendencies to all sorts of things will be present within the mind of this individual. Upon death, this mind can no longer be kept in check by discernment - Following its energies, the mind gravitates accordingly. If you wish to put it that way, you may even call it an aspect of karma.

If one lives a virtuous lifestyle, saturating his mind with meritorious thoughts, speeches and actions, the mind has less clinging. Having lesser clinging, upon death, the mind is pulled to subtler realms.

This is why we say that virtuous people go towards the formless realms and that non-virtuous people go towards the desire realms.

If there is one thing that you want to do, it is to live the Noble Eightfold Path with huge diligence. No longer will you entertain unvirtuous acts, speeches or thoughts. Training yourself, you cut them at the root and saturate yourself with the four brahmaviharas.

If one were to practice virtuosity as a lifestyle, instead thinking of gaining from it, this becomes powerful merit indeed. But even greater than all of that, is when one truly lets go of all clinging. That is when one will say that one has reached the end of rebirth and death.
