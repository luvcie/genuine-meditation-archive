                              Let us say pain arises in the body. For that one moment, as the will is not strong, it will inevitably turn towards the pain. Now recognising pain is fine. Pain arising is fine, it will eventually go away. All things are 'empty’ in nature. All you need to do is just acknowledge.

                              Now there is a subconscious factor to this as well. You might believe that you are not inviting or repulsing it. However, if your body tenses up from stress for example, as a result of the sensation, then you are subconsciously repulsed by it. Therefore, you should also remove the tightness by consciously relaxing the body.

                              Having removed the repulsion and clinging to whatever arises, you simply 'non-dwell’ as such. This is the highest instruction, and if you can understand just this itself, down to the deepest degrees, you will go far.

                              The purpose of your meditation at this stage is to remove all forms of 'reactivity’ to things that arise. Why does reactivity arise? The first and foremost is ignorance - this leads to a form of self-identity associated with whatever arises. You think 'my breath' or 'my sensation'. Of course not - it is just 'breath’, it is just 'sensation'. Remove all the clinging onto.

                              Now be careful - you might also say 'not my breath’, 'not my sensation’ - this is aversion. If you deny what it is, you create a duality. A duality simply means that you are saying there is a part that is 'me' and whatever arises is not part of that. Aversion is in fact, a subtle form of clinging opposing whatever arises.

                              What you want to do, is remove all of that verbal chatter. Whatever verbal thoughts arise, you see - ah! They are all empty.

                              Now I want you to be aware - there is an awareness, yet there is content of that awareness. Everything that you can witness - 'nothingness’, 'voidness’, thoughts, sights, sounds, tastes, smells, touches - all of these are all the content of awareness. To be even more concise, think of a mirror. The mirror is the awareness. Inside the mirror, you can see reflections. But are the reflections apart from the mirror itself? Of course not! When in pure awareness, there is pure energy. When things arise inside awareness, there is a contraction of energy to 'manifest’ things within experience. At no point has the 'content' separated itself from awareness.
