# Direct Pointing

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 8 points · 11 months ago

/u/blissfulencounters asked to clarify the trikaya and what they are, so this is a due post. This is what one calls the Bases, and understanding this can lead one to the introduction to the primordial mind state.

How does one practice with this state? If you are able to receive this, then the practice is no-practice - we call this contemplation of the primordial state. If one does not see, then one adjusts body, speech and mind - or physical, energetic and mental aspects - so that one is reintroduced to the emptiness of the state.

In the Anapanasati commentary, I wrote:

> Cultivation practices must have this triad of “realm, conduct and frutt”. :

Therefore, one must be clear that the trikaya is both under "realms" and "fruit".

Total Realization is when one attains the fruit of the three bodies. Death is utterly overthrown, all duality is transcended, and one attains the non-dualistic ability to spontaneously benefit all beings.

Here, the initial introduction is given so that one may realize this primordial mind state at the very beginning, if one's merit is sufficient.

We call this triad as the following: Essence, Nature and Energy.

Essence is energy, free from limitations or having any form. It is an awareness that is unrestricted. To understand this, one should look at the example of a mirror.

When red is present, the mirror turns red. When blue is present, the mirror turns blue. However, the mirror itself is stainless and does not change. It however, seems to be able to manifest without restriction.

This essence is what we call Dharmakaya.

Now this essence manifests as the essence of elements, which is light. We call this Nature. Now look at the example of crystal sphere.

When one places an object close to the sphere, the object appears as if it was cast inside the crystal itself. This holographic appearance is similar to how one's individual energy appears as an internally-experienced image. This is the non-dualistic level in which the Sambhogakaya is manifested, in its multiplicity of forms.

Lastly, when one looks into the mirror, the manifested appearances within the mirror itself is what we call Energy. One can think of a prism that separates light into a spectrum of rainbow colors.

While these colors seem separate from the prism, these colors are manifestations of the functions of the prism's nature. Similarly, an individual perceives a world of seemingly external phenomena, perceived by his 6 senses.

When realized, one sees that whatever is manifested in the experiential field is continuous and non-dual. All three - Essence, Nature and Energy are absolutely not separate.

As one as one remains in this dualistic realm, trying to manipulate an externally-perceived world, taking it as the "I", then the underlying senses of suffering will only continue.

The "I" that searches for enlightenment is the same "I" that searches for desire. Substitute what the "I" is searching for, and you will find myriads of masks and what one would call an ego. These has nothing to do with the true nature.

Luminous, aware emptiness - what illuminates your seeing, hearing, tasting, touching, smelling and thinking. It has no beginning and no end, so one cannot find it anywhere else. If you cling onto it as a notion, it has slipped away into the past. Naked awareness, with the cutting away of the six sense-spheres.

Look up, look down. Look at this apple. Now look at your hand.

Now look at your face.

If you have not grasped the state, then here is another one by Hui-Neng:

> The mind is the Bodhi tree, i The body is the mirror stand. The mirror is originally clean and pure; Where can it be stained by dust?

What one calls right conduct, is produced from right contemplation. When one practices right conduct, this is just an emulation. One has to awaken to the Tao.
