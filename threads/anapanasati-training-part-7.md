# Anapanasati Training (Part 7)

(9) Satisfying the mind

There is a difference between the joy and bliss in the previous stages and the joy in this stage. The joy and bliss in the earlier stages is still based off material form, a form of feelings. The joy in this stage is completely different. When you go into deep meditation in the second jhana, you have left matter and the physical. This joy is of pure mind.

(10) Steadying/absorbing the mind

This is harder to understand that the joy of the mind in the previous stage. This is when the mind becomes one with the Void, or emptiness.

Not a single thought remains. A thought only appears when one wants to use it. Otherwise, it will be completely clear. Just one thought can cover forever.

Imagine a camera. A camera is able to “absorb” all kinds of objects into it. However, it can also “project” its captured images out. In the same way, the mind is able to “absorb” or “project” any kind of object.

(11) Liberating the mind

This is when the mind is really released. Any kind of distress or worry is completely gone, and one goes from second to third jhana. The third jhana is mainly characterised by the new quality of equanimity. This state is extremely pure and clean.

However, when you’ve cultivated until this stage, you cannot say you have attained the Tao. Although your mind is freed, there is still no fruit!

Here, you will realize that the habitual energy/mannerisms of “greed/anger/foolishness/sloth” will slowly decrease. If your habits have not changed at all, then you cannot call this cultivation at all.

Here is when you reach the 7th consciousness. Now everything else after this stage is using your mind to observe and investigate.
