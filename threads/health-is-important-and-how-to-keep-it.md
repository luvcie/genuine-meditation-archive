# Health is Important (And How To Keep It)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Technique` · 5 points · 2015-01-28

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2txf5e/health_is_important_and_how_to_keep_it/>

One cannot stress that the health of the body is important. This is the exact reason why the Buddha denied any form of self-torture or deprivation. It was also when he ingested his first food from starvation that he became truly enlightened.

The health of the body also reflects the energetic balance of the body. Normally, when problems surface, it represents a disruption of the energetic balances in the body.

Most of the problems come from internal wind, this is why most diseases derive from respiration or temperature imbalances (such as exposure to heat/cold). Bacteria travelling in the air enter the body and penetrate the body through the gut or wind tubes of the body. Depending on the internal energy balance of the body, the body's primary and secondary immune systems will attempt to restore balance.

If it was disrupted due to poor sleep, poor nutrition, dehydration, obsessive mental habits, thoughts such as pride/misery/delusion/etc, then it is the case where the body's internal balance is askew. This allows the opportunity for wind invasions.

Yes, your thoughts play a role in your body's health as well. One who often has a prideful attitude is prone to inflammatory and swelling diseases reflecting the nature of that thought. It also goes without saying that cancer is one of these "swelling" nature diseases. When one is submissive, it also reflects as contraction-nature diseases such as osteoporosis or even anemia. Be careful of your own thoughts. Watch your thoughts like a castle.

I will teach you a method that one of the masters I've learnt from has imparted. This is known as the Six Healing Sounds. This should be done just before meditation to ensure restore energetic balance to the body.

1. Breathing in, imagine taking in fresh, bright vitality.

2. Let it rinse the respective area and imagine the black, dark energy being washed out by the bright chi.

3. Using the respective mouth-shape, breathe out through the mouth without making a sound. Imagine this dark energy being washed out of the body along with the breath.

Here is the chart:

Mouth-Shape|Targeted Organs|
Hall|Heart|
Full|Stomach|

Hey|Endocrine Organs: Pancreas, Pituitary Glands, etc.|

Hway|Liver|
Chway|Kidney|
See|Lungs|

Here is how to make use of the chart, along with the steps, as an example. Here, we take the example of "Hall" for heart.

1. Breathing in, imagine taking in fresh, bright vitality.

2. Let it rinse go to the heart area and imagine black, dark energy being washed by the bright chi.

3. Using the mouth-shape of "Hall", breathe out without making a sound. Imagine the dark energy being washed out of the body.

When you do not understand energetic movement, it might be difficult to understand how a certain mouth-shape corresponds to health in a certain region. Simply treat this as a mental-device to help restore balance. Just like when you go to a doctor and trust his prescription, use this as a prescription. When you are able to do it well, your body will maintain its vitality.

Note: This should not be a substitute for restoring vitality when the body is already exhibiting illnesses.

The technique described here should be accompanied with the Physical Relaxation technique for more potency. A third method of special pranayama will be taught soon.
