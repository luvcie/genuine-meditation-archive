# Another View for Unbinding

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 11 points · 1 year ago

Consciousness is a bridge spanning multiple dimensions, connecting energy (and matter) with information, with varying frequencies, varying time, and varying space.

Matter is energetic mass, organised by fields of information that give each frequency a unique function for a unique

purpose.

Master Nan often said that the Dharmakaya was likened to an energy-wave. From it, the ten thousand things spring forth, performing different actions. As such, there is the triad - Substance, Appearance and Function.

This also corresponds to the trikaya of the Buddha. Substance is the Dharmakaya, Appearance is the Sambhogakaya, Function is the Rupakaya. This is also the reason why there are three Buddhas in every Chan hall.

For example, water can be made into ice-cubes or steam or flowing-water or spinning-water, all with different functions, according to how information organizes the substance.

Energy cannot be created nor destroyed, and can only be transformed. This is realised by modern day science. Huge amounts of information are exchanged beyond light-speed, allowing energy to change forms.

Remember, Buddhism is a science that studies life.

However, when this information is exchanged, it is also memorised within a record. This is also known as the insight-stream, by which one is able to know all things. An enlightened master will be able to speak on every topic with perfect fluency, as long as he has appropriate channels.

When one is able to tap into this insight stream, one gains access to tremendous information that is beyond one's comprehension and logical processing capability. For example, when one goes into the Oneness, one re-downloads the original blueprint of the body and becomes revitalized, regaining one's vitality and health. This is the primary function of the physiological changes in deep meditation.

Now what exactly is this energy?

Energy exists in everything. The ancient Chinese called this Qi. A thought, emotion, or bodily experience all has some form of mass, some form of energy. The experience of a self is too, a form of energy.

There is a perfect intelligence within Nature that allows delicate things to fall into place. For example, the Earth spins at a perfect angle that allows life to exist. Everything in Nature is perfectly harmonized to allow everything to exist as it is right now.

As the Indian yogis once said, if you take a piece of food and swallow it, the universal intelligence plays a role that builds your body with it, without the need for you to think of it. Therefore, they called it the food-body (anna-maya kosha).

This intelligence does not control anything. Instead, it lays out the ground for anything to happen.

By focusing awareness onto this information received from the various frequencies of energy, one can understand its

purpose.

Therefore, one can certainly play in this energy. One can do many arts, perform siddhis and miracles, heal the dead, and unthinkable things - simply because if one connects to the cosmic intelligence, the bare substance from which all arises from - but this is simply a child playing in a sand box. There is no purpose.

In the end, the purpose is to open your heart. If one establishes oneself in kind deed, action and thought, without dwelling in dharma or no-dharma, one is in perfect harmony with Nature. For such a person, when the roots of unwholesomeness and ignorance has been uprooted, and one is said to be unbound.
