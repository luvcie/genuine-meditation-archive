# Meditating Lying DOwn

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **elnoxvie** · *(OCR)* · `Discussion` · 3 points · 2 years ago

Here is a little story, i have developed a shoulder ache during sitting meditation most of the time and as a laymen, having wife and work, i don't have enough quality time to sit down and meditate all day. So one day, I just think if it is possible to lie down and meditate while not falling asleep? If yes, how do I keep myself awake.

So after a little bit of testing, I am quite confident to say the answer lies on the 2nd Jhanas.

The reason is simple in 2nd Jhana and above the bliss is strong. it is able to keep you awake even in the most comfortable bed. And the Plus side, this method allows you to get relax easily since this is the bed we are talking.

So here is the little walkthrough.

Prerequisites:

1. Reached 1st jhana. 2. Experience tingling sensation.

Walkthrough:

1. Mindfulness on the body( watch the inside of hands and leg until tingling arises) 2. Keep your awareness on the tingling until the tingling fills the body and here you are already on 2nd jhana.

I also suspected that some highly attained people sleep with jhanas so their awareness is always on but I may be

wrong.

Any corrections and advises will be appreciated.
