- **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 4 points · 6 months ago

  Simply keep still the lips and teeth (keep the teeth closed) and breathe naturally. On the exhale, chant the sutra. On the inhale, do not chant. The key is to calm down the mind and the breath, therefore, chanting slowly at first is better.

  When one gets into samadhi from chanting, they will realize that time seems to dilate - and more mantras are chanted within a short span of time. At that point, one might even 'disappear’ - I leave that for you to find out.

  Simply take up a text, understand the meaning of the sutra and every line, and recite it daily. Recite it for your parents, your loved ones.

  Chanting it aloud and proficiently, just like namo amitabha, beings will stop to hear. Chanting internally, you must still match the breathing until it ceases.

                - **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 4 points · 6 months ago

                  I have already taught the method.

                  What does it do to your mind? That is the most important thing. You are looking for methods - you will not go anywhere even if you have the most powerful method.

                  You must understand - what is the purpose of the mantra?

                  To still the mind!

                  Your spirit is scattered, your mind is wandering, the body is agitated, and you are looking for more and more things to accumulate into your ego.

                  You must drop everything right now.

                  If I advise you to do prostrations, you will ask - what is the technique to do so? How do I do it in this way or that way?

                  If I advise you to do breath meditation, your mind will float up with a thousand questions.

                  Drop everything.

                  I will point to you the mind right now.

                  If you do not understand this, it is difficult to succeed in any of your methods. You will revolve around and around and get nowhere.

                  1. Close your eyes and visualize an eagle. This eagle can be anyway that you want.

                  2. Now I want you to simply just look at the eagle in your mind's eye and know that there is an observer and the content - the eagle.

                  3. I want you to investigate this visualisation of the eagle. Is it translucent? Is it tangible? Turn it transparent. You will see that your visualisations are all intangible and have no real-nature.

                  All your thoughts are like that. Everything in your experience is just like that - intangible and empty of substance!

                  Now follow along and do this.

                  1. Close your eyes and imagine the eagle again.

                  2. Now I want you to retreat away from the eagle and back to the witnesser without any content.

                  3. What happens to the eagle? If you did it correctly, the eagle will disappear.

                  4. Continue switching back and forth from seeing the eagle and moving back to pure awareness.

                  All your thoughts are just like that - apparitions.

                  What is the use of the mantra?

                  The mantra is empty!

                  You want to dissolve everything away and reach the pure awareness.

                  I will not answer any other question until you understand this.
