# Anapanasati Training (Part 12)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 1 month ago

(16) Observe Relinquishment

Attain the Tao? What Tao? There is no Tao, so even Tao has to be relinquished. If one becomes a Buddha, “who” has actually become a Buddha? There is no person who becomes a Buddha. When one thinks they have attained the Tao, have knowledge, have achievements – then this is no longer it.

The Buddha did not enter Nirvana, because there is no Nirvana to enter. The Buddha himself is already in Nirvana. This is the Mahayana stage, also known as “Great awakening”.

When all beings enter the womb, they tend to cling onto their bodies tightly. Just like how a piece of metal becomes magnetic after electricity, your bodies also are like that. This is why the deepest teachings ask you to put them all down.

Releasing is very important, you need to become one with emptiness. Seeing impermanence, you naturally already are impermanent.

The fact that we are born in our womb, is all due to us “breathing it in”, grabbing and clinging onto it. Especially when our minds are constantly grabbing, wanting money, people, life, businesses, positions, power, desires – and never letting go. This is why when we cultivate we must observe releasing.

In fact, observing these five – Inconstancy, Releasing, Dispassion, Cessation and Relinquishment, it is from the very first step you have already used them. Do not separate these principles from the entire of Anapanasati.

This is the what the Buddha taught – the 16 steps that one will go directly into Enlightenment. Isn’t it splendid? How marvelous? How simple? Countless people have penetrated through the meaning of the suttas and reached enlightenment through this.

Don’t be attracted by all of those people talking about how Buddhism should be like. Follow the words of the Buddha. Investigate by yourself. The Buddha never speaks lies or words that do not mean anything. Every single word uttered by the Buddha is so incredibly precise, that it puzzles so greatly why so many people have not been enlightened despite claiming to follow “breath meditation”.

Strive on, and may you be enlightened in this lifetime.

This is the last of the 16 Extraordinary Victories. This is similar to the Anapanasati Sutta, as this is from the "Dharmatrāta Dhyāna sūtra". I have chosen these 16 as they can be explained step-by-step in correspondence to the jhanas.
