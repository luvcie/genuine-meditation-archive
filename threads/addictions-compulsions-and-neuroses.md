# Addictions, compulsions and neuroses

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 8 points · 1 year ago

Attachments happen when one identifies with the external sensory world, and this information becomes stored within oneself.

Therefore, the way to break away from attachment is to simply withdraw from the senses. Again and again, one pays attention to spirit. Moving towards the pleasure that is birthed not of the senses, one naturally loosens the attachment to the body.

When one is steeped in lust, it is not wise to resist lust. Instead, that is pouring oil over fire and making it spread its roots. When there is a forest-fire coming towards you, the right way is to escape the opposite way until it subsides. In the same way, when one faces addictions, compulsions and neuroses, one goes into deep meditation, again and again, and this loosens the bonds that Mara has over you.
