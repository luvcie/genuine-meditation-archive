# Power of the Heart

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Discussion` · 5 points · 2 years ago

The deeper you go, the more insight you get, the more layers you unravel around the heart and more.

But the key to all of this is in the very foundations of what so many founders have taught. If you do not practice it, how then, do you penetrate to the higher levels of wisdom?

The first thing you must understand is that everything in the world are forms of energy. Matter is energy. There is light, sound, heat, even invisible spectrums of energy. From the microscopic to the macroscopic, you see energy. The universe is teeming with energy.

You, too, are a being of energy. However, you must also notice that this entire world is impermanent. No matter how eternal an energy form might seem to be, they never stay. It is forever changing - it is forever in chaos.

What is the origin of suffering?

The Buddha said it was ignorance. Following the 12 links of interdependent origination, you see that the heart of the matter was that one continued to cling on, and this generated suffering.

On a very physical and superficial level, imagine this. When someone says something offensive towards you, you hold onto this memory in your mind. Then as you hold this memory in your mind, it grows. When it grows in your conscious mind, the subconscious and the body starts to conform to this energy.

What happens? Your body deliberately imbalances itself - to fit your thoughts. From the imbalance, anger rises - frustration rises - pain rises - suffering arises. From this, energy is imbalanced. Eventually, diseases form - tumors form, heart attacks form, disorders form... All kinds of disorders happen.

You might be aware of it, or you might not be aware of it. That is where ignorance comes in. If you were aware of it, you would not have allowed it to happen. If you were partially aware of it and allowed it to happen, then you still have a degree of ignorance.

But what matters is that for the past few lifetimes - you have done exactly the same thing.

The 6 sense gates are: Sight, Sound, Smell, Touch, Feeling and Thought. Then erroneously, you receive these sensory things in the mind, and try to find a solution through the mind. Don't forget that the mind itself is the gate of thought!

What about the heart?

The heart energy is so underutilized, even though it supports nearly 90% of our subconscious thinking. It is so powerful, it is closer to the expanse of the universe - the universal intelligence - the intuitive stream.

When you think, use the heart. Be mindful of it. Why let these things happen to you in a moment's folly?

Therefore, it is important to understand the skillfulness of sati - of mindfulness, of keeping watch at the gate. Your perception - your mind - it tends to interpret things - It says "This is nasty" or "This is painful” or "This is delightful"”.

But you must understand that all of these thoughts have already been past. They are gone forever in history. Why then, do you continue to cling on to them?

If you bear grudge in your heart, you hate yourself, you hate that person, you hate that friend - even the slightest discord - it does all kinds of things within you. Such is the nature of clinging - Clinging onto objects, you cling harder and harder - But alas things are impermanent!

They come and they go!

They go!

But what happens if you know they're all within you, but you just cannot release them? Then at least pretend. Pretend that you forgive them, and eventually, it will break through into real forgiveness. Pretend that you love everyone, and eventually it will break through into real love.

Here is an exercise you can try, to introspect.

1. Imagine light shining behind your navel (about 2 fingers in). If you cannot imagine, then tell your mind "I see light in the navel".

2. Merge with the emptiness of the universe - become one with it. There is no difference with your body or the other contents of the universe. There is no boundary separating you from the rest of the world and its inhabitants. If you wish, you can even do a tantric practice by visualizing one of the enlightened masters joining you in this practice.

3. Holding this thought, visualize bringing the light from your navel-level up to the heart area. Tell your mind "The light is shining brightly and expansively as if it was the most brilliant sun”. Feel the beauty of this powerful force in your chest.

4. Now introspect to all the previous things in your entire history. All the people, all the animals, all the beings, all the deeds, all the thoughts, all the words - See them right in front of you. And say this: "I forgive you completely. I love you completely. I let go completely.”

5. Introspect your own treatment of yourself. Bare everything with honesty. Say in your mind: "I forgive you completely. I love you completely." What is more important, is to open your heart and feel it. Don't just do it once or twice, keep repeating it. Feel it. Feel it completely until the light envelops them.

6. Don't refrain from crying or out-bursts. Let any feelings of pain, anger, frustration, hatred pour out - let the light from the heart dissolve it all. For everything, just say: "I forgive you completely. I love you completely."

7. If you do it correctly, you should either feel or visualize the light getting so bright that it extends infinitely. You merge with the light from the heart and you are one with all beings in the universe. You are one with all the masters.

8. Introspect again and again. Be completely honest. Dig down to your entire history - Memories may surface where you do not remember. Past life traumas may surface. All kinds of things can surface. But simply use the same few words: "I forgive you from the bottom of my heart. I love you from the bottom of my heart."

9. There is no beginning or end to this. Form is void, void is form. Let go to the light. Feel your heart open and let the light loose.

10. When you are ready to end, then enunciate the following mantra, saying it internally in your mind: "Gaté, gaté, paragaté, parasamgaté. Bodhi! Svaha!" This means: "Go over, go over, over to the other shore! Awaken! So be it!" Repeat it at least 3 times, but you can repeat it as long as you wish.

11. Then bring the light (which may be extremely expansive, but bring the core of it) down to behind the navel.

12. Imagine it to be like a nebula, spiraling clockwise, becoming smaller and smaller until it becomes like a small

pea.

The mantra that is used here is the Heart Sutra. The posture you can use can be the Samadhi mudra seal, with legs crossed in half-lotus or full-lotus.
