# Clarification About Jhanas

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2vpzvc/clarification_about_jhanas/>

I've received a few messages about this, so I decided to write a post to clarify this.

In total, there are said to be 8 Jhanas in total, 4 being the rupa-jhanas (material jhanas) and another 4 being the arupa-jhanas (immaterial jhanas).

Firstly, why do we say rupa-jhana? What does that really mean?

Rupa-jhana corresponds to the rupa-loka realm (Form world). See this chart[1] . If you are able to master the four form jhanas, this does not mean that you are liberated because you still need prajna-wisdom (transcendental wisdom). If you have mastered these, your rebirth will be in the rupa-loka realm.

The mistake that I want to clarify here is this: You do not have to go through all four jhanas in order to experience the 4 immaterial jhanas. Some people leap into these experiences despite not having mastered the first four jhanas. The Buddha himself went into these domains but realized that the answer were not in them.

The answer lies in the first four form jhanas.

For example, some people might be experiencing "infinite space" and having the traits of the first form-jhana. This means that you are experiencing the fifth samadhi at the level of the first form-jhana. It is possible.

Do not mistakenly think that having experienced these formless jhanas, you have already mastered the first four. This is a huge misconception.

In fact, if you were to indulge in practices such as astral travelling without having any basis in the first four realms, all you will rebirth into is that of the kama-loka (Desire realm), even lower than that of a human being. Astral travelling without the bases of the first four jhanas is extremely dangerous. In fact, it lies just at the border of insanity.

No matter what it is: Siddhis that arise, out-of-body experiences, healing abilities, clairvoyance, clairaudience... You might even be able to see into bodies, see auras, perform miracles, but do not get sidetracked.

What is most important? Remember the four noble truths. How does a person alleviate oneself of suffering?

The answer is in the Eightfold Path. The right Jhana talks about the first four Jhanas. What is most important next is the ability to have transcendental wisdom. What differentiates progress after the fourth Jhana is really the degree of wisdom that you are able to penetrate into.

I hope this helps out a few lost souls.

There is also the 9th samadhi, which known as the "Arahatta's samadhi". This still has a very subtle trace of ego, which allows the Arahatta to stay suspended between life and death, but eventually still has to reincarnate. This is the difference between an Arahatta and Bodhisattva/Buddha. An Arahatta has a clinging towards the emptiness, while a Buddha embraces existence along with emptiness.
