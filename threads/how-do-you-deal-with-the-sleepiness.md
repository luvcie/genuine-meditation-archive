- **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 2 points · 2 years ago

  A few alternatives:

  e Do standing meditation. Simply stand with your knees relaxed and slightly bent. Hands just at side, palms facing back.

  e Sitting meditation is much better than laying down if you want to maintain torpor and haven't mastered overcoming that hindrance yet.

  e Before sitting for meditation, bounce up and down with your knees for at least 5 minutes. This gets the blood, lymph and endorphins all moving and reduces the likelihood of falling asleep.

  e When attending to the breath, make sure to introduce sensitivity to bliss and joy as well. If not, it is like paying attention to a very boring subject. Instead, you want your attention to be "absorbed", which can only be - if you find something pleasant to pay attention to.

  e Also realize that torpor is succumbing to unconsciousness. It is like letting go of your ability to be conscious, and fading off into the dullness.
