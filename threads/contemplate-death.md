# Contemplate Death

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 4 points · 1 year ago

When you do breath meditation, you see birth and death. When you contemplate phenomena, you see birth and death. When looking at the five skandhas, you see birth and death.

So what exactly is Dharma?

The original question posed by the Buddha was to investigate why people suffer - and his question was: "Why do people go through old age, sickness and death?" You must examine this statement very closely. What exactly is the Dharma talking about?

Real Buddhism is to find the true meaning of life. But what exactly is life? It is not about the worth of your life, it is about finding the true meaning of your life - This is the question. The problem is that many people want everything in their reality, but meanwhile also want to attain the Tao. There is no such thing.

The Buddha teaches that we have three bodies.

The first is our flesh body given to us by our parents, and this is simply a guest-house that we stay in for a few years. On the very first day that we were born, it has already started to die. Even if you live to a hundred years, this still has to die. This very existence of the body is suffering in itself. If one lives to a hundred years, one suffers for a hundred years.

We adorn this flesh body, we dress it up with clothes, we chase pleasure and enjoyment for this body - In fact, we have been deceived by this body. This has come from karma. Virtue bestows virtue, illwill bestows misfortune. Why does your body have so many diseases? Why is there pain? In reality, this body is borrowed and not yours, therefore this is the karmic body. Now, in this body, we are suffering from past karma.

In this life, whether or not our bodies are healthy, have money, are poor, handicapped, etc - it is all from past karma. Therefore, this is not the Tao. To attain the Tao is to find the Dharmakaya, which is not subject to birth or death. Once you find the Dharmakaya, it is possible to emanate billions of incarnations.

The real thing we seek is to firstly transform the karmic body. Once the karmic body is transformed, there is no sickness or pain and it resides in a joyful and healthy state. This is the first step. Slowly, using this body, we then find the Dharmakaya. Then after, does the Sambhogakaya, or emanation capability arise.

Don't talk about emptiness when one has not attained the Dharmakaya. Emptiness is the principle of the Dharmakaya. The average Buddhist always takes the fruit for the origin, flipping cause and effect. Emptiness is what we talk about in the end.

If you sit and seek emptiness, you are having a delusion. Hence, you must seek the Dharma. How? Through the Hinayana's 37 meditation objects.

Another point to raise up is that one should ditch away superstition and mysterious thinking about chi-gong. As I have mentioned, from the day of birth, your karmic body has already started dying. The Buddha said that life lies in just the span of one breath. However, this breath - what is it that can breathe? If you can find this, then you can consider it proper cultivation. You will also understand Vajrayana, chi-gong and the Tao.

Out of the 10 contemplations, death is the tenth and is extremely important. You have to really understand death. There is no one who can live forever. You might be busy for an entire life but in the end you will die.
