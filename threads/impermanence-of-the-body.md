  I found these pictures an excellent example of contemplation of the impermanence of the body, and thought that you might find it a good article.

  This helps with your wrongful identity as the physical body. If you are afraid of death, you are clinging to the physical body as an identity, instead of a garment or tool that you can part with at any time.

  The body is temporary. Within a flash of years, it decays. With a snap of conditions, it dies. Every second in the world, thousands of people are dying. Starvation. Genetic defects. Birth problems. Heart problems. In their sleep. Cancers. Lung problems. Influenza. Falling to death. Shock. Stroke. Murdered. Victim of accidents. Haemorrhages.

  This is reality. The body is just like any other thing in the universe. If it disappears, nothing bats an eye. Your family and friends will cry for you, but like anything, feelings disappear as they are impermanent. Within a few years, the memory fades to something distant.

  You must face your cultivation with the intent that every single second is your last. Then only will you achieve liberation.

Death is the final passing away of the last fetter of the physical body when one is enlightened, so that one may cross into parinibbana or go into the Buddhic realms.

What he probably wanted was for people to realize that the Buddha that resides within the true nature does not make a person become superhuman - but simply more of a human.
