(15) Observe Cessation

What is cessation? How can you cease anything when there is nothing left? Here, one ceases receiving, one ceases thinking. There will no longer be any false thoughts, no discussion, no difference, even no feelings - One’s perception will be completely empty.

If an Arahant enters the Samadhi of cessation, the last Samadhi, one will enter the Arahant’s Nirvana. It doesn’t matter what religion, if one enters Nirvana, it is the fruit of an Arahant. At this moment, one will be able to leave the cycle of birth and death. However, this will only be a short break - since this is a subtle form of clinging onto cessation. An arahant will thus enter the stream of life and death after many, many aeons.

This is in fact not the last stage. This is known in the Chan school as the Hinayana path, and this is still not the “proper Nirvana”. One must still relinquish, before one can enter the Bodhisattva state.
