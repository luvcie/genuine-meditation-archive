# The Physical and Mental

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 8 points · 1 year ago

One has to understand that there are two layers in the life-principle. One experiences life in two ways - The Physical and the Mental.

Let us explore what the Physicality aspect of Life is. Now simply put, this physicality would be the Body Consciousness.

Before we delve into this topic, let us reiterate the Mind-Only Yogacara School teachings. The entire world is mind-only, and can be separated into 8 distinct levels of consciousness.

1. Eye Consciousness

2. Ear Consciousness

3. Nose Consciousness

4. Tongue Consciousness

5. Body Consciousness

6. Mental Consciousness (mano-vijiana)

7. Self-grasping Consciousness (manas-vijiana)

8. Alaya Consciousness (a/3ya-vijiana)

The first 5 levels are the 5 senses. These are like gates with no discriminatory function. Imagine castle-doors opened wide, and guests moving into the castle at their own pace. These all culminate as the Body Consciousness as the 5th Consciousness. Therefore, the world of the Physical are all experienced within the first five, especially the fifth.

The brain itself belongs to the body, and is not responsible for thoughts. Instead, it is, just like the body, an interface for the being-aggregate to communicate with physicality.

Now this Physicality, as the Buddha teaches, can be separated into the Great Five Elements: Earth, Water, Fire, Wind and Akasha (Space).

e Earth is the solid material - such as your body's bones, organs and muscles, or the Earth's minerals, crust and sand. Carbon is a huge component of this.

e Closely associated with Earth with high affinity is Water, which can be seen in how water plays an important role in cells, bodies and even the Earth - It is normally 70% which is crucial for supporting life.

e Again, closely associated is Fire, which is the warmth and temperature. In Chinese, temperature is called "wen-du”, which literally means the degree of moisture. Therefore, there is a definite relationship between the interplay of Earth and Water and how it contributes to Fire. However, the element of Fire can be found everywhere - even if it is at absolute Zero, there is still warmth as an opposite.

e While Earth and Water forms the various landscapes, there is a layer of air that surrounds it, which is seen in the body's cavities, such as the pericardium around the heart, the pleura around the lungs, the air inside the lungs, as well as the Earth's atmospheric layer. This air by itself cannot be detected, until it starts to move, by which it manifests as Wind. This adds an interplay with the previous other elements, creating mixtures such as scattering Water into Steam that erupts from geysers.

e Lastly is yet another invisible material - Akasha or Space. This space is often mistaken as the emptiness that Buddhism talks about, but this is not true. This occupies the space of the "aura" (but is not the aura) of a human being, which spans from one outstretched hand to the other. Outside the Earth, Akasha is outer-space.

One curious thing to note here is that there are two things are pervade the entirety of the universe: Sound and Light. Therefore, these are two methods of stream-entry that two Bodhisattvas have used. One would thus see that the Five Elements all have light to some degree. This is why psychics can see the aura around beings. In fact, every element glows, and one can detect tiny particles of light coming from the body.

Why is knowing all these important? When one stops breathing and enters the state of Xi, then it is as if one has leapt out of Wind and entered Space. Astronauts have to wear strong suits because of the immense pressure of Space - but it is in Space that huge changes can occur.

Now on the other side, we have the Mental aspect of Life.

This is referred to from the Sixth consciousness and up. Again, these do not belong to the brain. It is not inside, not outside, not in-between, not everywhere, and not not-everywhere. In other words, there is simply no concept of spatial location to which this consciousness resides, because it is not part of the Physical.

The Sixth Consciousness has various "jing-jie" (realms/states). We can have an over-active state which one of our sangha members described as a "train of thought”, while we can also have the drowsy state which we can call dreamless-sleep.

However, there are many times when this stops, and one has to learn how to recognize this gap and enter samadhi.

This topic is very wide, and one can also classify them according to the Five Aggregates. However, this is another topic and we have discussed them in the past.

To lightly cover the forms: There is "biao-se" which is positional - like movement, staying, sitting, lying, stretching, contracting, obtaining, holding. There is "xing-se", which is shape - long-short, angular-round, high-low, straight-slanted. There is "xian-se", which is green, yellow, red, white, cloud, mist, smoke, dust, bright, dark, light, and shadow.

To lightly touch on the sensations aggregate, there are 35 sensations: Light, heavy, slippery, rough, soft, hard, slow, hasty, cold, warm, thirst, boredom, strong, weak, hungry, full, sore, itchy, bloated, numbness, thick, thin, sticky, non-sticky, ageing, sickness, death, tiredness, stillness, movement, earth, water, wind, fire.

The main reason of this post is to raise your awareness so that you can take notice of these things, and treat them accordingly.

In your meditation if you perceive light, darkness, numbness, mist, smoke, or all kinds of phenomena, know that they are all still inside the aggregates. It is not to reject the phenomena, but not to be misled by them, thinking that it is enlightenment when it is clearly not.

This is all for now, strive on with diligence.
