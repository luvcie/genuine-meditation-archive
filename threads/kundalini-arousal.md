# Kundalini Arousal

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 4 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2w6o8j/kundalini_arousal/>

Real kundalini is not fiery hot. If you feel fiery heat, it is more likely the manifestation of "friction" from your chi hitting obstacles in the body. This is essential to understand.

Real kundalini is associated with prenatal chi, not postnatal chi. Postnatal chi is "ordinary chi", which is responsible for the itching and prickly sensations you feel sometimes in the body when the body is healing.

How do you tell if you have a real kundalini experience? It is when you experience all of these:

1. Strong burning (not fiery hot, but as if on a cellular level, there is a very blissful warmth) stretching from the back to the whole body.

2. Pain when you feel kundalini entering the spine. This is normal, due to the unclean nature of the body.

3. The heart starts to flutter and palpitate very quickly. You can feel as if you were going to die from a heart attack. If you experience this, you must forget the body - and there may be an extremely loud sound when it goes through the heart chakra.

4. The whole body seems as if it was shaking. There is a certain movement crawling up from the toes towards the head.

5. It normally originates from the bottom of the perineum, NOT the spine.

If the kundalini is abnormal, then you might experience the yin-form of kundalini, which is typically not blissfully warm, but burning - exhibiting fevers. This is normally a limited kundalini, as it is blocked by obstacles. The increased heat is due to friction.

This is a huge reason why you should not try to forcefully raise kundalini, but let it take its own natural path.

Also, you must be very, very careful about interpretations of these things. Sometimes, it can be postnatal chi working, not the prenatal chi. You must be able to differentiate a real heart problem and an opening of the heart chakra. Be careful.

To understand these things, take a look at the Yellow Emperor's Nei Jing text, a text on Chinese medicine, where Buddhism and Taoism practice was literally ingrained into the government. As a result, there is a huge incorporation of esoteric principles into eastern medicine (Take a look at this pdf file for an excellent English translation[1] ).
