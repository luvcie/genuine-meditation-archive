Method

The main method I switched to is the body mindfulness meditation refugeinbuddha shared. What I used what this software to understand how the body looked like. It did help that my brother studied anatomy before so I knew of this software by looking at his boockmark on a shared computer (haha).

https://www.biodigital.com/

It has a free registration, and opens up a 3d view of the body. You can also filter the images by the systems... like skeletal system for the bones that was recommended.

I also sometimes use the auditory jhana-nimitta as a meditative object to very quickly get through the first jhana. I like to vary it around so that my practice is not stale.

28/01/2015:

While meditating on the ringing sound, I found that I had a tendency to concentrate too hard, like the thread said, so I eased back and it was kind of like bobbing on an ocean wave. If it went up, I followed it up. If it went down, I folowed it down.

What happened was I suddenly had an orgasm in my head. It had a few sounds as if someone was popping bubble-wrap. I could feel the tactile nimitta increase and if I opened my eyes my vision would be obscured by light patterns. That's it for now... Will post another soon.
