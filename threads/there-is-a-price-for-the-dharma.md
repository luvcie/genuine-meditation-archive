# There is a price for the Dharma

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 8 points · 2017-11-03

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/7aj35h/there_is_a_price_for_the_dharma/>

You must practice like your body is on fire, as if you are going to die in the next hour and as if you have nothing but death to prepare for.

If you try to retain your ego, intellectualising, analysis, and so on, this subreddit is not for you - neither is the Dharma.

If you had gold and precious stones, filling all the way to Mount Sumeru and back - this will not be enough for the Dharma. If you spent aeons doing good deeds, filling them all the way for kalpas - this is not enough for the Dharma.

Do you understand the immensity of the path you have chosen to take?

If you truly understand, you will be willing to pay the price. And that price is you - you alone.

You must give up the "I", the one who hides in the shadows and claims himself as King of the kingdom. At death, this King will become your biggest traitor. If you fall down to his occult, devious ways of illusion, you will fall into an endless cycle of suffering.

You cannot retain what is "you" and still have the Dharma.

**The price of the Dharma is to have complete and utter renunciation of everything ephemeral and impermanent.**

Do not fool yourself, thinking that you can really give up all your monetary gains, your relationships, your possessions, your identity and whatever you have accumulated in this and previous lifetimes - when you cannot even give up your ***life***. When death is in one's face, one is stricken by an unconscious struggle, an illogical and irrational fear of death.

If you do not build equanimity, if you do not do the holy work and what is required, then you will undoubtedly fall prey to this.

There is so much pride and identity-view in the world today, and they are one of the last few fetters that must be burned away even after Awakening.

An example of pride is thinking that one is special by practising the Buddhist path. Or that having a little gong-fu makes one superior to someone else. Or that having access to teachings makes one better than another.

You must see that all of these thoughts are Mara luring you deep into his den, and his horde of demons are all over you. If you give in to that pride, you have lost the Way.

The real work ***starts*** after Awakening.

Everything else is prayoga - preparatory-work.

Do not be put off by the immensity of this - You need to understand that this is doeable and completely achievable. Afterall, one's own nature *is* the Buddha-nature.

But the work must be done to destroy this veil of ignorance. Until then, one will be trapped in an illusory castle of self.

So cultivate like your head is on fire. You must seek the answer night and day, day and night, and never rest until you achieve what you want to know.

"Who am I before I was born, or after I die?"

When you sit there meditating, who is calling yourself the "I" who is meditating? Why do you think there is an "I" sitting there?

When you sleep, why is there an "I" dreaming? Why is there yet another "I" when you wake up? What are you in-between dream and waking-reality?

When you eat, why is there an "I" eating? When you walk, why is there an "I" walking? When you chant the Buddha's name, why is there an "I" there again? If there is an "I" here, then is there an "I" in your fellow sangha member's body? What is the common face in all beings?

Worrying about whether you open the front or back channel, whether you can purify all your channels, whether you can attain a diamond-vajra body - all of these are extremely minor things in the face of such a question. They are milestones, but not the whole of the path. Your view must be set absolutely straight - towards liberation -

**Point the bow of your ship towards realizing your true nature.**

If you do not practice like you are on fire, your view is not set right. If your view is not set right, the entire path falls apart.

If you do not know, do not claim to know. Not knowing opens up the possibility of seeking. Not knowing opens up the spiritual process.

If only you had the ears to hear Avalokitesvara telling you who you are, if only you had the eyes to see the Buddha giving his sermon in front of your eyes in your everyday life.

Continuously, all of one's being must be pointed towards that of liberation of you and all beings. Strive on, do not loosen your resolve, do not let your ego swallow you whole.

The ego has to go - it cannot remove itself.

Can an eraser erase itself? Of course not.

**Do not let the ego deceive you**, saying that you cannot find a master, and that you might as well give up. The only problem is with yourself - if you do not work on yourself hard enough, wanting liberation badly enough - the master will not come.

He will most definitely not. A master is not someone who spoon-feeds you. There are so many other beings out there who want liberation throughout samsara - you must prove to yourself that you want it, and that must come through utter renunciation.

A master will not waste his time on someone who is in the middle, despite one's compassion and love for them, because taking in a student requires a huge cost and the master himself only has a mortal incarnation and a finite time with his body.

There are many masters in plain sight, undiscovered by you, simply because you do not have the merit, nor do you have the perception to see them.

In my years of seeking a master, I have chanced upon dharma-protectors of Avalokitesvara, one of which subdued the Monkey-God Sun Wukong, Bodhisattva emanations from previous lineages and different world-systems, even emanations of Buddhas - so do not for once think that you will not find one.

As long as you do the work, guidance will find you.

Now what is this work I speak of?

They are right view, right aspiration, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness (sati), and right samadhi. The Noble Eightfold Path is the work, yet it is also the fruit.

If one's view is inappropriate, one should work towards the right view by reading suttas, receiving proper guidance from masters, and in one's own practice. Likewise for aspiration, speech, action, livelihood, effort, *sati* and *samadhi*.

Stabilize the awareness and open up the divine-abodes (metta, compassion, empathetic joy, and equanimity).

It is not just about *you* - this is just you feeding the ego. It is about the well-being of all beings, along with you. If you are not genuinely open-hearted, loving and caring for others, you will not be able to clear the path.

One should develop the 37 Bodhipakkhiyādhammā:

- Four cornerstones of mindfulness

  - body/kaya

  - feelings/vedana

  - mental states/citta

  - mental qualities/dhamma

- Four right exertions

  - preventing unskillful states

  - abandoning already arisen unskillful states

  - arising skillful states

  - sustaining and increasing arisen skillful states

- Four bases of power

  - Will/chanda

  - Energy/viriya

  - Consciousness/citta

  - Examination/vimamsa

- Five spiritual faculties

  - Conviction/saddha

  - Energy/viriya

  - Mindfulness/sati

  - Unification/samadhi

  - Wisdom/prajna

- Five strengths

  - Conviction/saddha

  - Energy/viriya

  - Mindfulness/sati

  - Unification/samadhi

  - Wisdom/prajna

- Seven factors of Enlightenment

  - Mindfulness/sati

  - Investigation/dhamma-vicaya

  - Energy/viriya

  - Joy/piti

  - Tranquillity/passadhi

  - Unification/samadhi

  - Equanimity/upekkha

And of course, the Noble Eightfold Path.

Masters find students, just as seekers seek guides. As long as you are ernest, genuine and diligent, burning with intention and compassion, then the master will find you. This is a process incomprehensible to one rooted in the physical world. The master knows when you are ready, even before you know it yourself!

May Dharma bring you to peace.

          To enter the stream, yes, this fetter, along with 2 other fetters (doubt in the Buddha's fruits and attachments to rites and rituals) are also eradicated.
