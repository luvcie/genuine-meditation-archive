# Recognizing The Five Skandas

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 4 points · 2 years ago

Do not think that enlightenment is easy.

One of the fastest ways to gauge a being's progress is to use the five skandhas (aggregates) as a measure. In other words, has this person penetrated through all five skandhas and achieved the fruits of exhausting them? In this post I will lay down the secrets behind the five skandhas so that you can cut through delusion and false teachings.

Firstly, the average person takes the clinging-mind as oneself. When Descartes said "I think, therefore I am", he is referring to this one very crucial mistake. The clinging-mind continuously clings to objects, swinging from one object to another, as if it was a monkey.

What are objects, then? Objects are distinctions. In other words, anything that has a form is an object. The words that you hear are objects. The words that you see are objects. Thoughts are objects. As you read each word in this sentence, your clinging-mind is clinging on to each object and swinging on and on and on. This is an over-simplification, but the gist is that the average person takes this clinging-mind as the original mind.

Wrong!

Let me ask you then. When you sit in a cold room and the wind is chilly, you feel the cold sensation and this is an object. Immediately, your mind shoots out a feeling of displeasure - This is an object too! But where does it come from? The original mind is where all objects come from! When all objects disappear, the original mind remains.

If you understood this, then let me emphasize that it is only an intellectual understanding. Realizing it experientially takes work.

Now what are skandhas? Skandhas are aggregates - realms - clusters or dimensions of these 'objects’ that the mind clings on. There is the form skandha, the sensation skandha, the conception skandha, the samskara skandha and the consciousness skandha.

We do not even have to talk about the last 2 skandhas as there is only 1% of the world who have even accomplished it, or come close to really understanding what it means. You must know that these steps are inescapable - the Buddha showed the exact sequence of going through these skandhas.

The first skandha is form. When your mind starts to still until there are no thoughts, but yet there is no inner light within, this is the skandha of form. It does not matter if you think you have some kind of attainment - you are only in the form skandha. Some even think they are enlightened! That is falling into a self-created delusion and binding oneself to the form skandha.

When the form skandha ends, the inner light expands and penetrates through all forms - this is the visual jhana-nimitta, fully culminated. There is nothing this inner light does not penetrate - mountains, walls, rivers, the entirety of the world, where there is no more darkness. Some of you might have seen a semblance of this light, but if it does not stay, it is not an extinguishment of the skandha, but an accidental glimpse.

This is the second skandha, sensation. Once you see the light, what happens? The problem here is that one starts to have delusions, thinking that one is faced by evils, when it completely self-created. They feel that they cannot move, they are stuck - They might feel the expansion, but they cannot move.

When the sensation skandha ends, one leaves the body in a subdued mind-generated body or yin emanation. However, this is not that powerful - the physical body continues to have a heart rate and a temperature, etc. Even though the person experiences this emanation, he forgets about it completely. There is no mastery and even no control.

This is the third skandha, Conception.One might feel as if there is an indescribable joy and freedom as one is liberated through the emanation. The false thoughts have become comprehensive. However, the problem is that it is like sleep-talking. One doesn't know what one is doing.

When the conception skandha ends, one allows the dreamlike false thoughts to dissolve. One is the same in waking and in sleep. Sleeping reality is no different from the waking reality. He sees reality as a dream within a dream - images that come and go without a trace. All dust disappears from the illuminated nature.

This is the fourth skandha, Samskara. One remembers past lives and sees all classes of sentient beings - womb-born, egg-born, etc. One sees the original source, common motive force of all life as a haze-like blur - the blur that zips into a womb, giving rise to a physical life - the blur that spurs life in any kind of being, in all the three realms.

Then comes the last skandha, Consciousness. Ending this is great enlightenment. The Deathless is found. One no longer is bound to life and death.

Now hear these words when I say - opening the channels here and there, they are useful and set the body in prime condition, but they are foundational - what you must see is the transformation of the consciousness.

Stop clinging on to objects!

If you hear my words right now and drop all objects, you will have the slightest inkling of enlightenment.

Drop it completely!

That emptiness you hold on to - Drop it! That is an object, a realm of experience. That is not true emptiness.

Tranquility of mind is still in the form skandha. If it comes without transformation of consciousness, it is completely useless.

Do not talk about the myriads of experiential states that are trappings within the form skandhas.

You can certainly be enlightened in this lifetime - but the work must be done. The fact that you are reading my words in this post is because you have enough merit. Strive hard - drop all impermanence and find that thing that does not change, even after all objects have fallen away.
