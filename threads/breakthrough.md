# Breakthrough

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **leahyoga** · *(OCR)* · `Discussion` · 5 points · 7 months ago

So I've been meditating using the breath guide and suddenly got a lot of insights. It all just burst in suddenly.

My mind is suddenly silent. No more buzzing around of thoughts. As if I was inside a forest and all the trees just fell down. My mind is like this expansive sky. My entire body is vibrating when I meditate. Get into weird spasms. Feels like there is a hurricane going on inside and outside of me.

All I did was just keep silent. Then be lucid. Like trying to be lucid when in lucid dreaming. Then being awake I see breath. Then I realized anything I put attention on is like putting my hand on a table to hold my balance. Then I would accidentally shift attention to something else in the past. But now it just disappears as soon as it arises. And tons of light shining inside and I am having funny orgasm-like feelings throughout body. It is almost like a bubbling spring.

Is this jhana! :o
