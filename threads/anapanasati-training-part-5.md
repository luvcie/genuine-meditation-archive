# Anapanasati Training (Part 5)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 1 month ago

(6) Receive joy & (7) Receive bliss

When you receive joy, you feel extremely happy, beyond any kind of happiness. This is when you know that the Dharma is real. This is not an ordinary happiness. It is knowing that your cultivation in this life is finally correct, even if it is just a shadow of it. Your heart opens up. However, this is still not bliss.

When you receive bliss, every single cell in your body feels happiness. It is indescribably and immeasurably comfortable and refreshed. It is at least a hundred times more blissful than sexual intercourse.

However, at any point, you have not left the “knowing nature”. You are still knowing the joy and knowing the bliss.

This is the first jhana. It is a feeling of separation from the Earth, with immeasurable happiness. The entire body is filled with joy and bliss.

Do not confuse this joy and bliss with the normal joy and bliss that you feel when normally meditating. There is a huge difference, in fact, the degree is too huge.

What is first jhana? This is the first proper Samadhi, where your distracting thoughts are completely quiet.

Firstly, you will realize that the “knowing nature” is separate from the feelings of the body. You will realize the different flavors of the four great elements of fire, water, air and earth.

The Buddha described that this life, this soul which leaves the body is just like a “bird out of its cage”, as if one’s heart was like the bird that was freed from its cage. This then transcends the flesh body, the obstruction of matter, and feels blissful beyond compare.

Joy is mental and psychological happiness, while Bliss is the change of the four great elements.

If you cultivate until you reach first jhana, your temperament, personality and habits all change. If you were to die, you will be reborn in the first form heaven that corresponds to the first jhana. Do not be mistaken, this is not the heavens spoken of in other religions, it is in fact in a much higher plane. This is the heaven where one jumps out of the Desire realm, which is where human existence is. The Desire realm has an association with lust and desire. When you are in the Form realm, you no longer have lust or desire, and all cravings will be purified.

Does this mean that one is not in Samadhi until you reach first jhana? That is in fact not true. There are many kinds of Samadhi. In the first few steps, normal meditation is what we can call a Samadhi of the Desire realm. There are many different kinds of Samadhi even so, with varying degrees.

There is no way to reach this through any form of intellectualizing or rationalization. You may debate this all the while, but you can only reach this state through cultivation alone.
