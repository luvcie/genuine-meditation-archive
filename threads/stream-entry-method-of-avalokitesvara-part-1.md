# Stream-Entry Method of Avalokitesvara (Part 1)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 1 year ago

Avalokitesvara is a Bodhisattva who recounted his stream-entry method to Gautama. The Buddha Manjushri then praised this method, claiming it to be the best possible method for beings of the Saha world (this world) to achieve liberation. Remember that one should simply stick to one Dharma Door. This is yet another explanation of another door, just like Anapanasati, etc. What matters is mastering one Dharma door, and using it to attain stream-entry. Jumping around will only cause confusion.

Don't use the 6 senses — when the six consciousnesses do not move, as if they were separate from you, you can meditate even in a busy town. There will be no need to want a quiet environment.

When thoughts come, you do not have to bother them, neither do you invite them. Naturally, they will disappear by their own. The more you want to take notice of the thoughts, the more you agitate and bring out more thoughts, and they become an external sound. If you can penetrate the understanding of this insight, you will gradually enter Avalokitesvara’s method of entry. The moment you sit, you do not care about your delusions and false thoughts. For example, when you hear the sounds of the busy street, one can think of them as the sound of the tides of the ocean. The sounds of an ocean can be very loud. However, you can hear but yet not be disturbed. It is not about “"not hearing” the sound, because this would be no different from a dead person. It however, is also not about purposefully trying to hear it.

When the sound comes, this is movement. When the sound goes, this is quietness. Movement and quietness are simply opposites of a phenomena. The function of hearing does not lie in solely movement or quietness - it knows when there is movement, and it knows when there is quietness.

When all Bodhisattvas described their own methods of stream entry to the Buddha, it was Avalokitesvara’s method that was highly praised as the best method for beings of this Saha world. This is because sound cannot be blocked by front, left, right, up, down, inside, outside, and any of the ten directions - this can lead to perfect stream entry.

"Om” is the sound of the head (headache/colds). "Ah” is the sound of the breast (chest diseases). "Hung” is the sound of the navel (digestive system diseases).

The human body originally has light, the universe also originally is light. Light and sound fill this universe, even up to space. Space looks like a black hole, but black is also light! Black color is black light, white color is white light - any color is light. For example, diamond and coal is composed of carbon, it is only the arrangement of atoms that is different. Diamond is formed by octagonal structure, made from high pressure. When diamond is dissolved, it will be exactly the same as coal. In the same way, dim light and bright light is only the matter of different luminosities.

If one practices this Dharma door, one will see huge results in simply cultivating for half a day. However, one should know that if you practice this, it is possible to initiate abilities, and you may be able to hear sounds out of this world. You may also naturally know things that are about to happen. If you follow this direction, it is very possible that you go into an errant or demonic state, where you are self-satisfied with this ability and hence block the path of enlightenment. If one were to use the example of a hundred floors, supernatural ability is only at the second level, and one will be stuck if they indulge in this, never reaching the hundredth.

The Dharma is an extremely scientific investigation. Every individual will take different roads. If one chooses one Dharma door, they should stick to this one door until they die. There are people who recite the Great Compassion Mantra, recite the Buddha’s name, recite the Six Word Mantra (om mani padme hung), cultivate the Six Excellent Doors, White skeleton visualization, etc. All of these methods can result in enlightenment, but the note is that one should not change here and there and stick to just one method. If you cannot choose, then use Avalokitesvara’s door.
