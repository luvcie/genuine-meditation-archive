# Bubble

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda*

Bubble
A bubble rises
in the ocean
And disappears
soon after.

Grasping it
It pops
What you hold
Is already gone.

Like a lightning flash
Across the sky
It is gone
without a trace.

Light, smells,
Hearing, touch
Taste, thoughts
Their nature
Is just like that.

What Bodhi?

What purification?

Huineng's mirror
has no surface
for dust to land.

Just like that
Asking a question
Your mind is open.

Dwell but
not dwell.

An empty glass
is not empty.

Emptiness is not
the way.

Emptiness is
A bubble too.

Nature does not
stand still -
Leaves sprout
they turn red
and they fall.

The birds fly
away from winter
and back
when
the time is ripe.

Facing evil,
one should see
that blackness
is too,
a color
And that its nature
is that of seeing.

Your heart
is small -
Let it not
hold onto dust.

Do good deeds
Speak kind words
Think good thoughts
Build merit
if you do nothing.

Having lived
a good life,
one does not
fear death.

Live with heart,
Move with wisdom,
the empty glass
is not empty.

Chase not
servants of the ego
abandon all self
and give.

This is the
Bodhisattva's way.

Giving, not knowing
that one is giving.

To one who follows
the dharma -
the Teacher will
always be
in you.

Trust the wisdom
Let go, let go.

Study well,
Be clear on the Dharma,
and that is how
you avoid Mara.

Buddha, buddha.

Dharma, dharma.

Sangha, sangha.

May all beings
take refuge
in the triple gem.

Strive well, and may you be enlightened.
