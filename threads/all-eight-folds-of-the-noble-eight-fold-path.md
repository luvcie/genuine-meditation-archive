# All Eight Folds of the Noble Eight Fold Path

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 1 year ago

I have been receiving a lot of messages about meditation, but no one asks anything about discipline (sila), the monastic or layperson's code, or even wisdom (prajna).

The Noble Eightfold Path is complete and not one fold should be found missing. Why? Because its carrying out is the semblance of being enlightened.

One who has awakened in the first stage naturally carries out the Noble Eightfold Path and is called a stream-enterer. By this time, one has gained the 5 fruits of the holy life.

The Noble Eightfold Path is as the following:

Right view

Right intention

Right speech

Right conduct

Right livelihood

Right effort

Right mindfulness/sati

Right jhana/samadhi

If any of them are missing, then it is no longer the genuine noble eightfold path. The goal of this is to develop clarity into the insight of reality - the three marks of existence (dukkha, anicca, anatta), abandon unwholesome states (avidya) and ultimately reaching nibbana.

Right view can be subdivided into two forms:

Mundane right view: This brings merit, supporting favorable rebirths in the future.

Supramundane right view: This includes understanding of karma and rebirth, leading to liberation from rebirth and dukkha.

There is still a lot to be said about the noble eightfold path, so one should constantly study the Pali Canon especially, and read up on the precepts that the Buddha gave.

In fact, simply following perfect virtue can lead to the jhanas. Why? Because even the acts of virtue lead to the opening of energetic channels in your body, as mind and body are linked. On top of virtue, one restrains the 6 senses, is skillfully mindful, abandons luxuries to live simply, and abandons the five hindrances.

This is written in the Samannaphala Sutta of the Digha Nikaya.

So please do not erroneously think that the Buddha's Dharma only lasts on your short time on the mat. Every single breath you take - every step you walk - every word you speak - the Dharma is found right there.

Strive hard for liberation.
