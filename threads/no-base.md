# No base

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 1 year ago

No base

There is no ego, no personality, no being, and no notion of a self.

If you believe that “the self” can “unself”, then you are mistaken. An ego cannot get rid of itself.

If you believe that there is a “Self” outside of this ego, then you are falling into the ego’s game, reaching yet for another mental illusion. Advaita teachers substitute a “Self” for the “self”, but this does not work. It has to cease, not move yet to another mental construct.

How can an ego be less egoistical? An ego will only continue to be an ego. As long as you look through a telescope with a red lens, everything inside the lens will appear as red. You cannot try to make it “less red”, because its very nature is red.

Some try to get the ego to become more compassionate, more mindful, have good conduct, etc – but you should focus on the illusory nature of the ego and what it experiences! Doing that, when one lets go, they manifest all of those qualities naturally.

The ego has to cease itself. It cannot project itself into a different form, or it will be an endless ego game.

When the Zen Patriarch Hui-Neng wrote his poem, he said that the Bodhi mind is surfaceless, because there is no dust that can ever rest on it. On the other hand, the unenlightened elder monk wrote that the Bodhi-mind had to be cleaned continuously, because there is dust that rests on it.

How can dust rest on nothing? How can ego, or any concept, let alone a “Self” rest on it?

The Tao that can be spoken of in not the Tao.

Both the *observer *and the observed has to vanish.

When the body’s sensations are received, the gates of the five senses, as well as the thoughts in one’s mind, the subconscious binds them together into a single entity.

The conscious mind understanding this cessation, thinking about this, imagining itself to be this, is not the real thing. It has to be a “knowing” at the deepest level – from the consciousness itself that is doing the projection of a self. This way, the projection shatters, and no ego will be found.

When you say “I” – that is a name, and a name can only be attached to things that have substance. That which cannot be spoken of, cannot be uttered, cannot be – is that which lies behind all of existence.

When one awakens to this, one finds that one was never the “personal self” or narrow self-identity they always thought they were.

Remember. All methods, all “no methods”, all devices, all movements, all concepts, all teachings, etc – They are simply fingers pointing to the moon.

Don’t look at the finger, look at the moon. But when you look at the moon, don’t “think” it is a moon. If you think of it, it is simply a reflection of the moon in the water. Just go in the direction of the finger.
