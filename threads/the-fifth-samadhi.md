How do you know if you've really separated as a mind-generated body?

Sometimes it might not be that obvious that your astral body has already left the physical body. This is because the moment you become an astral body, wherever you think - you immediately appear to.

The suttas describe it like a snake (astral body) shedding its skin (physical body), but you might find that the experience might not be as gentle as that, perhaps even abrupt.

If you are in a complete expanse of space, a black void, then you might want to realize that you are in the expanse of outer-space.

The key is not to confuse this with anything else. In the fourth Jhana, you would already have somewhat of a transitional stage. You still have the sensation of a physical body, but you might experience infinity of dimension and timelessness.

I will add more to this post when more people get into this stage.

      > What is the difference between "sensation of a physical body" and a mind-generated body?

      Sensation of a physical body refers to the external or internal senses using the eyes, nose, skin, tongue, ears and brain. A mind-generated body is what people call a subtle or astral body, which is mind-derived as well, but has access to the full expanse of spiritual faculties such as sight (clairvoyance), hearing (clairaudience) as well as all the counterparts to memory, insight, smell/taste, tactile (which is the OBE experience itself), etc.

      > How does a mind-generated body appear?

      I do not know the exact process, although I have experienced it, so I cannot give an affirmative answer. The Taoists have a theory that it is the accumulation of chi that leaves through the crown, generating what they call an "inner yin body". However, it appears that this body is completely controlled by will - as well as its faculties.

      > Is there a predominately a first or third person perspective of the mind-body?

      First-person, and possibly even more. You look directly into the nature of things. You can see auras, etc. You have the sight of an eagle. You teleport instead of having to fly.

      > Is it possible to bring the perception of a mind-generated body to cessation while remaining in the arupa-jhanas?

      This is where you slowly graduate from the fifth samadhi to the higher realms. You move from infinite space to infinite consciousness, and so on. It is all a very systematic approach.
