# Post Your Meditation Journal

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Notice` · 5 points · 2 years ago

I highly recommend people to post their meditation journal. The reason for doing so is five-fold:

1. Other experienced members can comment and newer members can learn from your experience.

2. By reflecting, you understand what were the conditions that brought you to that state and you can become more consistent in your progress.

3. When looking back after you have progressed, you can then look with a different perspective at your previous experiences.

4. You can write down insights, then build upon them in future when you have opened up more levels of wisdom.

5. It ensures accountability and allows you to have a dedicated schedule towards liberation and proper meditation.

It does not have to be a long journal. It can be simply, "I sat down 20 minutes to meditate. To add a thread, simply create a thread, then under the message, change the Flair to "Journal”.

Looking forward to seeing your journals. Of course, this is up to you. I do hope that everyone comes together as a fine community of contemplative friends.
