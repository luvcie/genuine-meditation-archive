  It's not literally air... but the feeling as if wind is passing through in waves through the body. You can't technically ‘increase’ the air.

  Go into deep meditation and it will increase... Do anapanasati, watch breath in... gap... breath out... gap... stay aware but don't do anything. Calm body down, down down... Relax... Let go...

  So mind can become dull... making you sleep... or mind can become agitated... making your mind frustrated... or mind can become crowded, full of thoughts... or mind can become slothful, unable to be aware of what is happening...

  The key is.... whenever you know it happens, then you can prevent it from taking over your mind. Kind of being a security guard... if you know the intruder is coming, then you can stop him... if not you'll just let him pass by without noticing.

  To get through these mini-hindrances, you can easily get through them by putting a slight smile on your face and becoming compassionate and loving, by thinking of something loving... like embracing a young baby, or petting a baby dog/cat... or just lying down on the beach against a palm tree with the wind in your face... that kind of thing.

  So key is... be aware, STAY aware... even when these things come out to distract you... keep the awareness strong like a torchlight that gets brighter and brighter... NOT TIGHTER, BUT RELAXING/LETTING GO... then you'll suddenly find yourself in a state where the outside-breath becomes deep/imperceptible... at that point you can start to feel all that energy stirring in the body because the mind is becoming still. That's inner-wind!

  But don't play around with it when it comes, just let it do whatever... It's like when you open a window and the room gets a gust of air... the air's balancing the pressure, kinda like that... Just keep saturating in joy and bliss that arises with it...
