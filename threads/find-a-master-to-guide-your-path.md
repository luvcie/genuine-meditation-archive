# Find A Master To Guide Your Path

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 9 points · 5 months ago

My late teacher: http://i.imgur.com/z3YYYQM.jpg

As I've expressed many times, I cannot be anyone's master or teacher, neither will I agree to. All I can be is your friend, and I cannot accept anything of the status of a teacher. I try to share as much as I can in the subreddit, but all of this advice is frankly - preparatory work/pre-yoga - until you truly find a master that can guide you on your path.

True cultivation is not easy and that is why you need someone who has been on the Path to guide you. Being lost for innumerable kalpas, it is not easy to undo this problem.

The true master is inside you, the inner Guru, the Buddha-nature.

You are a Buddha that has been blinded by ignorance and swept up in samsara, taking the erroneous aggregates as the self. You are not this illusory self, you are the Universe - you are one with all the Buddhas of ten directions.

I can only hope whatever I have posted here is of help. In fact, I have complicated the Path with more words. The Tao that can be spoken of is not the Tao, there are simply no words that can describe it. Any further words do it no justice.

If you are really unable to find a master, then do the Cundi dharani. Chant it day in and out, chant it to calm down the mind and turn it away from mental, verbal and physical sankhara. This pulls you away from the impermanent streams that drag you along. Do a million repetitions - the Cundi Mother Buddha herself will teach you. Don't think a million repetitions is that easy! Chanting fast does not work - the mind must be stilled - internal recitation.

Additionally, read the sutras. Not the commentaries, unless it is by a true master who has shown the fruits and has penetrative wisdom. Or - just read the sutras. The deeper your practice, the more you will understand what the simplest instructions mean.

I bow at your feet friends, I love you all, and may you be enlightened in the Path. Strive well, strive hard and do not give up. The Path only rewards those who have feverish intent to liberate.

When I'm gone, please work hard. This body will not last long, just like any body. Your life is short and it will pass faster than you realize. This life is so short, when you go to the next life, you may not be a human being. Practice warm-heartedness, charity, kindness, humility, benevolence and gratitude. Do good deeds every single day. Don't think any kind of accumulation of memory makes you superior in any way - it makes you inflexible. Empty the cup and just sit, every single day. Bring it to your daily practice.

              Perhaps I can put it in a different way:

              There is no choice but to give.

              Why?

              Simply because everything is empty in nature. This means that things arise from conditions, age from conditions, and die from conditions. The moment anything rises, it is bound for death. This is the law of impermanence.

              Therefore, 'giving’ is natural - another phrasing could be to 'let go'. Why do you let go? Well, because it is impermanent. The only reason why you cling onto anything is simply because you take it as the self.

              When a monk sits there with a bowl to beg for alms, he accepts whatever comes his way. Making no discrimination whatsoever, he eats from the bowl. This is the highest method. Eat what is given, shit what is done. In the same way - thoughts come, thoughts go. At no point do you dwell.

              Charity comes on all three levels - thoughts, speech and actions. What you think, speak or do - you give up the ego - you do what is needed, what is beneficial, and what is wise for all.

              Sitting down on the mat, you just sit. Things come, things go.

              However, most people sit - and their habitual stream comes back in - unconscious and unaware, they are dragged and entangled within it. Entangled within it, they cannot tell the difference between thoughts and who they are.

              Therefore, for most people, it is a reversing process to cut off all of these - so that you can reach the point where you simply sit. Nothing else is added or subtracted.
