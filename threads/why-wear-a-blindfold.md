# Why wear a blindfold?

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 7 points · 8 months ago

This is in response to a few questions posted and sent to me by PM. One of the biggest obstacles that people face is the lack of knowledge towards things in practice.

It is of utmost importance that one learns Buddhism well. Practice is one thing, but one must also read, memorize and study! This does not mean commentaries - This means receiving the wisdom of the sages who have gone through it before - the suttas and shastras!

If you are unable to recite the principles by heart, you have not studied enough.

All the sages of the past, even Zen/Chan masters, have studied and memorized the scriptures, knowing them to the absolute core. If you were to pick out one question, they can answer you intellectually and perfectly. This is mundane wisdom, while it is not supramundane, it cannot liberate you, but it is absolutely required.

Why? Because there is so much room for error in cultivation. One can easily find oneself dwelling in a samadhi, of which there are millions of, thinking that one is enlightened.

For example, there was one teacher who was shooting light from his fingers, showing emanations of Bodhisattvas. Who would not be amazed? But if you know the Buddhist principles - it says: "Do not seek the Buddha in light or form."” You know immediately that it is a side path.

It is immensely important!

Scriptures like the Surangama Sutra, Diamond Sutra, Lankavatara Sutta, Yogacarabhumi Shastra, Dharmatraya Dhyana Sutta, all the Hinayama Agamas/Pali Canon should be studied and well-memorized.

Studying them, do not cling onto them! Don't reverse effect for cause! You have not personally realized them. Studying serves as a guide to prevent you from falling into mara states and as a result of weak wisdom. The Buddha and the Bodhisattvas have been so compassionate to leave these words of wisdom, how can you let them pass you by?

Do not wear a blindfold in cultivation. If you do not know, read it and study it well. Knowing the principles well, you will know what to do at the right moments.

For example, if one goes into deep bliss, one nearly immediately throws away their understanding that everything is impermanent - and that the four foundations of mindfulness are all to be skilfully mindful of. If one is at the brink of death, body diseased and sick, unable to meditate, then one will only have themselves to blame! Do you think the Dharma is only on the mat? No!

It is of utmost importance if you wish to be truly liberated from samsara.
