# Use the heart-mind to watch

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 8 points · 1 year ago

The knowing-ness does not come from the brain.

When one uses the heart to look at phenomena, fewer thoughts will run in the brain.

How does one sustain attention on something?

Have you seen a child become transfixed to a cartoon on the television? Firstly, the child is curious and watches the cartoon to know what it is about. Secondly, the cartoon grips the child’'s mind and effortlessly, the child is transfixed on the cartoon.

In the same way, when one attends to anything, including a meditation object, one watches the phenomenon to know what it is doing. Secondly, this transitions into an effortless attention, simply because one is enjoying it.

What do you think binds attention easier - something that is not pleasurable, or something that is pleasurable?

In the same way, remove the five hindrances so that the mind is in a wholesome state by appropriate investigation. Then, the mind that is bright and pliant, will naturally find the meditation object pleasing, and gravitate towards it.

When you hold a hamster lovingly in your hands, what do you do? Too light and the hamster drops down onto the floor, injuring itself. Too tight and the hamster is injured by your force. In the same way, tend to your meditation object.

> In the same way, remove the five hindrances so that the mind is in a wholesome state by appropriate investigation. Then, the mind that is bright and pliant, will naturally find the meditation object pleasing, and gravitate towards it.

This has been my experience so far. The more I observe my feelings/sensations, the more they calm down, the more they disappear, the more wholesome feelings I feel,. Then it's easier to focus on anything.

At the moment I focus on the 5 hindrances as they appear in my daily life. I want to remove those to gain time and energy for breath meditation. Then I will focus on my 5 hindrances when they appear during meditation. Then I will focus on achieving jhana.
