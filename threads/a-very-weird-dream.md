  Your second experience could've been sleep paralysis: a common phenomenon that occurs usually because of a lack of sleep or a change in your sleeping schedule. There's nothing to be worried about however. Dreams are dreams, just as your reality is a dream. They have no affect on the self, and generally serve no meaning. To create meaning for a dream would be to dwell in the transient.
