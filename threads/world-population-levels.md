# World Population Levels

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Discussion` · 5 points · 2 years ago

Take a look at this population clock to see the numbers in real-time around the world: http://www.worldometers.info/world-population/

This is a small measure of how fragile human life is, yet how quickly lives are brought into this world.

Feel fortunate that you have lived today and are cultivating the Dharma. Every single second, people lose the chance of receiving the Dharma. Every second, many rebirths take place. Every morning, thousands of people did not live to see the next day.

Take into perspective how short your human life is. It cannot be wasted on frills and mental fantasies. If you want to grasp the Dharma without any kind of cost, then that is mere fantasy. In the past 2500 years since the passing of the Buddha into parinibbana, only 5-6 people have accomplished full awakening, with the acquisition of all 3 bodies (dharmakaya, sambhogakaya and nirmanakaya).

Just today, literally hundreds of thousands of lives have been lost. Nearly a million lives are lost in just the past week.

Do not take your life for granted. Treat every second as if it was your last. Do not squander the chance.

If you ever feel unfortunate, then look at yourself and your loved ones around. They are still there, so are you. What is there to be miserable about? What is there to hate? What is there to be envious about?

A great gift is the wonder of life, but even that passes away. The Dharma however, does not. Strive well, cultivate to open your dharma-eyes and see what is right there.

Realization means awakening to the truth that has always been there. Nothing is ever original. It is simply realized in a certain way.

The world is not as stable as you take it to be. It is ever-changing. You do not notice but the Earth swings around the sun, but yet the whole solar system swings around the Milky Way. We are never at the same state. The planet flies at astronomical speeds - Being oblivious to it all is being blind to the ever-changing nature of things.
