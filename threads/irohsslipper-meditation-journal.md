Been meditating for around 1 hour and 15 mins every morning the last 3 or so days, was doing around 45 mins before along with various sessions throughout the day to bring it up to around 2 hours a day. Last week i was hitting a point in my meditations where i experiencing pleasurable sensations buildilng in the body in every session, im not sure if it's full blown jhana, but once the mind becomes quiet and concentrated it feels like an inner energy starting to flow over my whole body, no goosebumps or tingling sensations but still very blissful.

Had about 4 days after this where i was meditating and seemingly doing the same process yet i kept getting a large build up of pressure in my face and head, upon reflection i think i was trying TOO hard to get to jhana and to concentrate.

The last few days i have watching a few videos, reading variours tests relating to breath meditation and realised i was clinging to the previous sensations, and as a prerequisite for advancing within the path of breath meditation i should incorporate a bit of relaxation at the start of the meditation, present moment awareness and silence of the mind before i meditate.

The last few days have again brought about pleasurable sensations and what seems to be a recurring theme - erections when these sensations arise. I have been making a much bigger effort to remain mindful throughout the day and it's a very lovely feeling, made easier when there is a flow of pleasurable sensations in my body for hours after meditating.

My immeditate goals are to master 1st jhana, the bliss like sensations are there but i want to be able to enter, leave and control at will. Like i said before im not sure if it's actual jhana im experiencing but there are factors for sure.

Just going to keep meditating and upping the intensity/amount done every day, i feel this path calling to me and i love it. Exploring the mind is the greatest game, learning to remain present also is very fun.

I will update this journal every 3 days or so. Peace to you all and i hope everyone is having good meditations and insights
