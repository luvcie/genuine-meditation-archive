# The Demonic States

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2yhz5h/the_demonic_states/>

What exactly is a demon?

Many people who practice become quite concerned about demons. Firstly, let us differentiate inner demons and external demons. The demons that we normally refer to are "inner demons". Now these demons can be psychological afflictions, emotions, states of mind, etc.

Now these things are very hard to tell apart from enlightenment. There are many, many deluded beings who think that they have some spiritual attainment. However, the truth is that they have become possessed by their inner demons. The worst thing is that these people cannot tell that they have fallen prey to the subtle inner demons.

How can you recognize such demonic states?

• These people cannot keep to the precepts of Buddhism, although they may even preach about Buddhism. They lie, steal, etc. This is normally the standard of recognition. There are quite a few frauds out there who do not realize themselves that they have fallen prey to such states.

• There are subtler demons that can be hard to tell apart from an enlightened being. These people can become angry and vengeful when people do not listen to them. This represents a state of affliction and lack of equanimity. The main thing is that they seek a form of power.

• If you wish to see all the various forms in which one can fall prey to a demonic state, take a look at this section of a Mahayana sutta, the Surangama Sutta[1] , as the Buddha explains it to Ananda.

When in jhana, you are untouchable by demons, just like boiling water to ice. However, if you were to interpret something unattained as attained, that would be becoming confused by the five aggregates - And that would be the possession of a demonic state.

Now there certainly are external demons and mischief from malevolent beings. However, you should not be worried about them, even if you meet them. Just like the saying "Misery loves company", if you were to stoop to their level, then you would certainly be affected by them. If you were to treat their actions as nothing, then no power can be given to them. If you were to meet them, all you have to do is to intonate your refuge in the Buddha: "Namo Amitābha". From personal experience, this is really all that is needed. The simple act of saying the name causes vengeful spirits to depart. Demons will not want to touch you when you are in jhana and equanimous. This is why equanimity (having removed the psychological afflictions) is extremely important.

This is why you have nothing to fear about, even for external demons. They are afraid of you when you practice the right path. What you must be cautious in is to never fall into delusion and thinking that a small attainment is a huge attainment. This would be akin to "creating an inner demon" and falling into it. It is a little like insanity or self-created psychosis.

How can you make sure that you do not meet demons? Well, it depends on your kamma. Can you keep up to the precepts and sila? One thing is that you should never be afraid of them. Work on equanimity and no power can be given to them at all.

Strive hard for enlightenment.
