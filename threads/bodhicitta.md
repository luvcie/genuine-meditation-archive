# Bodhicitta

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 7 points · 1 month ago

Friends, if one does not engage in loving-kindness, one does not have the teachings of the Buddha. Friends, if one does not practice this, one does not too, have compassion, sympathetic joy and equanimity.

As expressed in the Cullavagga:

> For those without feet, I have love. I have love for all with two feet. For those with four feet, I have love. I have love for all with many feet. May those without feet do me no harm. May none with two feet do me harm. May those with four feet do me no harm. May none with many feet do me harm. May all beings, all living things, All who've come to be — one and all — May they see every blessing! May no evil at all come to them! Without limit is Buddha. Without limit is Dhamma. Without limit is Sangha.

These four immeasurables are the Mind of Buddha (bodhicitta). They too, are called the Divine Houses of God, or Divine Abodes.

Towards the virtuous and the wicked, the friend and the enemy, the pleasant and the unpleasant, the being or non-being, the legged or non-legged, the womb-born or other-born, the human or non-human - all six realms of sentient beings, as well as the Buddhas in all ten directions - sit in quietness and give rise to these minds.

Pervade with metta the front quarter, the back quarter, the left quarter, the right quarter, the upper quarter, the bottom quarter, all around, far-reaching, omnipresent.

How does one pervade in such a manner? One firstly holds mindfulness to the fore, holding sati as fore, one recognizes the rising of unwholesome states (cittassa upakkilesa). Rooting them out from the psyche, one is then able to permeate one’'s mind fully with the four brahmaviharas.

Strive well and may you attain enlightenment in this lifetime.
