(5) Get rid of the body

From this stage onwards, you no longer care about the "knowing" anymore. This is because the "knowing" is always there, it has never moved. "Knowing" has no more use from this stage onwards, so just leave it be.

In this stage, one gets rid of the obstacle of the body. Without the feeling of the body, body and emptiness become one. All obstacles of the body are completely gotten rid of, and the body is filled with chi. In fact, all the organs and even the central nervous system itself has changed. The autonomic nervous system's organs, or Ren mai, have completely changed.

In this stage, one will experience light raining down through the crown from the emptiness/void, giving an extremely freeing and cooling feeling.

There are many secrets here, when this is mastered. For example, it is possible to walk on snow without a footprint, or you may float without touching the ground while walking.

Here, the 3 channels of Indian Yoga and the 7 chakras are said to be completely opened or purified. If one checks their heartbeat, modern doctors will think that you have an abnormally slow heartbeat.

In fact, one's life habits will change. Lust disappears, and there is no stress whatsoever. One finds that they do not like superficial things. It is still possible to force oneself to do it, but it will be as if one is chewing tasteless wax. Even sex will become like an exercise, with no lust.

Why so? It is because the body has been gotten rid of. Without the body, how can there be any thought of body-related lust?
