(12) Observe inconstancy

What is observing inconstancy? It is not “knowing”, not your mind, nor your body. This involves wisdom.

The physical world, spirit world, body and mind are all impermanent. Inconstancy is talking about phenomena, even the stages that you have been through previously. All the stages, the joy, the bliss – all of them are impermanent. All things in the world constantly change, and when they pass, it is as if it was a dream or fantasy.

The cultivation method is only a crutch, do not be trapped by the crutch itself. See the impermanence in all things.

Inhaling and exhaling are also inconstant, aren’t they? The Buddha said that everything in the world is inconstant, everything is suffering, everything is empty, and that everything is empty of self.

The three marks of existence are: Inconstancy, no-self, and suffering.

When you use a method to cultivate, do not become trapped by the method you use! Otherwise, you will think that it is permanent, when it is in fact impermanent as well.

In fact, it is not just this step that you see impermanence. Right from the very first step, you have already been observing inconstancy.

In order for you to understand the Buddhist teachings, your wisdom must be combined with Samadhi in cultivation.

Even if you can cultivate yourself until you can fly around, what is so extraordinary about that? It is also inconstant, isn’t it? If you don’t cultivate, don’t practice it, your skills will wear away. That is why these superpowers spoken of are still impermanent.

This is not a stage, but a way of penetrating into wisdom.

There are many ghosts, many of which are not attained, who have supernatural abilities. They can see the future and past, they can float through walls, they can teleport short distances, etc. What is so marvellous then, about such abilities, if they do not show that one is enlightened?

One might train to be able to bilocate, split themselves into flesh bodies, have telepathic powers, be able to read minds, be able to talk to spirits, etc - But still not be enlightened. There are many people like this - they master a certain samadhi state and produce certain abilities - thinking like this, they deceive themselves that they have attained the Tao. Worse, they lead people along with them to the wrong Dharma.

Be aware that supernatural powers do not equate to enlightenment - and instead, pursuing them will lead down states of delusion. Such disasters have been seen throughout the yogic and tantric traditions, and have people cast into rebirths stuck in a horrid cycle.
