# The 4 Changes Of Right Meditation

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 9 points · 1 year ago

Whether one is practicing body mindfulness (or the white skeleton), or Anapanasati, or any other meditation technique, there will always be 4 changes of the body that will occur.

1. Pain disappears

2. Comfort

3. No more sense of the body

4. Light emanates from the body

When one properly sits in meditation and chi flows smoothly, one will feel blissful, joyful and warm.

However, even if chi does not flow smoothly, it works its way through, and one can feel any of the 35 phenomena:

> Light, heavy, slippery, rough, soft, hard, slow, hasty, cold, heat, thirst, boredom, strong, weak, hungry, full, sore, itchy, bloated, numbness, thick, thin, sticky, non-sticky, ageing, sickness, death, tiredness, stillness, movement, earth, water, wind, fire.

All of these are realms - realities. Just as anything that appears in front of you, they are all shifting realities. If something can appear, it is also able to disappear.

The key instruction is simply to non-dwell. As they happen, acknowledge their coming, then simply ignore them and let them leave as they came.

Meditate for longer periods - an hour, two hours - That way one can steadily change their physiology and step closer towards proper cultivation.
