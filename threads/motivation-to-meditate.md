# Motivation To Meditate

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **orihh** · *(OCR)* · `Question` · 1 point · 2 years ago

After coming across this subreddit, I initially found great pleasure in meditating. I meditated every night for a week or so and I was eager to continue. After this period, however, I began working for 4-6 hours after coming back from school. This left me with little time to do the things that I wanted: martial arts practice, writing, making music, feeding my intellect, relaxing, etc. Whenever I did manage to make time at the end of the day, I found myself to be too tired to focus properly on the meditational object, and grew frustrated of my state. Now I've released this burden of work and I do in fact have time and energy, but I'm now lacking in motivation to meditate. The willpower and energy required to sit and actively focus on the meditational object outweighs my desire to do other things... Is there any way to sustain motivation? I know that if I become inspired, this inspiration will only last a couple days before it wears out. I need a proper way to keep myself meditating day after day.

  The reason why people need sleep is to balance the exertion done in daily work. Therefore, physical exercise or mental work is certainly compensated only by resting oneself from physicality and thoughts.

  Sleep is unconscious meditation, and if you do meditation deeply, it is like conscious sleep without torpor.

  Before you sit, you should have a goal. What is the goal of meditating? If you wish to meditate for self-improvement, so be it. If you wish to meditate for self-transcendence, so be it. If you wish to meditate for the sake of all beings, so be it. Whatever it is, whether to bring joy to others, to be a better healer, to solve a world problem, keep the vow and do not change it. Keeping this steadfast, with a goal, you will then naturally have right intention.
