# Hallmarks of cultivation

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 8 points · 6 months ago

Accumulation of jing leads to physical bliss. Jing is the generative force, bodhicitta, water-element, hormones, etc. Typically 3 months of mental celibacy.

Accumulation of chi leads to internal light. Chi is the vitality force, wind element, soul, etc. Typically 10 months of embryonic development.

Accumulation of shen leads to no-thought mental realm. Shen is the spirit, light, tejas, etc. Typically 3 years of developing the yin and yang shen.

Shen turns to emptiness. Typically 9 years. This is Hinayana enlightenment.

Blood transforms after 2 years - turns white.

Bone marrow turns white after S years.

Bones transform after 7 years.

Typically at least 9 years for entire nirmanakaya transformation.

To preserve lucidity in dreaming for dream-yoga, chi rises to throat chakra or above. To fly, one's chi ascends to the head. There is a secret tantric technique that involves the pinky fingers - but it is better to rely on preserving mindfulness in daily life.

Emptiness is returned to the Tao. Typically merit-driven time. This is Mahayana enlightenment.

Even after returning emptiness to Tao, there is still the Consciousness Skandha to be purified, so that the Alaya consciousness is transmuted to the Tathagata's. This is then perfect enlightenment.

Do not be discouraged. Once the Path is stepped on, one can only ever proceed towards the goal. Just do not waste time.

      The entire universe is one big Self - called God, Atma, Allah, Tao, the ultimate 'something'. Because of how ineffable it is, any description fails - the only way is to personify it. Just like how electricity powers all kinds of appliances but cannot be seen directly, everything comes from this one big Self.

      The first goal of liberation is to destroy the perspective of the small self. When you finally become 'selfless’, this limited perspective is suddenly realized to have been erroneous and has never actually been there - it is a mere illusion. You have all along been the big Self without knowing it.

      Compassion, loving-kindness, equanimity, sympathetic joy - these are all qualities that radiate, or rather, originate from this source. There is absolutely no 'ego’ to work with. However, there must be a system to go from one end to the other - in other words, slowly destroying the sense of the small self.

      The idea of a 'death’ is wrong. It is simply the conversion of one type of manifestation into another. In the big Self that is the universe, it is uncreated and undestroyed.
