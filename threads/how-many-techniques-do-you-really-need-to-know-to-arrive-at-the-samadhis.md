  > If this is all true, then you only really need a method that will bring you to the first Jhana, right?

  That is correct to a degree. There are many roads to the same thing, as repeated hundreds of times throughout the many suttas of the Pali Canon. It is all about the different degrees of letting go. One must learn to see it as a natural process and not be too cognitive about it. Simply watching without interfering - Seeing the rise and falling of phenomena. Most importantly, it is about intent, intensity and how well one understands and follows the noble eightfold path.

  There are other methods, such as the body mindfulness technique shared on the subreddit, which eases the physical transformation and accelerates the process. You will find that there are various things transformed along the path - mainly the mental and the physical aspects. When you get to that experience, as many others have here, then you will naturally understand.

  Also, it is not about the technique, but the mental state. What exactly are you doing in a technique such as that? You are using the crutch of a technique to attain a certain state of mind. By doing it, you then are able to enter altered states of consciousnesses, such as jhana, and access various degrees of the void.
