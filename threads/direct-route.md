# Direct Route

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 8 points · 2016-04-02

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/4d0ey4/direct_route/>

You are not the contents of what you see.

What you experience, you are not it.

What you witness, you are not it.

Afterall, how can you see yourself?

Don't listen to these words with your thinking mind.

Listen with your heart and know them.

If you want to be free of suffering,

Then listen to the truth of Suffering.

The Suffering begins when you cling to what is not you.

All things you witness are not you

They are non-self.

When you wake up from a dream

Then you realize it was a dream.

When you are not awake,

You are like a man mumbling in his sleep.

You are spouting words

But you don't know that you are.

How to get out? How to be free?

Turn the light back in

And find out what it is that witnesses

Reside in that

Do not identify yourself as

Body, feelings, thoughts or mental qualities.

Simply be a floating leaf

dancing along the surface of a gushing river.

The river of life with its banks of

Pleasure and pain.

When you let go of wrongful identification

You let go of these things without self nature

Letting go releases suffering.

The habit knot unties by itself.

Simply reside in the Tao

The indescribable subtlety.

The thing that never dies and is called the Deathless.

The thing which all things rise from.

The thing that is not a thing.

Keep flowing, keep flowing, keep flowing.

Let go, let go, let go.

Whether it be the desire, form or formless realms.

Simply letting go is the direct route.

You can use all kinds of techniques

to help focus the mind,

Or remove energetic blockages.

But in the end,

The One is the One.

You are the One.

The root for everything else to arise is I.

To know everything, find this One.

Stop the habit of clinging to things.

Everything in nature flows.

Turn to the Spirit

Hear its silent whisper.

Letting go,

its current brings you into eternal peace.
