# The Crucial Importance of Keeping Promises

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 5 points · 1 year ago

If one vows to the Buddhas that they will preserve the precepts, then it will be in the best interest of the person to preserve it.

Whether you see them or not, believe them or not, this is an advice given from pure heart and concern. Do not offend any beings. Observe the five precepts well.

The enlightened ones are always in the protection of devas, dharma-protectors and gods.

If one practices a potent mantra, such as the Surangama Sutra, and does not realise the conditions of tapping into such potency, breaking promises, then the repercussions can be unthinkable.

The enlightened ones are compassionate and do not punish people - however, sometimes the deities that follow them can become enraged when they do not see purity in conduct and adherence to the conditions of entering that dharma-door. This can bring some trouble for you.

Once, there was once a practitioner who prayed for a windfall, promising to donate half of what he yielded to charity. When it really came, he had a turn of heart and did not. I do not want to go into details about what happened after, but it was ugly. There is really a thing called divine punishment. Therefore, be careful of what you promise - Be honest, sincere and genuine.

If you are virtuous, simply being pure in conduct, can indeed help you in many ways.

Always be respectful of other beings. It does not matter whether you think that the enlightened ones are above them or not - regardless, deities, for example, require a huge effort and virtuous conduct that many people are not capable of in this age.

If you have made a promise, please make sure you keep it.

      Don't think of it as a transgression. It was taught to be a transgression because that was the only way the illiterate people of the old could understand - show the ropes, then let them understand.

      Instead, think of it as not being genuine in your practice.

      If you were to say you had lust in your mind, how can you say that you are cultivating? If one further invokes higher beings to come and help them, but is yet insincere, it would not be unlikely that any being that has not cut their own fetters would become enraged, just as the disciples of the Buddha were enraged in certain situations.
