# PG meditation journal #2

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **philosophyguru** · *(OCR)* · `Journal` · 1 point · 2 years ago

This is my second attempt at keeping a meditation journal. I haven't updated my previous journal for several months, although I've continued my practice daily.

My current practice is 15-20 min of anapanasati meditation a day, with the "milestone goal” of attaining jhana.

I feel like my practice has stalled since I was last journaling, which is in part why I stopped updating the journal. I can get to the point where the breath seems to slow, but I can't push past that point. If there is a pleasant sensation, I can't switch my attention from the breath to that sensation—the next breath draws my attention back to the focus on

breathing. I have tried to let the breath become even more subtle, but when I do I feel a sense of panic and feel my attention race to gasp for another breath. I have tried deliberately embracing calm and thinking of withdrawing my attention from the breath, but neither technique has succeeded yet.
