                          1. Yama [moral codes] 2. Niyama [self-purification and study] 3. Asana [posture] 4. Pranayama [breath control] 5. Pratyahara [moving away from 5 senses] 6. Dharana [concentration] 7. Dhyana [meditation] 8. Samadhi [absorption]
