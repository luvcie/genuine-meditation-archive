# Aware, be awake

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 7 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3la3nf/aware_be_awake/>

I will share the dharma of awareness. I hope this will awaken some of you. This is a fast path of awareness.

The mind has agitation, so does the body. The mind has movements, so does the body. Sometimes when the mind moves, the body moves. However, the body does not move when there is no mind movement.

The buddha-nature is in all of you. Just like mud is composed of clear, bright water and sand, your mind is the bright, fast, nimble and clear buddha-nature intermingled with thoughts.

In an ordinary human, these thoughts are like a gushing river. The awareness is dragged away by the velocity of these thoughts. Fast thoughts, when unaware and unconscious of, bring up anger, delusion and pain.

Place both of your palms on your thighs.

Lift up your right hand, being aware of its movement, then stop, being aware of the stop.

Rotate your right hand, being aware of its movement, then stop, being aware of the stop.

Rotate it back, being aware of its movement, then stop, being aware of the stop.

Do the exact same thing for your left.

This is similar to Anapanasati (breath meditation). When energy is taken in, be aware. When the breath stops, be aware of the stop. When energy is given out, be aware. When the exhale stops, be aware of the stop.

When thoughts rise up in your mind, be aware. When they stop, be aware.

It is not dullness, it is not over-excitedness. Finding the right balance, be aware of movement and stillness.

Now as you walk, do the exact same thing. When the left foot moves, be aware. When it stops, be aware. When the right foot moves, be aware. When it stops, be aware.

It does not matter what you are doing, as long as you are aware of the movement and stillness.

If mind is sluggish and you easily fall asleep, fold your arms and walk. Be aware of your feet.

If mind is overexcited, sit down and focus on breathing. Be aware of your movements.

Now this is not straining, concentration or effort. It is simply awareness.

Every time something happens, and you are unconscious of it, it translates to your suffering. If you are conscious of something and it happens, you do not suffer.

Do it slowly. Gradually, incorporate more of what you are aware of.

Doing this continuously, without stop, from waking to sleeping - If one is able to sustain this continuously, eventually, going through the four jhanas will not be a problem at all. Insights will be reached, and as absorption is associated with insight, absorption will naturally deepen. Eventually, you will reach a degree of enlightenment.
