- **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 6 points · 2 months ago

  You are close, but not exactly.

  Merit can be re-translated as the "Cultivation of Virtue". What exactly is virtue or conduct? It is merely 'abstaining’. For example, abstaining from telling lies, slandering, divisive speech, Killing, lust, indulging in sensory pleasures, etc. In a way, the abandoning of unfruitful thoughts, speech and actions can lead to 'emptiness’, or the abandonment of the self-view.

  The true achievement of merit is this: When the Noble Eightfold Path flows naturally - when every thought, speech and action are virtuous and based off the ever-flowing wisdom of the Tathagata.

  When one conceives of a 'self' to carry out virtue, what merit is this? When one conceives of a 'self' to be emptied, what merit is this? When one conceives of benefits to carry out virtue, again, what merit is this?

  There are many 'expedient teachings' out there which do not necessarily reflect the true reality of things. The pointing out of Mind straight to the original nature cuts out all things, but cannot be jumped to without going through the relevant progressive states or realms.
