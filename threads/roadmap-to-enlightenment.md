    - **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 4 points · 1 year ago

      > I have consistently felt like I am not my body or my mental processes or even the overwhelming bliss or new light.

      Nature can continue even without the ego. This, you must understand. The world does not revolve around the "I", as many may think. You are the intelligence in me, in him, in her, in everyone - the intelligence in every sentient being, and we call this the original Buddha-nature.

      But this "I" has to go.

      There is no my body. Instead, "there is a body". There is no my mental processes. There is no my bliss. There is no my light.

      The problem with this I is that it is elusive. The more you subtract things away from it, the more it tries to find something else to attach to. It is an illusion, a magic-trick. It has never existed. Do you see it?

      In the sensed, there is only the sensed. In the cognized, only the cognized. Not I sensed, not I cognized. Don't take what I say for it. Internally experience it right now.

      When you say "I am not my body", you subtly mean "I am something else other than the body". That's not it. The more you divide the ego, the more it goes deeper and deeper, forming more and more fantastic delusions and explanations to attach to.

      It then tells you: I am God, I am Tao, I am Buddha, I am the light, I am etc.

      No!

      Any concept, any thought, any notion of an I, personality, being, ego - it is all a magic trick.

      There was never an I. From the very start, there was never one.

      All there is, is the original Tao. This Tao is unreachable, because it is not a thing to be attained. You cannot say "I am Tao" or "my Tao".

      You say you meditate!

      There is no 'you' meditating. While I say that you should go meditate, this is an external appearance thing. It is the finger pointing to the Moon. Don't look at the finger, look at the Moon!

      Don't take my word for it. Look. You already see it. Dive deeper. Look at it realistically and tell me what you see.
