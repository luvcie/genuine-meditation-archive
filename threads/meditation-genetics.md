Meditation & Genetics

Now what exactly does meditation do to the body? Firstly, I wish to emphasize that meditation is not mystical, it is entirely scientific, although modern science has not caught up to it, neither has it been adequately exposed to genuine, deep meditation. I am no scientist, as these are all understandings derived from an experiential view, and hopefully it will help one understand more about the process of cultivation.

For example, Maitreya Buddha who will come in the age of the Dharma-Ending Age, will be one based in science and medicine, and at this point, science will burst forth with a completely different paradigm. How does one know if Maitreya has come? When the Great Arahants from the age of Gautama the Buddha have joined his side. These Great Arahants are all hidden in mountains, in deep samadhi, engaging in Anapana to prolong their lives. More details as to the conditions of the Blessed One's arrival are all found inside the sutras.

Now what makes up a body? How does a body grow up to be what it is? And what is a body made of?

Nowadays, everyone knows that the body is based off a specific coded information in the form of DNA. Depending on what genes are inside the DNA, the body grows accordingly, while being influenced by environmental factors.

Science has shown for example, that certain individuals possess certain variants of genes such as the hDEC2 which allows one to sleep for as little as four hours and wake up rejuvenated, compared to the norm of 8 hours. This is the same case of someone who has entered deep samadhi and activated this variant of the gene.

How are these genes modified in the physical plane? They are in fact controlled by the Seventh Consciousness. In Yogacara, this is known as the Manas or Self-Grasping or Discriminatory mind. This consciousness is also responsible for siddhis.

Whenever a thought arises, firstly, the energy of the 7th consciousness is stimulated, which stimulates the sixth thinking sense. In layman terms, the 6th thinking sense is the judge which unifies all the experiences and forces in past lives (karma) to determine how your eye, ear, nose, tongue, and body consciousnesses will respond.

Now this process moving from the 7th to 6th consciousness is fully automatic. However, there is a space in between this that allows one to change the trajectory. This is where sila, virtues, and discipline come in - the production of karma that is neitherbright-nor-dark, bright, or dark.

If it is not changed, then one's past unwholesome karma will certainly enter the sixth sense from the energy of the 7th, without regulation, and force one to continuously fall into unwholesome acts.

All stimuli in the environment around oneself is continuously being memorised, recorded and logged, whether or not one is conscious of it. In skilled hypnotism, it has been shown that extraction of certain memories like a car plate's number from a crime scene, for example, can be done.

When these external stimuli suddenly turns towards a situation of danger, the seventh sense will immediately respond, its energy surging through the entire body, causing genetic mutations.

Now what then, is the best way for changing one's genetic makeup and expression? It is the mirror-like wisdom, the Buddha-nature.

The method, is to not identify with any 'self'. This is the whole goal of the spiritual process. When one does not identify with self, one is able to unite the physical body and spiritual body to bring about large-scale changes.

When one attains Bodhisattva-hood in the thirdbhumi level, where one has attained a spiritual body, one is able to freely control the DNA of the body. The spiritual body is in fact formless and able to transform into countless transformations. Although one's karma determines what DNA one receives while having a body in this current incarnation, spiritual cultivation is what will allow a conversion of this energy!

For example, if one simply sits there in silence, and feels a circular portal in the sky open up, with a brilliant pillar of light that shines down through the entirety of one's body, down into the great earth - one absorbs that universal light, discarding the dysfunctional chi, merging with the light completely and entering samadhi. Consequently, one starts to effect changes on the genes.

Therefore, to answer the question raised by /u/ PouringStreamofLight, yes, one can indeed change genetics depending on one's absorption state.

However, this ability is not accessed by a mind full of delusions, craving for sensory pleasure, and desire. It is instead, achieved by a total renunciation of self. It is too, further aided by the cultivation of the Noble Eightfold Path. Therefore meditate deeply, strive for the liberation fruit, and one's health will certainly improve.
