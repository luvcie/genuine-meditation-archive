# Wind-element in Anapanasati & Direct Entry into Cessation

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 12 points · 2017-02-11

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/5tga5a/windelement_in_anapanasati_direct_entry_into/>

This question was asked by /u/sherlockian_holmes , and it is quite an important question especially for beginners in anapanasati, therefore I made a post for this.

Before all this, one needs to understand that Anapanasati - what is it? It is ana, it is pana. It literally means entering breath, leaving breath. As to what this 'breath' actually means, it is not important right now, the practical use of it is what is important.

I have covered this partially in a previous thread here.

Let us revise what they all are:

1. Wind (Feng)
2. Panting (Chuan)
3. Qi
4. Xi

So what exactly are these? These are the transformations of the Wind element (Feng Da) into Xi, which is the point where there is no more inhalation or exhalation.

In the world, the Wind-element is the circulation of air.

So how are all these 4 interlinked with each other?

Well, let us describe what xi is like. It is like when you close all the windows and doors of a room. And this 'trapped' air with no movement is called the state of Xi. However, if just one tiny movement, such as the movement of a hand - it causes a slight movement and this is called Qi. If it is even bigger, that's Wind!

What is panting? Well, when you breathe like this without stopping, and you can even hear the sounds of your own breath like we do in normal life, that's panting.

Now what is the breath the Buddha is talking about? It is actually the Xi!

Now I want to tell you this. There are moments where your breath momentarily stops and your mind is free from thoughts. The only problem is that you do not recognize it!

The only reason why you are not in this state is because you have started to place your attention on all of these thoughts that have come into your mind. They may not just be thoughts, they can be sensations, discomfort in the body, even a 'thought' that thinks it is not dwelling, but is in fact dwelling!

Treat all of these things as guests.

Imagine your mind's activity as some kind of fan in the room.

Originally, everything is still! If you can cut your mind right now, you are already at Xi!

Everything else is an extra movement!

There was a Zen story:

Two monks were watching the flag move.

The first monk said: "It is the flag moving."

The second monk said: "No, it is the wind that moves."

The master came over and hit their heads, saying, "It is the heart that moves."

This is in fact a secret instruction of anapanasati.

Can you recognize it?

The flag is moving - this means that you see that the thoughts are moving, the sensations are moving etc.

No, the wind is moving - this means that now you see that the wind-element is connected to the thoughts. You think the wind movement moves the thoughts.

However none of them are correct. It is because the heart/mind has moved - that is exactly why everything else appears as it is. The flag moves, the wind moves - all of these appearances come from the heart-mind.

Everything starts from xi, it is the mind's disturbances that move it. Do you understand yet?

Many instructions I taught in the past use this exact principle.

I will teach you now the high-method, the fastest-method, the one that requires the highest wisdom for direct entry into breath-cessation.

Counting breaths? No, leave that aside. Trying to focus on the breath? No, leave that aside. All of these methods are expedient means, they help the beginner, but some of you are no longer beginners, so simply use this method instead.

Understand what is the knowing-nature. Right from the very start of the Anapanasati instructions, the Buddha tells you to 'know'. So what exactly is knowing?

Firstly, you might think that the knowing comes from the brain. That is a first-step. You are not wrong. Thoughts mostly appear and seem to be from the head. However, the knowing - where does it come from?

Now I want you to examine... you have sensations in your body that come and go. Discomforts, or any of those like - how do you know where these come from?

Now unite these two - how do you know when thought perceptions appear, and body feelings appear? From where do you see the common origin?

Stop right there.

The moment you think of anything else, the moment you add a label, the moment you try to chase any thought or word... You lost it.

That, my friend, is . That is the first step. As you can tell, this knowing has no location whatsoever. It might seem to be from the head, but thoughts are from the head. It might seem to be from the body, but feelings are of the body. So where exactly is it from? You don't need need to care. Just know, and rest in this still peacefulness where the knowing is chasing nothing.

It is as if you are a restaurant owner, and the guests are coming in. Oh, the thought-guest is coming in, oh, the breath-guest is coming in, oh, the body-guest, so on and on. But now I want you to just be the owner. Just be the owner. This is the fastest path I have taught to many here, but this is another post to reiterate the point.

Now as you be the owner, realize that you are still 'knowing' this. So just let go of the content and just know. Remember, this knowing is at step 0, not even step 1. The moment you take one step, that is no longer knowing. Pure, naked knowing.

Now that you got this knowing down, let us proceed to the next step.

Step two. We will regulate the breath and remove all the impurities within our bodies using the 6 word-breath. I have explained this post before, but it appears that it is not that popular and not given the credit that it should have among the other techniques.

1. Using the 'knowing nature', be aware of the breath and let it go on its own naturally without controlling it. Mouth is closed with tongue on roof as normal.

2. Find the correct mouth-shape in the chart inside the link above. When you are aware that the body is taking an exhalation, use the designated mouth-shape. Now this shape is paired to a body-part. I want you to visualize dark, black chi in this area, and then blow it out from this area, imagining it coming out together with the breath into the emptiness of the universe. No sound should be made on the exhalation, so it should be a very silent exhalation.

3. When all the breath is exhaled, then do not force anything. Wait for the body to respond and start taking in the breath again naturally.

You can do them repeatedly if you feel uneasiness in any of the areas. After doing all 6 mouth-shapes, you can proceed on to the next step.

Third step. After all of this is done, then you may realize that the body has become extremely calm, or relatively at ease as compared to before. Now do not bother about controlling anything about the breath and go back to pure knowing-nature without controlling the breath.

If you do this step right, you will see that the knowing-nature stands alone, and the breath fades in the background into Xi.

You cannot expect to go into Xi, because this expectation indicates that the mind is moving. If you start thinking of the breath, mind is moving. If you start thinking of this or that, or whether you are doing this right or not, mind is moving. As long as mind moves, you are outside Xi.

No movement. Not even the thought of 'no movement'. Just pure, knowing, that is not the brain, nor is there an intention!

Now if at this point, your mind becomes very discursive and unwilling to settle, then there are a few causes:

1. Your mind is not tamed throughout the day through virtuous conduct. In other words, you have broken precepts.

2. Your energies are untamed and your body is slightly/severely sick.

3. Your knowing-nature has departed away from Xi.

How to tame it? All you have to do is just become aware and stay with the inhalation and exhalation for a while. Just match the knowing-nature together with the Xi, then when mind no longer has thoughts, go back to pure 'knowing'.

Step four. If you did the above right, you will spontaneously, without any expectation, without any thought-provocation, enter state of Xi. This can be temporary for a few seconds, like between an exhalation and inhalation. This can be mid-way of a inhalation. This can be at any time. At this time, you might not even notice, because your knowing-nature has unified together with no movement of mind in Xi.

At this time, we truly say that the mind and breath are one.

Step five. Now from here onwards, we can talk about the very basic cultivation within the Form skandha. Your body can react differently, depending on the karmic obstructions that you have. If you have a sickness, then the blockages will clear out.

Here is the trick. The trick is let go of it completely, as if it were dispersing outwards into millions of butterflies into the emptiness, or as if it was smoke, or as if there were dirty insects flushing out from the soles of the feet or wherever else the blockages are. It is NOT visualization, but letting go.

You might feel heavy pain or heat or coldness or dampness or any kind of sensation. Let it go.

You might feel blissfulness arise and compound intensely. Do not be attached to it, because this is all just part of the process.

This Xi, you only know it as this tiny portion. The real accomplishment is when even the body-pores stop breathing! That is what we call "Zhi Xi", or the stilling of Xi.

So keep going on. This might be out of the range of many of the beginner cultivators. If you got it, congratulations to you. If you have not, then work on clarifying this by asking questions or seeing this experientially.

Note: I noticed that many scholars tend to skip things in the suttas, such as the entire paragraph before the 16 steps of anapanasati sutta:

"There is the case where a monk, having gone to the wilderness, to the shade of a tree, or to an empty building, sits down folding his legs crosswise, holding his body erect, and setting mindfulness to the fore. Always mindful of ana; mindful of pana.

The bolded word above is the key - it is bringing the knowingness to the front. In the past, these people or the disciples of the Buddha did not have as bad karmic obstructions as modern people do, therefore, they easily slipped into the state of Xi/what they call breath.
