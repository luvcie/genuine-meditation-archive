# Anjali Mudra

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 3 points · 2015-11-13

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3sn4w9/anjali_mudra/>

Place your palms together, fingerpads of either hand touching each other. Also, relax the shoulders and broaden the chest. Do not press your hands forcefully - just enough force to hold a sheet of paper is perfect. This is the anjali mudra, also how monks greet each other in monasteries.

What is the reason for this gesture? *Anjali* is sanskrit for a form of offering, of surrender, of unconditional love.

The fingers contain channels that link to various organs in your system. By spreading your fingers, you automatically open these channels. By closing your fingers, you close them. The palm also consists of a point where energy can be projected from. When doing the Anjali mudra, you unite the left and right sides of the nervous systems, and connect the heart energies in a single circuit.

Any scattering of energy is immediately stopped and energy moves down to behind the lower navel automatically. If you focus on your heart, it can be easier to develop joy and blissfulness in the chest area.

It is also possible meditate with your hands at the chest level, in the Anjali mudra. By doing this for at least 10 minutes, you will see a difference in your heart energy.

The reason why it is used as greeting is because it gathers the heart energy and is a compassionate way of honouring the other person. Therefore, everyday, at least to something or someone, do the Anjali mudra and with deep respect or reverence, practice the emptiness and let the love energy light up your heart.
