# The Essence of the Teaching

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 10 points · 1 year ago

The ultimate essence of the teaching is:

To recognize the impermanence in all phenomena.

Recognizing this impermanence, the insight that there is no self nature contained within rises.

This recognition is experiential, and all afflictions are burnt, with all the higher fetters severed.

When all fetters are severed, ignorance is lifted and one has found the end of suffering.

When one does Anapanasati, one becomes increasingly aware of impermanence. When the Buddha got awakened, what did he awaken to? It was the impermanence of all conditioned things.

The Buddha's last words:

> “Behold, O monks, this is my last advice to you. All conditioned things in the world are changeable. They are not lasting. Strive hard to gain your own salvation."

When a person awakens from a dream, nothing is attained, isn't it? What powers does one get from waking from a dream? What achievements are there?

The Buddha's dharma is everywhere, and it is in front of you at every moment.
