# How do you know if you are meditating correctly? (Explanation of chi movement)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 5 points · 2015-06-16

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3a0hkx/how_do_you_know_if_you_are_meditating_correctly/>

There is certainly a right way to meditate, or the Buddha would not have included "right meditation" as part of the Noble Eightfold Path.

The first sign is warmth and blissfulness. Are you feeling joyful when you are meditating? That is the first sign.

The second sign is a mind that clings to fewer things. Does your mind let go of thoughts more easily? Are you able to let go of things? Or are you still constantly distracted by thoughts that go by?

The third sign is the arising of "yang chi". Has your yang chi risen? What do I mean by this? This post will help to explain what will happen physically:

We have a back channel known as the Du Meridian. See this picture to see an approximate pathway. It rises from the "bottom of the sea" (or the bottom of the trunk/perineum) and goes along the curvature of the back to the crown of the head. It then turns around and moves to the forehead and finally to the nose.

Realize that if you are still insensitive, you will not be able to feel movement of actual chi. You might however, experience aches/numbness or problems manifesting at these areas. For example, if chi is pushing through your back of the head, you are going to have tons of visual hallucinations, because that is where the occipital cortex (in charge of vision in the brain) is.

This is the pathway that people often talk about kundalini rising. Please do note, that FORCING kundalini up is only going to cause problems. The analogy is as follows: Which is easier to walk through... A room full of cluttered objects or an empty room? Similarly, it is only when all the impurities (yin chi) have been cleansed that the yang chi has "lesser obstruction" to rise. Removing this yin chi takes a lot of time and patience. Simply meditating will do it. Do not try artificial breath control/pranayama (other than the one shown in this subreddit) if you do not have a master helping you.

The final result for opening the Du meridian is a "straightening of posture" and entrance into a deep absorption. You will feel a liquid-like energy pour over the front of the body, coating the whole body. Until you reach this stage, your physical cultivation has not reached the first step as of yet.

After this step, there will be the opening of the front channel, and subsequently the central channel, which also leads to the opening of left/right channels described in yoga.

If you have not reached this step, combining anapanasati with daily pranayama and perhaps "emptiness meditation" (sati with reference to thought objects) will bring you to this stage. Patience and diligence is key.
