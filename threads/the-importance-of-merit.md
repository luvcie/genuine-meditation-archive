# The importance of merit

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 2 points · 1 year ago

Good deeds and virtuosity are like the fragrances of heaven.

Doing good is an expression of the Divine nature. It is its natural expression. Doing good for the sake of attaining something is not as good as doing good simply because.

However, to receive sacred teachings, one must be meritorious.

Many ask: Why is this or that not taught? Well, simply because the merit to understand or even listen to it is not there yet.

From now on, strive to be of service. Teach what you know of the Dharma, but not what you do not. Give what help you can to others, but not what you cannot.

Once a King built many Buddhist monasteries and asked Bodhidharma what his merit was. Bodhidharma replied, "No merit."

What did this mean? Meditate on it.
