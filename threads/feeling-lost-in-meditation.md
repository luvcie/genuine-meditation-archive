  > I aim for at least 10 minutes of meditation a day, and I have gone over the Beginner's Guide to Meditation and various other threads on this subreddit.

  10 minutes is unfortunately, hardly enough for any fruit. Mindfulness throughout the day, as well as a full meditation that goes deep into the jhanas is essential.

  You should start out with 10 minutes, then go on to 20 minutes, and then 30 minutes and so on, up to at least an hour. The reason for this is because one cannot reach the various jhana states if you do not remain in meditation for sufficient time. It takes time for the body to calm down.

  > I'm creating anxiety during meditation

  Anxiety during meditation, which is not caused by deep meditation, is an unwholesome state of mind. If you felt anxious, it means that you failed to investigate your mind state. An over-excited mind state can be treated with letting go. Accept that you are anxious, then investigate the cause of this anxiety. Let go of your anxiety and simply focus on your meditation object

  > My eyes constantly feel dry, tired, and stressed to the point where it feels they will burst.

  Is it possible that you feel fatigued? Perhaps dehydrated after the endurance run? If the body is not able to sit comfortably, it can often be the result of physical problems with the body. The body's condition is linked to the mind, and it can certainly cloud it.

  > These days it's not new for me to lose my motivation and just sleep in the evenings, be materialistic, or even be rude to someone because of how irritated I feel.

  Don t be too hard on yourself. It is so easy to set ones goals too high. Imo, the mind has to be fostered like a child. Any effort you can do is 1000x better than not doing any. Treat 5 minutes of mindfulness like a victory. As little as just letting go of a single though, it all adds up in the end. If you can stay with this attitude, your practice will increase in intensity with time. You may have to give yourself a pep-talk at times, but I found that the intensity kinda undolds on its own. You are on the road to the mountain, keep your eyes at the road in front of you, stop gazing to the mountaintop

  > It just feels like I'm creating anxiety during meditation or I'm trying too hard but I can't seem to let go and relax.

  Watch your thoughs coming and going. Notice what your reaction is. Ask yourself: do I have to think this? When you see this again and again, over time your mind will be more quiet and more easily let go of random thoughts. This is part of what the path is about. Anxiety is an emotion in the body, and its created by thought. It is amplified by thought. When its there you should be careful not to dwell in your thought, but ground yourself in your object of meditation

  > I started endurance running, but I do not want to stop and I feel it is helping my body become more healthier though it seems like my mind is the complete opposite.

  If you experience restlessness or anxiety after running, again maybe you're too hard on yourself. Treat exercising as a way of relaxing as well. Don't push it. Try and enjoy instead.
