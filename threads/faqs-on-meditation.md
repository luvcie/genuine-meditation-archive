FAQs on Meditation

Q: Can I meditate and cultivate properly even if I do not have a teacher? Will I go into demonic states or enter errant paths?

A: Yes, you absolutely can. Modern people like to talk about entering demonic states, but there is actually no such thing. The only problem is knowing *how* to sit. Also, it is the problem of how your subconscious has mysterious, supernatural thoughts. As such, the spirit-mind and thoughts are not pure, and one creates for oneself their own fantasy - this is then what we call entering demonic states. In the past, there were many cultivators, none of them have ever entered demonic states. Why? Their reason for meditating is to cultivate their mind, how to purify their minds - that is why there has never been an incidence of entering these errant paths in the past

...

Q: Are there special requirements before meditating? Do I need to sit in a temple, or burn incense, or wear robes?

A: Meditation can be done anywhere, in any clothing, in any preparation, in any posture, and in any situation.

...

Q: What kind of posture do I need to meditate? What if I am missing a leg with a handicap? Is there any problem?

A: The importance of meditation is in the quiescence of the mind, there is not an inkling of concern about posture. When the mind is quiet, that is meditation.

...

Q: Should I meditate when I am drowsy and tired?

A: When one meditates while tired, this is resting. If one wishes for attainment, then they should sleep and recover from fatigue before meditating.

...

Q: Do I need to be vegetarian?

A: No, there is no requirement to meditate.

...

Q: What time is best to meditate?

A: Meditation timing is done when one uses Taoist cultivation methods. However, meditation by itself does not have any time limitation.

...

Q: Can I meditate when sick? Constipated? Having haemorrhoids? Or having any kind of illness? When pregnant? When having mental problems?

A: Yes. In fact, the sickness will recover more quickly. However, if the pregnant woman has never done lotus-position before, then this position is not recommended. For mental illnesses, one will require a person to assist - if one does not teach meditation correctly, more delusions will arise instead of helping the person.

...

Q: Can I meditate in a noisy environment?

A: You may not sit down in meditation, because you will seem weird and outof-place. Instead, meditation can be done by quietening the mind in any place. Sounds do not disturb you, your mind moves instead.

...

Q: Can i meditate straight after food?

A: For beginners, after eating, the intestines will work and it is not suitable for meditation. For attained people, meditating will help the food be immediately digested. However, beginners should wait at least 30 minutes before sitting. The best time for beginners is to meditate when one is not too full or too hungry.

...

Q: Can I meditate after sex? After alcohol?

A: For beginners, it is not recommended. Instead, one should rest their body, and meditate when their mind is stable.

...

Q: Why do we need to meditate?

A: This is a question you have to ask yourself, and get the answer to.

...

Q: How long should I meditate?

A: Beginners start from 5 minutes, and slowly add up to 30 minutes. Advanced meditators should increase from 30 minutes to 50 minutes. For serious practitioners, they should increase 50 minutes to 3 hours.

...

Q: How do I increase the number of hours of meditation?

A: For people wanting to achieve 50 minutes, firstly, sit for at least 30 minutes before coming out of samadhi. Then adjust your body and mind, without getting up, enter back into meditation. Sit until you do not want to sit, then come out of samadhi. Slowly, you will cultivate until you can sit continuously for 50 minutes. For advanced cultivators, firstly sit for 50 minutes, come out of samadhi, adjust without getting up, then go back into meditation again. This will slowly improve your time.

...

Q: How do I know if my posture is correct?

A: This will depend on your own feelings. If you are insensitive to your body, then this is a problem. As you meditate more, you will become more sensitive to your body and you will be able to adjust your posture accordingly.

...

Q: Why do I feel trembles, shivers, coldness, heat, sweat, etc? Pains, aches?

A: These are manifestations of illnesses in your body, or imbalances. There have always been problems inside your body, but in meditation, you become sensitive and clear to its presence. If a disease is trivial, the body will slowly recover. If the disease is heavy, then one should see a doctor and treat it with medicine. However, one should be clear that it is not meditation that creates the disease, but reveals it before it gets worse.

...

Q: What if I can't sleep at night?

A: If one meditates well, then it originally can replace sleep. However, don't treat this with importance. Just like eating three meals a day, sleeping at night is a habit for most people - there is no definite need that requires you to need it. One should know that sloth or torpor is an obstacle in meditation, so sleeping itself is a hindrance to the clear mind. If one can cultivate to the point of right depth, it is possible to go for two days without sleeping.

...

Q: Sometimes I have movements while meditating?

A: There are two parts: Mental perception and feelings. When obstacles that block chi and blood are suddenly broken through, they can result in these movements. However, whether to develop or stop it, this requires wisdom, so it depends. If one does not have a stable mind, then it is best to stop, because one might start to think they are a spirit medium. On top of that, if one has supernatural thinking, then it can become a sickness.

...

Q: Can one have sex, or abstain?

A: This is a complicated question. On the basic level, there is a need to abstain in order to not leak jing. If one cultivates in order to have strong sexual organs in order to satisfy the female, then this is courting early death and is not a good thing at all. For normal people, moderation is key - indulging will bring harm.

Q: Erections or sexual drive after/while meditating?

A: Many people will give in to their sexual drives when this happens. The best way is to empty your thoughts. On a long-term, the best way is to cut down on food intake.

...

Q: Can a female meditate during her period?

A: Beginners should rest. However, for those who can see the emptiness in one's body, then this is no problem.

...

Q: Why can I smell things during meditation?

A: A majority of the time, it is the transformation of the body. As a result of the cleansing of the digestive system, it can produce a form of pleasant smell. Originally, there is always fragrance - it is just that most people are unhealthy and imbalanced, so it has made them smelly. In rarer cases, it might be the arrival of a master. Do not confuse them, however, as most of the time, it has to do with bodily changes.

Q: Diarrhea after meditating sometimes?

A: If one meditates well, diarrhea can be a good thing, because it shows that the chi channels have opened and this is cleaning up the bowels. However, one must be sure that this is not from a disease, do not confuse them.

    - **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 2 points · 1 year ago

      Follow the teachings, and not the person. Follow the sutras before you follow commentaries. Do not take the teachings as faith until you have investigated it for yourself and found it to be for the benefit of one and all.

      When looking at a teacher, ask: Does one encourage personal investigation? Does one talk about how the right noble-eightfold path, in its various ways, lead to liberation?

      If a teacher dislikes sacred mantras, his teaching is questionable. If a teacher teaches to chase sensory impressions as law, his teaching is questionable. If a teacher teaches supernatural abilities as the goal, rather than for liberation, his teaching is questionable. If a teacher discourages investigation and enforces doctrine, this teacher is questionable.

      Lastly, one should seek whether this teacher is enlightened or not. A teacher who is not enlightened can teach you mundane wisdom, but this teacher cannot point you in the right direction.

      Can this teacher bring out teachings from the insight-stream, and be able to answer questions with deep wisdom?

      Can this teacher understand any Buddhist scripture, even though it is the first time looking at it?

      Can this teacher be unobstructed by any situation, but yet be able to have perfect conduct?

      Can this teacher be unobstructed by evil realms?

      Does this teacher have the 6 extraordinary attainments, the fruit of right practice?

              As stated in the Samannaphala Sutta, the discourse on the fruits of leading a contemplative life guided by the Noble Eightfold Path, the Buddha listed, along with some elaboration:

              1. mano-maya: mind-generated body or the Yang-shen. Along with this body comes various abilities, such as travelling to realms as far as the Brahma heavens, to the Sun and Moon, flying through mountains and walls, etc. Also known in Chan as "unimpeded body actions".

              2. dibba-sota: clairaudience, divine-hearing, hearing sounds near and far, as far as the universe.

              3. ceto-pariya-nana: mental telepathy, penetrating knowledge of the awareness of other beings.

              4. paticca-samuppada: Knowledge of previous lifetimes, and insight into interdependent origination

              5. dibba-cakkhu: clairvoyance, divine-sight, able to see beings passing away and re-appearing.

              6. Complete extinction of afflictions, with nanadassana (purification of knowledge and vision).

              Most important of all six powers is the very last - as this ensures that one frees oneself from the cycle of samsara.
