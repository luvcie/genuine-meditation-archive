# Energy and Enlightenment

In this post, I will clarify once and for all, the confusion between energy and enlightenment.

Firstly, Lao Tze declared in the Tao Te Ching:

The whole universe and consciousness comes from this original substance, and it is what we call the "original nature”. Other names of this include God, Wuji, Shiva, Dharmakaya, Tac, Buddha-nature, and Nibbana.

This "substance" is undifferentiated, indivisible, infinite, omnipotent, omnipresent, omniscient, unmoving, unborn, deathless, eternal and actionless. It is as if it was the "stem cell” of which all possibilities can arise from, except that it itself is not changed in any way.

Instead, from this "Oneness"” comes Two (yin and yang). And turning into Three (with consciousness), the interplay of yin and yang produces the countless manifestations and emanations that appear in the world.

The Buddha proclaimed the first link in interdependent origination to be Ignorance. What is this ignorance? It is simply because there is no way to know or explain how manifestation arises from this unchanging, primordial nature.

For example, all kinds of movies can play on a cinema screen, but the screen itself does not change. Another example is the sky does not change its form, but the clouds and contents of the sky can. In the same way, this primordial nature does not change a bit while emanations appear from it. There is no beginning to it, and there is no end to it.

How do we understand this primordial nature?

Let us look at nature. For example, if you look at an ice-cube, it is a solid state. For it to become subtler, it will melt into water, which is a liquid state. For it to become yet even subtler, it becomes gas, which is a gaseous state. At this time, it already becomes invisible. If you were to go yet even subtler, you can go into plasma, and eventually energy itself. So this is the realm of fire, earth, wind and water.

Now what can be subtler? The space, akasa or ether in which allows energy to manifest its effects is far subtler.

Yet another step up, you will realise that it is consciousness. Everything, including akasa/space, can arise within consciousness itself. While you may observe space, the root of consciousness itself cannot be observed. Consciousness

simply cannot find itself.

However, if one attains enlightenment, they have developed a spiritual body made of the foundational substance of consciousness itself. If one has not attained this body, it would be impossible to experience this true essence that permeates the entire world, including us.

Consciousness does not move when it is resting. Therefore, it is not about trying to experience "nothingness”, but rather training non-attachment, so that thoughts that come eventually go away and the mind rests in its natural clarity.

Therefore, both consciousness and matter all originate from the same fundamental substance, also known as Hou Tian Qi. The Tibetans call this the Clear Light.

When a person adopts the Hou Tian Qi as their manomaya (mind-made body), their mind is at a much higher energy-level. Therefore, they can sense all kinds of frequencies from lower realms, such as mantras chanted or thoughts of other beings.

"Nothingness" state

Now at high levels, one is able to reach the state where consciousness is alone without subject or object. However, being "empty without thought"” at this state is still not the experience of the primordial nature. There are far higher, subtler levels. Remember, this is not about suppressing thoughts, but allowing thoughts to be born, and just not attaching to them.

Chi moves at a very high frequency. When one looks at a fan, it might seem to be a circle. However, when one slows down the fan, one will notice the fan blades. In the same way, chi is moving so fast that it can seem as if it is motionless. In fact, chi is always moving in different ways, transforming continuously.

In order to be enlightened, one must cultivate until you develop a manomaya (mind-made body) composed of this Hou Tian Qi.

Now what is beyond Hou Tian Qi?

This is what we call Xian Tian Qi, which can be subdivided into Yin and Yang.

Yin refers to the unmoving state of chi that is unmanifested, but has the potential. In cultivation, this is reaching a state where thoughts are not born (samadhi of no-thought/ignorance). This is like zero-point vacuum energy. However, this is in fact simply only a temporary stage so that cultivators can cultivate yet another higher body.

Yang is the moving state of chi that is manifesting.

Temporary Experience

When thoughts (frequencies in consciousness) cease and disappear completely, the thoughts that produce the "I" thought also disappears, and the knower itself disappears.

However, this itself is only a temporary experience. In this state, chi is experiencing iself - a state of pure being. Sages never cultivate this as a permanent state because this is not enlightenment, and there is no knowledge of the world.

Yet, there is still the True self-nature that is beyond this. The true self-nature is the ultimate root of the origin of consciousness and everything else.

No being is an independent self. Most people take mental processes as themselves, but they are simply just a "lump” of higher, subtler energy manipulated in various appearances.

All of these transformations produced a whole range of life-forms, from unconscious sentience like plants, to animals like humans, etc.

There are really no sentient beings. Beings are just biological machines that evolved to "trap” information as memories and form thoughts to interact with the environment.

What separates beings from other types of things in existence? Consciousness - the ability to know things - thoughts.

In actual fact, whenever a being says "I", this is not referring to the mental process, but rather, pointing to the common origin that also composes the materiality of the universe.

You want to cultivate a state where the mind has full, clear, direct knowing when thoughts arise, and is empty when there are no thoughts.

Beyond Xian Tian Qi

This is possibly the limit to which anyone will teach. However, in total, there are about 5 subtler substances that make up 5 different types of bodies. It is through these bodies that masters exhibit different abilities. However, you simply do not have the merit to receive the teachings either, unless you have attained to at least the Xian Tian Qi manomaya.

Therefore, you can only imagine how profound the Buddha's dharma was - and that he was fully awakened.

How do you reach such a high level?

The key is regularity, diligence, patience and right practice.

Emptiness meditation, or sati, watching as things rise and fall away on their own will naturally allow you to reach a quiet mental realm.

The second way is to either use a mantra, which also asks for assistance from higher realms, and also visualizations. Both "bind" the mind so that the passing thoughts rise and fade away.

Virtue, wholesome deeds, meritable behaviour - all of these help the movement of chi in people's bodies through the heart especially, and other regions.
