                    - **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 1 point · 2 years ago

                      If you were to dig into the meaning of meditation, there is really no "meditation”. We simply go into a meditative state, regardless of what anyone does. What one calls "meditation” is really the process or a certain method to move a person from an unwholesome state into a wholesome state of mind, hence opening the gateway to the absorptions.

                      It is much easier for a person to sit down, lower one's metabolism and respiratory rate, so that the thoughts calm down as well, and hence enter a meditative state. It is however, very possible that a person, in jhana absorption, walks around doing tasks in perfect equanimity, bliss and joy.
