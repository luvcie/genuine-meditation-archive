# Verbs?

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **blissfulencounters** · *(OCR)* · `Discussion` · 6 points · 1 year ago

I just realized the meaning of why the Buddha awoke to the realization that everything is impermanent...

We're verbs... not nouns...

We're never static at a spot, we're always, always changing, moving, dynamic, flowing...

When my body dies, it goes to another place, when my body takes birth, it comes from another place...

We're flowing, flowing, flowing!

When we call ourselves a "self", that's where we think of ourselves as a noun. Only a noun can "perform an act" on something else... and that's the limitation of our English language... There are no nouns, just temporarily "flowing togetherness" which we call an object and put a name to...

I call that animal a puppy... but it's bone, muscle... if i break it even more, it becomes atoms... if i break it even more it's just pure energy... And it's not like the body stays the same... it's flowing, flowing, flowing! And it's a puppy, then it's a new puppy, and a new puppy, and a new puppy... then suddenly it's a dog... and then soon after it becomes a

corpse...

But fundamentally we don't change at all because we're energy at the most fundamental level...

Even "energy"” is a noun and doesn't describe it... You never find energy static anywhere anyway... it's always “moving"” in visible or invisible ways...

Like if we swing a ball all the way up with our hands, it flies up, then pauses a while, then comes back down...

And that's like our breath!

Birth and death... are movements of energy...

In and out breath are movements of energy...

But even the word 'energy’ cannot pin down what this actually is, this complex, moving, enabling, flowingness...

So how can we have a self....

In my own awareness now I see a body... it's because of my body that I believe that I am a noun... but it's the body that gets born and dies...

I simply cannot exist as a noun, do you see??

If I look in our body, I see body temperatures moving up and down constantly... the birds migrating every single moment... the ocean tides moving up and down... the cells contracting and expanding... the lungs huffing and puffing... the muscles tightening and loosening.. the bones compressing and releasing... the clouds gathering and raining... the world spinning and spinning... the universe moving... the sounds... the visions... the smells... the touches.. the tastes.. our thoughts... our perceptions... our egoic sense...

Any noun is not a noun!

We can't be a single thing when we're constantly flowing... you're part of the universe, so am I... (temporarily!)... so is every other being... and OBVIOUSLY we need to "love thy neighbor!" How can we not love ourselves?

boom :)

just wanted to share :)
