There is a secret in sounds that many teachers refuse to teach when a student is not ready. The power of a sound, properly uttered, can destroy things in a snap. The power of a name, when properly uttered, can invoke the energy of a being. The start of the universe was said to be from the utterance of a word.

"Ah" evokes the heart chakra, "Uu" evokes the throat chakra, "Mm" evokes the third eye chakra. Reciting the Cundi helps to open the heart chakra. There are also many types of yoga used to open the central channel. Avalokitesvara's stream entry was from the method of sound - It is also one of the most successful entry methods.

In this post, I wanted to introduce to you another mantra that evokes the energy of the bodhisattva Green Tara.

      Yes, you can choose one or two mantras, if you wish. It does depend on what you wish to achieve in cultivation. For example, if you want to open up the heart chakra, the Cundi mantra can help a lot. If you want just a few, you can focus on just the Cundi mantra.

      When doing most mantras, seek the reverberation of the mantra. This is hard to describe. In simplistic terms, I describe it as a particular wobble. For example, the table has a different wobble from the chair. Different individuals also have different wobbles. That's why names are quite powerful.

      Here is a story that might help:

      > ‘A monk visited a hermit, who lived alone on an island doing retreat. The hermit had given himself three years to complete chanting ten million of the powerful six-syllable mantra of the Compassionate Buddha. The hermit had been told that attaining this level of practice would awaken his yogic powers. The mantra was "OM MANI PADME HUM". The monk listened as the hermit did his mantra and, with the best intention in the world, leaned over to him and whispered: “I think you have got the pronunciation wrong. This mantra should be chanted this way...” and he proceeded to demonstrate. The hermit listened attentively and then watched as the monk walked back to his boat to leave the island. Ten minutes later when the boat was halfway across the river the monk heard his name being called, and looking around, he spied the hermit and heard him call: “Listen to this, have I got it right now?” and the hermit proceeded to chant the same mantra but with the monk’s intonation. Astounded, the monk turned around and saw the hermit walking on the water next to his boat. In that instant he realized that the hermit’s faith and sincerity had given his mantra recitation far more power than he had realized.

      You should pay attention to this quality that is beyond just the pronunciation. The energy/intent/faith behind the words are also important. I regret not being able to put this in a more clear manner, but I hope this helps.
