- **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 2 points · 2015-09-23

  **Torpor**/**Dullness**: Sleepiness, lethargy, dullness of the mental state, leading to unconsciousness, losing energy, losing self-awareness.

  **Unwholesome mental state**: Poor mental state that is not bright - either anxious/fearful/over-excited or torpor. Usually because the mind is bothered by the five hindrances (sensory desire, ill-will, sloth/torpor, anxiety/fear/restlessness/worry & doubt). This sutta ([MN 7](http://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.007.nypo.html)) may be able to help. These include all kinds of 'bad states', including the "Dark Night of the Soul"/Makyo's Cave/etc.

  **Wholesome mental state**: Good, bright mental state - the Middle Way. This is when the mind has four brahmavihara factors:

  1. *metta*

    - loving-kindness

  2. *karuna*

    - compassion

  3. *mudita*

    - sympathetic joy

  4. *upekkha*

    - equanimity

  Developing these, one moves towards the seven factors of enlightenment:

  1. *dhammavicaya*

    - Investigation of the Path

  2. *viriya*

    - Kundalini

  3. *passadhi*

    - Tranquility

  4. *sati*

    - Mindfulness practice

  5. *upekkha*

    - Equanimity

  6. *piiti*

    - Blissfulness

  7. *samadhi*

    - Jhana

  **Rapture**: Normally erroneously translated from *piiti*, which means bliss. The word *rapture*, however, comes from Christianity and hence should be used in the same manner, which means out-of-body experience in Buddhism. Unfortunately, many commentaries have bad translations and use this term recklessly.

  I hope this made things clearer.
