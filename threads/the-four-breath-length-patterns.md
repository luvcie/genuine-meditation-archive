# The Four Breath Length Patterns

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 5 points · 2 months ago

The four types of breath lengths:

1. "Long within the long". Both the exhalation and inhalation are prolonged.

2. "Short within the long". After the long exhalation goes out, one combines with an empty realm, and one does not return for quite a while. When one returns, they might find that the inhalation becomes very short and it is already sufficient.

3. "Long within the short". The body's insides continue to intake a long inhalation, continuing the inhalation until the energy reaches the soles of the feet.

4. "Short within the short". Both inhalation and exhalation are short.

This is all part of knowing the breath as long or short. I invite you to look at the Dharmatrata Dhyana Sutra.

Remember, this is not the first step but the second. The first step is knowing the breath going in or out at every given time, regardless of what you are doing.

After this step, it is knowing the breath through the entire body, where the entire body is breathing - this is when the chi channels are all opened and the chi is rejuvenated.
