# Is there a soul?

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Discussion` · 5 points · 2 years ago

If there exists a material soul, a mentally-generated material soul, a mentally-generated immaterial soul, it must be the case where consciousness itself and the soul are two separate things. But how may it be so?

Is the world finite? Is the world infinite? Is the soul the body? Are they different? These are all questions to which there is only an answer when an opinion exists. Why so?

It is because when a person has knowledge of Dharma, these questions are misguided and do not have a benefit to understanding the truth. There is a saying that goes: To find the right answers, one must ask the right questions. To extrapolate, it is also the case where when the wrong questions are asked, there can be no answer which can be given to the state of mind in which the question is asked. The only answer can only be that of guidance or silence.

What matters instead, what brings you directly to the truth for you to know for yourself, is that of the Eightfold Path: By having right conduct, right livelihood, right calming of mental and physical agitations, right knowledge, right insights, right attainments of the fruit of the Path and Nibbana.

One who claims that the questions asked above were not answered is simply blind to the Dharma and still within the realm of illusion. The questions were in fact, answered in the best manner possible. Only the ones with right wisdom are able to discern the true meaning behind those words - In fact, the answers were given directly - it is only that of people without 'eyes’ to see.

There are three realms in this world:

(1) The Material. This realm has four elements. The Material body is nourished by food and movement of the four elements.

(2) The Immaterial. This realm has no physical coarse form and is made of mind. All the sense fields are complete, organs are perfect.

(3) The Formless. This realm has no form completely, whether coarse or subtle. This realm is composed entirely of consciousness.

Now let me ask a question: Why is it the case by which one experiences all three realms without separating oneself? Why is it the case that one is able to have a physical body, an immaterial body and a formless consciousness with no separation from the experience?

Who is behind this? Who experiences all these realms?

Now when one is in possession of any of these three modes (Material, Immaterial or Formless) of personality, do any of the other two become unreal? It is the case, by which the other two do not exist.

Similarly, turn to this question: Is one is in possession of the future or the past or the now? The right answer is: I shall be in the future, I was in the past and I am, now. One cannot be in the future, the past and now at the same time. When one is in the future, the other two do not exist in consciousness.

What is the lesson to be learnt here?

There are names, expressions, ways of speech, concepts, signs and all kinds of symbolisms in this world. The sage uses the name as a way of pointing, while the deluded man uses the name as its object. The sage uses the concept but is not led astray by them.

I hope you learnt something useful here. Otherwise, it is okay to come back repeatedly among phases of your cultivation to see if you've understood anything more as written here.

              > For me, God or Creator is one big ocean of consciousness and unit is its droplet .

              This is a Kabbalistic view, but is still limited in wisdom. What is subject to birth is subject to change, death, decay and impermanence. Even this huge ocean of consciousness itself is impermanent. What then, is the point of an impermanent being to seek the point of another impermanent phenomenon?

              > I see one individual who is in incarnation composited of three parts: subconsciousness,consciousness(unit) and higher consciousness(higher self).

              Your understanding is close, but not its full perspective yet. The end-result of what you speak of is known as the three bodies of a fully enlightened being. The Nirmanakaya, the Sambhogakaya and the Dharmakaya. Respectively, they are the Transformation, Reward and Truth bodies.

              The Transformation body is the physical body and subtle body, which is what is subject to transformation. The Reward body is the manifestation of the mind, such as one's behaviour, consciousness and communication. The Truth body is the formless nature of the Dharma itself.

              If you were to relate it to the imperfect forms, you can closely associate the Transformation body as the perfected subconsciousness (although this is quite inaccurate as it does not include the whole physical manifestation itself), the Reward body as the perfected consciousness "unit” and its manifestations and lastly, an even "higher" plane of the Reward body as the higher self. True Dharmakaya is not rediscovered until one becomes enlightened to Dharma, although it is indeed inherent in all sentient beings.

              Remember that seeking the Dharma is the ultimate realization for all seekers of the Path.

              > This three are going through various incarnations and evolve.

              It is true for the rest. All that is subject to birth is subject to change, and eventual death. However, the Dharmakaya does not change.

              > We are that middle consciousness unit who go through various experiences and collect karma through incarnations, higher self lead us, subconsciousness direct body function and manage memories from this and past

              The Reward body is the manifestation of the mind, also known as the point at which karma is accumulated and experienced. The higher self itself is just another plane of the Reward body, which is subject to change. The subconsciousness you speak of is the Transformation body. By observing right sila and practicing right samadhi, one develops respectively the Transformation and Reward bodies.

              > When time for enlightenment come , all three join together first and then evolve up one level.

              This is not true. When a being becomes enlightened, that being has access to all three states of Transformation, Reward and Truth bodies. Saying that they join together means that this being is stuck in the formless realm, which is not what the Buddha was talking about.

              As a similar example, you can see masters of Zen who awaken to the Dharmakaya. Once awakened to the truth, they are at the verge of enlightenment and not fully enlightened yet. Once they have awakened to the Truth body, they will take time off to seclude themselves and practice right samadhi and sila in order to cultivate the Transformation and Reward bodies. Sometimes, a being might simply get rid of the physical shell and engage in Bardo enlightenment practice as the physical body itself is difficult to transform and cultivate. This is only possible at the point of near-death after a human birth. Nonetheless, the Master has full ability to all three realms and beyond. Being free of ignorance the Master is free of all conditions.

              > Subconsciousness become consciousness unit(I call them very young souls because of lack of experiences) , consciousness become higher self, and higher self go higher in hierarchy.

              The purpose of this happening is a vicious cycle, where multiple units or off-shoots are going through thousands of lifetimes. This is exactly what the Buddha is trying to stop - the delusions that have caused endless suffering. It is almost like a slaughterhouse.

              The process is by which a "soul" returns after death. Feeling that one is still not transformed or awakened to the dharma, the process of life continues again from this ignorance.

              There is no merging or hierarchy in dharma. The simplest man in physical nature is able to become enlightened. This Dharmakaya is everywhere - it is only with those with perfected wisdom to see with the opened dharma-eye.

              Sometimes, if someone has reached a certain understanding but is unable to do so, it is because their Reward body has hindered that - Such as the killing of a person sowing huge unmeritorious seeds of karma.

              > I wanted to ask you about your experiences in regarding your soul, when you saw her, more details, if you may share with us.

              How should one practice and cultivate this transformation body? Through the practice of sila, samadhi and prajna, one is naturally transformed and liberated.
