# The Need for Self-Realization - Not Memory Work

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/33pp6f/the_need_for_selfrealization_not_memory_work/>

One of the most important things you have to realize is that advice and suttas you read/hear from the enlightened sangha cannot possibly substitute the real wisdom derived by your own inherent nature.

Many people read suttas and principles and learn them by memory work and "common sense". This is not bad. However, it is bad when one starts to think that one has already realized these principles by one's own when it is pure memory work - Taking another's effort for one's own effort.

Realization has to be from within oneself. Words and advice are given to spark that realization in another person - Like a finger pointing towards the truth. A dharma teacher can point his finger at the direction to go, but he cannot transfer what he experiences directly to you. You must go in that direction to find out.

For example, what is the concept of interdependent origination? Many people who have read it know it conceptually. Some say that it is "emptiness". Some say it is "cause and effect". Some say that "form is emptiness and emptiness is form". However, to understand this meaning truly, one has to apply it to one's life and living, then be able to realize it for oneself.

We know that a table 'exists'. What if we chop this table into many pieces? Where is the table now? Can you call one fragment of the table a table?

If you trace the table back in history, you can trace it to the factory, the trunk of a tree, the seed of the tree, the parent tree and all the way to countless causes. For these causes to sprout, just like how harvests only ripen seasonally, conditions (which are causes themselves) must be present.

What exactly is a table but a 'false name'? It is a temporary name for a temporary mental concept. Everything that starts has an end. For that short timeframe, it is known as a 'table'. It does not mean that the table does not exist, it means that the table is in fact wood.

You see the gold in gold jewellery, instead of the ornamental features of the gold. You know that a gold necklace exists, but yet you understand that it was smelted from gold and will one day return back to gold.

Similarly, the world is generated from the interaction of polarities. These polarities then interact in several ways that show fire, water, earth and wind elemental properties. Everything else is just a complicated mash of cause and effect.

For example, right now, I am Paramananda. If you were to chop off my head, would you call my body or my head Paramananda? Similarly, if my body becomes a corpse, would you call my body Paramananda?

There is no-self. Instead of hearing it from me, live a contemplative life with the Eightfold Path and strive to realize it by yourself.

If there is truly a self, this self will be able to control things under it. Can you stop your body from ageing? Can you stop your body from getting diseases? Can you stop your body from death? This is why your body is not you. This is no-self.

The way to liberation is not simply wisdom - It is living it. It is abiding to the precepts as well as the mind that supports and allows it to unfold naturally. The Eightfold Path - All 8 factors of it, is the fastest way to gain merit. There is no doubt about it.

So study hard, but do not forget to apply hard, investigate hard, and realize hard as well. Strive hard and while your human life comes to an end, I sincerely hope that you have reached enlightenment in this lifetime.
