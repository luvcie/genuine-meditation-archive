# Welcome, friend.

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Notice` · 9 points · 2 years ago

My goal of opening this subreddit is not to have a teacher-student relationship. Instead, we strive as friends - as seekers of the Dharma. When we have reached stream winning, we then help the fellowship of stream-seekers. We are all mentors to each other.

Please be honest with yourself in this subreddit. There is no need to censor anything. Talk about anything. Talk about the deepest pain, talk about the problems that are hindering your path. Share about the delight you undertake. Share your progress. Share your insights.

Over time, I will be helping to post help regarding meditation and lifestyle living. Ask a question if you deem fit, do not be afraid to.

If you find something questionable, question it. However, one should not rely on the sayings of another nor the teachings of any holy scripture, text, etc. You should investigate it on your own.

Lastly, strive well on the path.
