(8) Be sensitive to the mind

There is a switch towards the realm of the mental.

In this state, the external state of the body still seems as if it was the body given to you by your parents. However, the four great elements in your body have changed in energy, it is no longer the ordinary flesh body. Even the feeling is completely different now.

In this state, we say that “When jing is full, we do not think of lust; When chi is full, we do not think of eating; When shen is full we do not think of sleeping.” In this state, we don’t have to eat, we only need water.

Here, we can go from first jhana to second jhana, which is characterized by bliss and joy birthed from tranquility.

In this stage, we can see clearly our own thoughts, habits and shortcomings. We can see the rising and falling of thoughts. There is no association with “breathe long and short” anymore.

What is the second jhana? The defining quality is tranquility, and this tranquility refers to the non-dual quiescence of the mind.
