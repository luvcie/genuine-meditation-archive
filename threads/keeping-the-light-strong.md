# Keeping the light strong

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 10 points · 1 year ago

The clear light of the mind never ends, but the false self sees it as having dimmed away.

When you sit in anapanasati and pay attention to the breath, do not disregard other things that come to your mind. As they appear, realize that the same "knowing nature” is looking at them. Do not let the "knowing" weaken in its strength.

Look at it as if it was a bright light. Paying attention to the breath, one's light is bright.

However, some sangha members seem to have gotten this part wrong. When a thought surfaces, they have a two-fold mistake:

1. Firstly, one immediately disregards the thought, and as a consequence, weakens the "knowing nature”, "dimming the light".

2. Secondly, one loses mindfulness, and follows the thought wherever it goes, as if one has fallen asleep, later to snap awake.

In both ways, one has fallen prey to the hindrance of torpor.

For the former, one should in the same way, be aware of the thoughts or the distractions that has arisen, noting that they have risen. Now, knowing that they have risen, while keeping the "knowing nature" strong and bright, shift the light of attention back onto the breath.

This is what is known as vitarka and vicara.

If one does this right, immediately - joy will start to rise within the heart. The mind will be clear and free from the five hindrances.

At this juncture, I would like to share what is known as the teaching of the Clear Light, which comes down from the direct teaching lineage of Milarepa.

We have a conscious awareness, known as the Child Light, in the head. The Mother Light, resides in the heart. At no point were they ever separate - however, the mind itself has generated the appearance of separation.

When most human beings die, they, having lived their lives as the Child Light, look down to the Mother Light, and see the crystal energy channel that connects the two. This is also known as the "tunnel of light", or kati.

Now the Mother Light bears the original nature and exudes unconditional love. The Child Light merges into its origin, and one enters enlightenment.

Ramana Maharshi once remarked:

> When the mind eventually sinks into the Heart, undisturbed bliss is overwhelming felt. There is then a feeling that is not divorced from pure awareness, as an example, head and heart become one and the same.

Metta is an excellent meditation to practice this.

The secret is not in inhalation, but exhalation - The "giving" is the secret. Practice meritorious acts and deeds, live in charity with a kind heart. Speak, act and think kindly.

Every moment, every mental thought or event, every feeling, every perception is an expression of the perfect Buddha Nature. Dharma is found everywhere.

As the Christian Bible says:

> Split a piece of wood, I am there. Lift up the stone, and you will find me there.

Every single thing can be a point for you to awaken.

Strive on and may you be enlightened in this lifetime.
