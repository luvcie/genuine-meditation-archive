# Lying Down Meditation

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Technique` · 3 points · 2015-06-02

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/388kq2/lying_down_meditation/>

This post will be on lying-down meditation, which is a method of reaching deep states easily, especially during sleep.

The 'technique', is simply holding on your consciousness as you let the body fall into sleep. Simply watching, observe all the phenomena unfolding as the body goes through the processes of relaxation and finally sleep.

Avoid caffeine or any kind of mind-affecting substance. Remove the pillow and lie on your back, this will prevent gravity from drawing away the blood from your brain during sleep. I find that lying on the side puts pressure on the body organs and can reduce the time that the body remains in the posture. As such, the ideal posture is lying down, as if one were a corpse.

This is not a difficult thing to accomplish, although it may take quite a while, perhaps months to do. The key thing is to keep up a regimen of meditation in the waking hours, so that when one lies down to sleep, one is able to keep awareness.

Doing this, one will easily find themselves in states of samadhi, where there is a transition into an out-of-body experience, past-life recollections and various siddhis. Most importantly, if one were to pass away on dissolution of the body, in the "bardo period", one will be able to stay aware and decide one's rebirth.

Again, one might be able to feel jhana traits. Also, one will also experience jhana-nimitta. However, this technique will train one to understand what it means to let go. It will also offer a peek into the immaterial and increase one's faith in the Dharma.

Strive well and may you find enlightenment.

One thing to add:

You should realize that this technique allows one to reach fifth Samadhi and beyond. However, if one's saturation in the 4 jhanas is not great, then this still does not constitute real cultivation.

The Buddha had mastered out-of-body samadhis under two masters (two of the highest in the immaterial realm), but it is only in the jhanas that he found liberation. Getting out of one's body is something any mind can do. In sleep, nearly everyone projects their consciousnesses out, it is only a matter of whether one is aware or not. However, to truly escape birth-and-death, that is where one must seek refuge in the jhanas and reach transcendental liberation.

However, to truly have a faith and appreciation for the Buddha's dharma, one can have a peek through these various samadhis. By doing so, you will also understand the rationale, as well as the Four Noble Truths to a greater degree.

It is important to note though, that any siddhi is secondary to liberation. Ghosts and demons can have siddhis too, what makes them so special? It is nothing special. What is truly special, is the liberation of mind.
