# The Treasure of Attention

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 9 points · 1 month ago

If one can pay attention to a leaf, a stone or a flower, without a single break in attention, without a single fluctuation, without a single arising of self-thought - one will be able to penetrate this object's being. There is nothing in this Universe where the 'divine breath’ does not touch or enliven.

One's precious commodity is attention - attention applied sustainably without fluctuation on an object gives rise to absorption into a certain mental state.

The average human being walks around with one's mind filled with chatter and delusional thoughts. A sudden shock, and suddenly one's mind is as free as the open sky. One moment later and one's self-consciousness comes back to discriminate the world.

It is the same as mystics and meditators. At the moment of sitting down, the mind is free. At the thought of meditating, the mind is imprisoned. There are multiple gaps whereby this entry appears, but it is such a pity that many do not grasp the doorway!

It is not the object that you sustain your attention on that is the problem - your mental state is the problem!

One might say: My mind is as free as the clear sky! The truth is that when one is perceiving it as such, there is a discriminatory process that has arisen after that - the self-image has appeared and there is a 'being’ that is perceiving that clear space.

Be intensely conscious and do not let it slip by. What one attains through deep meditation, do not lose it after standing up from the mat.

As one always knows, there is a mechanical, cyclical nature of habit that constantly pulls one back into a mundane, muddled, delusion-filled state that is like "sleeping while awake".

It is not easy to reach such a state, but yet it can be very simple. What one must have is a razor-sharp absorption that does not waver, a feeling of devotion/wonder/mystery/awe that drives the connection towards Spirit, and the sense of the body and its energy.

At its peak, one will find it wondrous - how is it that another is speaking to me, but yet it is like I am speaking to myself? Reading about the insights of another person is not the same as finding out by one's own efforts.

Do not be lazy, one must strive on, one must have vigor. Whether it is utter submission to a Guru who has gone the Path, or prostrating at the feet of the divine, or surrendering to the sound of Tao that contains all harmonics of the universe at its subtlest, one must utilize this compassion to drive both mind and body towards the One Buddha Vehicle.

As you read these words, is there a self reading? You can see how spontaneously it arises. One must never allow one to be satisfied - if you sit in a routine, never striving, you will never reach the path.

On the Path, you are not allowed to be comfortable - you must strive continuously. A master will bend you, tear you apart, and move you towards where you are constantly supposed to be moving towards.

It is okay to not know things. Encourage the sense of wonder in the mystery of the universe. Do not try to find the answers to questions that you do not know. It is okay to say that you do not know.

Thinking that one is filled with knowledge is one of the stupidest things to do. It is filling your pockets with stones and trying to climb up a mountain. Without continuous practice and investigation, this knowledge is nothing but dung. Strive hard and may you attain enlightenment in this lifetime.
