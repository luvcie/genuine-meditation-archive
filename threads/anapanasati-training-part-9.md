(13) Observe Releasing

If you have “chi” and you trap it in, but yet do not observe releasing, you don’t have any wisdom to breakthrough. When you are observing releasing, you have to let go of all chi so that it goes into emptiness. You are no longer cultivating chi, you are cultivating emptiness.

There is a secret here. It is possible that when you constantly observe releasing and reach a certain stage, you not only can foresee your death, but you can simply write a hymn and die by turning into light. If you have penetrated through this, you can be just like those Tantric masters who were like living Buddhas. Whenever you want to go, the entire body burns with fire and turn into light, leaving nothing behind.

This is why you need to take note of this. Whenever people cultivate their bodies, they forget all about releasing, where you need to discard all your cultivation skills, your body – everything. Once you have let go of everything, even Samadhi realms, you will feel as if everywhere is light. You will merge with the universe and emptiness to become one.

Releasing, is where you exhale everything out. This is why when you have a sickness or obstruction, pay attention to your breathing – Breathe it out.

To think that just like in Yoga, you breathe in all the “chi” – this is wrong. Releasing, is about giving, where you give all meritorious achievements back to all sentient beings.
