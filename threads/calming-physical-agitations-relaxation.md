# Calming Physical Agitations (Relaxation)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Technique` · 6 points · 2015-01-26

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2tq2qj/calming_physical_agitations_relaxation/>

One of the things that get in the way of many meditators, even experienced ones, is the physical body's agitation.

These agitations can be in the form of pain, tenseness in the muscles, soreness, aches, etc. How does one learn how to remove these physical agitations?

Doing this full-body relaxation method, one understands how the body can become fully relaxed and without physical agitation and learn how to apply it with regards to their meditative object.

1. Sit in a meditative position. For this particular exercise, it does not matter if you sit cross-legged or in the chair. It has be comfortable and you must be able to maintain your spinal balance when the body is relaxed.

2. Close your eyes. Imagine a huge block of bright, warm liquid floating above the crown of your head. It does not matter if you cannot visualize, simply feel its warmth.

3. As if taking a hot shower, imagine this block of liquid melting and trickling downwards.

4. Now for every part of your body that this liquid trickles over, feel the warmth of the liquid in contrast to your body. I want you to feel the skin, as if it were agitatedly vibrating. If you focus and become aware, you will find that some parts of the skin seem to vibrate. Let the warmth trickle over and relax it. If you are unable to release the tension, breathe in to hold the tension and as you breathe out, relax all muscles in that area.

5. The course for this liquid should be as such: Crown, back of head, forehead, eyes, nose, cheeks, mouth, jaw, front of neck, back of neck extending to shoulders, shoulders, front of chest, arms, forearms, wrists, palms, fingers, front of stomach, back (imagine it sinking as the muscles relax), front of hip, back of hip, thighs, knees, legs, feet, toes.

6. Take your time for each until you are completely free of tension in that area.

7. Now when you reach the bottom, do the same without the warm liquid now. Simply feel the tension, and release any remaining tension. Working your way up from the feet, each time you release, lose all feeling in that body part. You will find that if you have sufficiently released your muscle tension, you will not be able to feel anything in that body part when you shift your attention to the upper body parts.

8. Work your way all the way up to the navel.

9. At the navel, imagine that the bright, fragrant, warm liquid from earlier has already accumulated and risen to the navel level. Imagine it simmering, giving off cool steam that floats upwards.

10. Release even more tension. Now continue relaxing any remaining tension all the way up to the head, until you can no longer feel anything from your body.

At this point, you may choose to start a meditation method, or even just get out from the relaxation. Your body will feel so refreshed it is as if it has taken many hours of refreshing sleep.

When you have done this, you will understand what the meaning of "calming physical agitations" as stated in the breath meditation thread really is.

Another thing that might happen is that you might simply transit into first Jhana simply doing this. The reason for this is because of the momentary cessation of mental thought and sensations (physical agitations have already ceased), allowing the bliss of Jhana to shine through.

This is also used as a therapy for anybody who is having tense/headaches as a result of excessive concentration.

In fact, if you are able to reach the point where the whole body below the navel is warm, you will find that your health improves the more you use this technique.
