# Fasting

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Technique` · 4 points · 2 years ago

There are some people who have asked about fasting. For those who have not experienced its benefits, here are

some:

o It shifts energy away from digesting food and into allowing a smoother flow throughout your body. If you are constantly eating, it can tax your digestive system and a significant percentage of energy goes towards this digestion.

Apart from digestion, the body has to work hard to process the food as well as in detoxification.

If the food is in excess, then energy has to be stored in form of fat. Otherwise, excessive spikes in nutrients can tax the body in various wear and tear.

Having more energy to bring towards meditation, it allows you to stay longer in emptiness.

Many people also do not realize that within food, there are many different chemicals, such as preservatives. There are some compounds that can be addictive. Some people do not realize that some meat can be injected with steroids and that they are addicted to the steroids instead of the meat. By maintaining a length of time without them, you can identify such compulsions.

One becomes more sensitive to what one eats, and starts to develop healthier options that lead to an increased health.

The yogi refer to this physical body as the 'food body’, simply because food is converted into a part of our own body. But over-intake simply taxes the body and does not give any extra benefit, except for gustatory sensory pleasure. Enough should be taken adequately for maintaining energy levels.

However, there is a method to approach fasting, and you should not have a harsh transition. One should gradually introduce the fast, by slowly eating light, such as half the amount in the day before you decide to fast.

Also, when coming out of a fast, little amounts should be introduced. Healthy food should be taken, or the 'cleaner gut will have trouble adjusting back to it.

The more one does fasting, the easier this process will also become, and the more one will enjoy the sensations of having a more purified energy. If you meditate in the morning, after a long hour's sleep, without breakfast, then you would immediately notice the huge difference, as compared to directly after a heavy meal.

Starting a Fast

1. Decide how frequently you will want to fast. For a start, two times a month is good. Decide whether you want to fast for just one day or not. Do not change your mind after setting it.

2. You will simply take in fruit during a fast. Therefore, if you chose a one-day fast, then you will have an apple for breakfast, lunch and dinner each.

3. After getting used to it, then if you want to raise it up to two days, decide so, then eat 3 apples for the first day and 2 apples for the second.

4. After becoming comfortable with a 2-day fast, go towards a 3-day fast, where you eat 3 apples for the first day, 2 apples for the second and 1 apple for the third day.

You can go for pine nuts instead of apples, if you wish. They contain good nutrients and energy. Also, continue to take in water throughout the fast, approximately seven glasses of water the whole day.

I do not recommend that you go without water, unless you are under the supervision of a master.

I hope this helps. Strive well.
