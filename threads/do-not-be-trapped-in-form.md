# Do not be trapped in form!

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 4 points · 2016-03-24

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/4bqflh/do_not_be_trapped_in_form/>

You and I, we are no different.

You and the Buddha, there is no difference.

But notice who I am talking to right now.

Who is aware of these words?

It is not your physical body.

It is not your feelings.

It is not your perception.

It is not your impulses and habits.

It is not your cognition.

Who is listening?

When you sit down to meditate, who is aware of it all?

When one thought leaves and another arrives, who is there to watch the gap between the two?

When the body has an inhalation and then an exhalation, who is there to watch the gap between the two?

Who is the one thinking that they are becoming a spiritual person?

Who is the one identifying with the physical body as self, the sensations as self, the impulses as self, and the cognition as self?

Who is the one saying "I am this"?

If you answer that it is your awareness, then you are not there yet. Who is watching that awareness?

When you were a child, you felt light in your body. Your mind buzzed with ambition and possibilities. You were full of joy.

Now that you are older, you feel more sluggish and rigid. You think you have learnt things and are wiser. You no longer have that same joy.

But have you really perceived a change? Who is the one who has been there when your body was a child? Who is the one who is still here, reading these words right now?

Emptiness is form, form is emptiness.

Why is it empty? Because everything is let go of. There is nothing to grasp onto, because there are no restrictions.

Emptiness is not a vacuum. It is pure love. It is not just a feeling. It is a state of being.

That is who you are.

But you cannot just mentally understand these words.

You must realize it for your own. Do not be trapped in your thoughts, thinking, "I am making progress. If I follow this and that, I will be enlightened."

You are either awake, or you are asleep. There is no in-between. When the Buddha was asked what he was, he simply replied, "I am awake."

Who are you?

Who are you before you assumed this physical body?

Who are you after you die?

Who is meditating?

Who sits there reading my words?

Who contemplates the Buddha?

Who wants enlightenment?

Don't look at my finger. Look at where I am pointing to.

I cannot point to you.

Gaze from the reflection and peer into yourself. You cannot see yourself. But you can realize it.
