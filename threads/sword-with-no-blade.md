# Sword With NO Blade

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **orihh** · *(OCR)* · `Advice` · 3 points · 1 year ago

Oh what empty joy!

What vacant laughter and reasonless sobbing

Arises with the relief -

The ever-present relief,

When you find yourself staring down at that which you once called

", "me", "mine".

This sword with no blade,

It is the butt of the most supreme joke.

Truly, there is nothing more funny,

And nothing less funny,

Than the moment when you see.

Look.
