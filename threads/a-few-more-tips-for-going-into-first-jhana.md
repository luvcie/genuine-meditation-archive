# A few more tips for going into First Jhana

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/38ou2w/a_few_more_tips_for_going_into_first_jhana/>

There were some messages asking me to give more tips on going into first jhana, extending from this other post[1] . As I mentioned in that thread, not pushing away bliss is one of the most important things to note.

In the presence of a loved one, or perhaps when you feel completely loved, how does that feel? When you sit at a beautiful landscape and gaze upon the skies, lakes and mountains, and think to yourself, "Wow." How does that feel?

Now all of these things are very small and insignificant times where you might feel a little bit of bliss. When you appreciate the beauty in nature, or perhaps when you feel refreshed after a good sleep. Your mind switches from an unwholesome state bothered by the five hindrances, to that of one with a very bright, wholesome state.

The joy of the jhanas is much greater. It is overwhelming, so overwhelming that one would never doubt it once attained.

There are certain tips that some meditation teachers give, such as "smile" or "see the object as beautiful". All of these can be summed up as "let go and allow bliss to rise".

The first tip, is for you to enjoy meditation. Sitting right there, meditation is not a task. It is not a difficult thing to do, simply sitting there. You are releasing everything of the past and future, and residing in the now.

Secondly, you must release and let go of cognitive thoughts and the five senses. Is bliss associated with constant questioning? When you are in wonder - You certainly do not talk or verbalize thoughts. You do not need the five senses either.

Thirdly, excitement is one of the unwholesome mental states, apart from sluggishness. These two mind states are what you want to watch out for. If your bliss is not rising, your mind is too sluggish, you are suppressing your bliss and not allowing it to naturally unfold. If your excitement is too much, you might start to think and this will upset the one-pointedness you had developed.

I hope this helps out more.
