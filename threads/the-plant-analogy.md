# The plant analogy

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 4 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3dq03n/the_plant_analogy/>

When helping a seedling grow, there are three ways.

The first is to ignore the plants and leave them alone. Without weeding, without watering and patience, the plants die before they blossom. Some might even be reading so much into theory and technology, that they forget to nurture the plants.

The second is to be impatient and eager. Being eager, they pull the seedlings, they add excessive water, they sanitize the soil so much, that the plants become impeded in growth.

The third is to simply watch the plant. When it needs water, water is added. When it needs weeding, weeding is done. Having patience, over a long period of time, one watches the plant blossom.

This also applies to cultivation practice.

The first kind are the ones who have never learnt how to purify the mind, or the ones who neglect their practice. Neglecting diet, neglecting mindfulness at all times, neglecting practices, neglecting sila (right thought, speech and action) - All this will do is allow the weeds to grow. Weeds eventually strangle the plant, and the plant is impeded from growing.

The second kind are the ones who have learnt how to purify the mind, but are having misguided conceptions. Thinking that they can control energy, they disrupt the flow of energy instead. Using different techniques, they do not realize that they are wrecking the body. Having good intentions, they commit actions without wisdom. Virtuosity is also in how sensible a person is.

The third kind are the ones who have learnt to let go of the body, sensations, thoughts and mental qualities. By letting go, they simply watch them without being identified as them. Letting go of mental chatter, the mind is purified. Withdrawing from sensory pleasure, letting go of false thoughts, energy starts to work naturally. Everything that was unnatural starts to reset back to its natural way of working. Without forcing energy, the chi/energy becomes harmonized with nature.

What is right now is inevitable. There is no way to escape any situation that is right now. Hence, the only action you can do is to accept it. Even if you do not accept it, the present is inevitable. There is nothing you can do. When you accept it as such, contentment flourishes. When you stop resisting, nature takes its course.

However, the mind is habituated. It flickers back and forth, from past to present, never staying in the present moment. It clings on to the moment now, but it has already went into the past. The moment it clings, it has deviated. Hence, "pulling the weeds" is to reverse this mental fantasy. What has past does not exist now. What is going to happen is not inevitable. The only thing that is inevitable and acceptable is right now.

If you do not stop to pay attention, you will miss it. If you are caught up in your thoughts birthed from clinging onto memory, or birthed from a past-projection into the future, you will miss the present.

The internal identification with the physical body as the self causes trouble. You can only fear change, age, decay and death. The identification with the etheric body is also trouble. Any internal body image is trouble. It is never the self, it is only that we have been habituated to see it as the self.

If you can see that this physical body is not you, and drop it, you will find suddenly that all your tensions will collapse away. Even the physical pleasures and pain are not you. Sexual drive is not you. Physical contact is not you. Movement is not you. Stillness is not you.

Sit there and close your eyes. When you see things, that is not you. When you see "darkness", that is not you either. What you hear, touch, taste or feel - All of that aren't you either. Do not mistake it as you. Just because you have sensory contact with it, does not mean that it is you.

The body knows how to recuperate. So does the etheric body. Hence, you only impede it with false notions. While being ignorant, people flood the mind with many false notions. Weeding them, you allow the mind to function naturally.

When you get to a higher wisdom, you will even realize this: That there were no weeds in the fundamental nature. Because you have identified as the busy thought-train, you conceived of weeds. There are deeper levels of the mind than just thoughts.

Just sit there, witnessing what is not-self. Tie the attention to the natural movement of the body and its breath. Allowing the mind to settle down, the breath calms down. Just watching it, you reach respiratory cessation. From then on, the purification of the elements proceed in the following order:

• Wind (air movement)
• Water (hormones, vital essence)

• Fire (kundalini - cleansing period to warm, comfortable bliss)

• Earth (body starts to change physically, projection of bodies)

• Akasa (spatial changes)
• Consciousness

Hence, you should understand that kundalini is a mere starting point. It is not even halfway there. Patience is needed. Years of dedicated practice are needed to reach the final point.

All of these unfold, the more you can disidentify from the wrong notions of a self. Remember that being disidentified does not mean that you completely disregard it. If you are drained from life, that is "dead-tree practice" - You become joyless. Joy and involvement, not detachment, is needed on the path.

May Dharma bring you to peace.
