# Skin

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Discussion` · 2 points · 2 years ago

For one who is working through blockages, it is effective if one does the following:

e In your breath meditation, focus not on the nose nor chest nor lungs, but the entire skin.

e Hands should be palm-up, non-interlocked and resting on the thighs, with fingers outstretched, such the fingers do not touch each other.

This should work for some who are struggling with blockages.
