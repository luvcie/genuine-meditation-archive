# Two modern erroneous paths

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 11 months ago

It is strange to see partial teachings in supposedly established monasteries, which led me to this short post.

To cultivate jhana without wisdom leads to delusion. To cultivate insight without jhana leads to lunacy and derangement.

They come hand in hand, and can arise together. Do not disregard wisdom for absorption, nor throw away absorption for insight. Without absorption, one falls into the deviant paths easily. Without insight, one is constantly in bliss but it is as if one is on drugs and does not attain anything of use.

The five sensory outflows restrain one from entering absorption, so your morality must be perfected, so that the mind remains at ease. These are the five thieves that run away with your hard earned work.
