# Anapanasati Training (Part 1)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 11 points · 1 year ago

/u/infinitesustain has asked how anapanasati leads to seeing the Tao. This is a complex question and will be answered in a series. To understand this, one needs to understand and practice anapanasati to a greater depth.

There are 16 important points that one must understand when practicing Anapanasati. I will talk about the first two in this post, and cover the rest gradually.

Know when one is breathing in.

Know when one is breathing out.

Do you truly understand these two statements? It is unlikely.

As you read these words, are you aware of your breathing in and out at the same time? When you do anything, be it listening to a person speak, or accomplishing a task, are you aware of yourself breathing in and out? Try it - can you read and still be fully aware of your breathing in and out?

If you cannot, what cultivation do you have? Only one who can do that is worthy of saying that one is cultivating.

This "knowing" is accomplished by your heart - not your eyes or your nose.

You need to become one with this, no matter whether you are talking, listening, or doing anything.

What use is it if you simply sit on a mat and then start being aware of your breathing? Do you then forget it when you stand up from the mat? That is not cultivation.

At all times, know your in and out breath.

Don't think about your dantian or your stomach or your torso. Ignore everything under the throat. What one needs to know is the breath, and that is all. Don't add the burden of the body.

If your mind goes to thoughts, do not worry. Just use your awareness and know that your mind has moved to thoughts, then move it back to the breath.

Hence your training for this is to know at all times that you are breathing. You cannot say you are cultivating if you are only aware of your breath on the mat!
