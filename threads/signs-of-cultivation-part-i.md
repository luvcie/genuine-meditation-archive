# Signs of Cultivation (Part I)

This post will be the first of a series of signs to look out for in cultivation. Do not take it as a ends-to-a-means. Signs can surface in a multitude of ways.

Human beings possess both heaven and earth within our dualistic nature. Hence through our thoughts, speech and actions, we polarize towards an animalistic or divine nature. Spirituality or carnality - That is chosen by oneself.

Introduction to Chakras and the Etheric Body

The human body has an invisible etheric body, just as all things Yang have its Yin. This Etheric body, or astral body, is composed of prana, breath, life force, vitality or "qi". Just like rivers and lakes, this energy is carried by channels across the etheric body. Chakras are like distributive wheels along these channels. They are sometimes called "lotus flowers" to symbolize this distributive quality through the petals.

Each petal symbolizes a sub-channel or a minor channel that branches from the central channel. The heart chakra is said to have 8 petals, and hence there are 8 sub-channels emanating from it.

Many people know that there are 7 major chakras, but they do not know that within these chakras are mini-chakras themselves. The whole etheric body is lined with thousands of mini-chakras.

Currently, we are unpurified energetically. Our energy channels, once cultivated, blazes with kundalini (or original "qi"). However, uncultivated, our mind clings to false thoughts and form, taking this physical body of the five elements to be our true self. By cultivating through meditation and non-clingingness, the five elements of the body become cultivated and even the channels and chakras will transmute to become that like of devas and heavenly beings. Free yourself of attachment to the physical body. Meditation is the only way to learn detachment.

Our physical bodies came from a template from a heaven within the Form Realm, which in turn came from the Formless Realm heavens. Hence, there are remnants of the Formless Realm heavens within this coarse physical body of ours.

Kundalini

By cultivating correctly, one then awakens the kundalini or original qi (also called "internal wind"). Fromthe bottom of the perineum, this sleeping force arises when conditions are met. This condition is when you are able to have the stillness and emptiness of mind. When the thoughts settle down, it is only in this stillness where the regenerative power arises.

Let go of consciousness by introspection and investigating its origin - Then through insight, you will arrive at "realization" at its source.

There are two aspects of kundalini.

• The first is unpurified kundalini, which generates friction against obstructions within channels. It is painful, hot and not the least comfortable. Heat, irritation, sweating, shaking, jerking, sleeplessness, discomfort and all kinds of unthinkable phenomena happen when the cleansing stage happens. There is huge ferocity in kundalini when your energetic channels are clogged and full of obstructions.

• The second is purified kundalini. This only happens when obstructions are cleared, generating a very warm and blissful circulation. On its first ascent, it rises to the crown of the head and then descents to coat the skin layers, as if you were being "bagged" by layers of cellophane (This is "genuine baptism"). Eventually, you get seasoned to it and do not feel it anymore when all the channels open up.

If you are a male, one way to easily measure your strength of kundalini level once arisen, is to look at one's erection when you are devoid of lust and sexual desire.

Mind and Body Linked

Mind and wind go together like pairs. When you truly let go of clinging to consciousness, the body may vibrate or feel warmth, indicating kundalini movement.

There are many ways to awaken kundalini strenuously: Breath retention pranayama (such as the one shared in the subreddit), concentration meditation on certain points of the body, etc. However, by simply letting go of attachment ot thoughts and sensations, witnessing them without getting invovled, you can truly make a change instantly.

This is why we should have "constant mindfulness". Practice must be regular and diligent. Patience, time, consistent practice and the method are all factors to contributing to cultivation results. There is no room for laziness or compromise.

No one can realize your true self for you - Only you can. Unlike worldly possessions, spiritual cultivation is carried forth to the next life.

Very few have the deep desire to do this. Many say that they want to be liberated, but they do not have the deep intent to do so, because they simply want to believe that they intend it. The Buddha has said that results can only come from practice of the Path. There is no other way.

Stopping of the Breath

When kundalini starts to rise, this is normally when the breath stops. If you can maintain that state without breathing, without exerting force or strain, it will greatly transform your body.

When thoughts dwindle away and stop, your breath dwindles away and stops as well. This is where you engage in "embryonic breathing".

Actionable point of this post:

Practice pranayama on top of anapana or concentration meditation. It will accelerate your progress.
