# Dead-Tree Meditation

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Discussion` · 3 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3mnfmf/deadtree_meditation/>

There is a difference between cultivating to become insentient like a rock and cultivating to raise your sentience to the level of the cosmos.

The deluded man
Grasps onto traits and appearances
Thinking that sitting still without movement

And suppressing delusions so that they don't arise

Leads one to the samadhi of union.

Stuck in that delusion,
the man wonders,
Why have I not attained the Tao?

Little does he know
Holding still, suppressing, deluding oneself
Only makes him as dead as a rock.

Without kundalini,
Without bodily transformation,
There can only be a false void.

So throw not yourself
into the darkness of a false void,
But cultivate with a bright awareness
With joy, bliss, tranquility and equanimity
Let the energy rise within you
As a spiritual light
Only then can you say that is true void.

One who has succeeded
Bears the thirty-two marks of a Great Man

On one's hard-won physically transformed reward-body.

At will, one sends out his transformation-bodies.

At still, one is forever in dharma-body.

All three bodies cultivated,
One is the fully enlightened Buddha.

Do not think cultivation is that easy,
Strive hard and you will attain
Be negligent and you will abstain.

But stay not attached to transient form
Nor to the form of subtle mental states
Nor to the form of strange phenomena
Non-dwelling
Like a man with no roof over his head
He stays out of shelter
And continuously seeks.

Fervently, he attains the Tao.
