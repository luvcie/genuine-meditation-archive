# The Correct Mindset of Giving

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 3 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/30srbs/the_correct_mindset_of_giving/>

Not all giving is equal. Intention itself plays a huge role alongside what the deed does. But let us talk more about the psychological side of giving for now.

What is the Ideal mindset of giving?

One gives...

• Without seeking profit, reward, storing for oneself, or for enjoyment after death (With: Rebirths as the company of the Four Great Kings)

• Without thinking, "Giving is good" (With: Rebirths as the Devas of the Thirty-Three)

• Without thinking, "This was given in the past, let me follow the old family tradition." (With: Rebirths as the Devas of the Hours)

• Without thinking, "I am well-off, hence I should give to the less well-off" (With: Rebirths as the Contented Devas)

• Without thinking, "Sages of the past gave great sacrifices and hence I will give in the same way" (With: Rebirths as the Devas who delight in creation)

• Without thinking, "Giving makes my mind serene and joyful" (With: Rebirths as the Devas who have power over creation of others)

• Thinking: "This is supportive to the mind" (With: Rebirths as Brahma's Retinue and becomes a Non-returner)

Remember that to be enlightened, one does not simply lead a good meditation session. One has to live a rigorous lifestyle, with the Noble Eightfold Path as the lamp down the path.

What else takes more residence in your mind than your own lifestyle?

Reflect upon the Four Noble Truths, and apply the Noble Eightfold Path to your life. That way, you will see more fruit when you do sit down to meditate.
