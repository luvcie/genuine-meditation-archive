# Divine Abilities: The Divine Ear

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2tlr80/divine_abilities_the_divine_ear/>

Today's topic is on one of the abilities that the Buddha has talked about.

Lohicca Sutta

"With his mind thus concentrated, purified, & bright, unblemished, free from defects, pliant, malleable, steady, & attained to imperturbability, he directs & inclines it to the divine ear-element. He hears — by means of the divine ear-element, purified & surpassing the human — both kinds of sounds: divine & human, whether near or far. Just as if a man traveling along a highway were to hear the sounds of kettledrums, small drums, conchs, cymbals, & tom-toms. He would know, 'That is the sound of kettledrums, that is the sound of small drums, that is the sound of conchs, that is the sound of cymbals, and that is the sound of tom-toms.' In the same way — with his mind thus concentrated, purified, & bright, unblemished, free from defects, pliant, malleable, steady, & attained to imperturbability — the monk directs & inclines it to the divine ear-element. He hears — by means of the divine ear-element, purified & surpassing the human — both kinds of sounds: divine & human, whether near or far. When a disciple of a teacher attains this sort of grand distinction, Lohicca, that is a teacher not worthy of criticism in the world, and if anyone were to criticize this sort of teacher, the criticism would be false, unfactual, unrighteous, & blameworthy.

And a few lines from the Mahati Sutta:

Mahali Sutta Abstracts

• 'Suppose a contemplative, Mahali, to have attained one-sided absorption with the object of seeing such heavenly forms in any one direction,--in the East, or the South, or the West, or the North, or above; or below, or across,--and not with the object of hearing such heavenly sounds. Then since he has attained one-sided absorption, with the one object only in view, he only sees the sights, he hears not the sounds. And why not? Because of the nature of his samadhi.

• 'And so also, Mahali, if he has attained one-sided absorption with the object of hearing, in any one direction, the heavenly sounds. Then, and for the same reason, he hears the sounds, but he sees not the sights.

• 'But suppose, Mahali, he has attained meditative absorption (samadhi) with the double object in view of seeing and hearing, in any one direction, those heavenly sights and those heavenly sounds. Then since he has attained meditative absorption with the double object in view, he both sees the sights and hears the sounds. And why so? Because of the nature of his meditative absorption.'

Yes, even the Bible.

"I saw the glory of the God of Israel coming from the east. His voice was like the roar of rushing waters, and the land was radiant with his glory." -- Ezekiel 43:3

It is the case that in almost every mystical experience, there may be what is known as the "divine ear" experience.

This is often heard as a whistling, a bird song, rushing water, buzzing, ringing, etc.

Now this is easily confused with tinnitus, which is a medical condition. To understand where it originates from, ask this question: Did you suffer a head cold? Inflammation? If it is deemed to be physically not the case, then it must be the the occurrence of the Divine Ear.

This also means that due to a past merit or meditation effort in your past life, you have obtained the blessing for insights early in your life, leading to this Divine Ear.

If it is the case that you are hearing such a sound, it is possible for you to take this divine sound as a meditation object. Be careful not to exert force on it. Just like any other meditation object, simply be mindful. Strike a balance between attention and exerting strain physically.

Without expecting, simply let it be. Just like the visual nimitta, this ringing can expand. Remember, this only happens when you don't try to expect or look forward to it, just like the nimitta. Let go, don't grasp.
