# Antidotes to the Five Poisons

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 9 points · 2016-04-23

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/4g3ole/antidotes_to_the_five_poisons/>

Some have asked me about the five poisons or hindrances disturbing their meditations and causing obstruction. Here are the remedies.

1. Over-excitement/anxiety: Treat with contemplation on impermanence. All exciting things end. Another treatment is physical calmness, or humbling actions such as prostrations.

2. Sluggishness/depression/loss of energy: Treat with contemplation on the Triple Gem (Buddha, Dharma and Sangha), with love, with luminosity, with joy and the sense of brilliance. The root cause is the loss of longing for meditation, as well as the admiration of luminosity of the path. Contemplate on the fortune of learning the Dharma. Another way is physical exertion.

3. Doubtful/Discursive/Paralyzed mind: Treat with solving the question with a one-pointed mind.

4. Desire/Dissatisfaction: Treat with contemplation on the impossibility of satisfaction from material things that do not last. If desire on the body, treat with contemplation of the corruptibility of the body. If desire on money, treat with contemplation on the endless dissatisfaction even after acquiring material possessions, as well as their transiency.

5. Aversion/harmful thoughts: Treat with compassion, forgiveness and love. Aversion burns oneself first before anyone else, therefore one should learn how to take care of oneself first.

Understand your mind and tune it like a stringed instrument. Watch your mind like a loving musician who takes care of one's own instrument.
