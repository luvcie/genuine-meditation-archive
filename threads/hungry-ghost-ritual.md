# Hungry Ghost Ritual

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 2 months ago

Some members have asked me about the hungry ghost food offering ritual, and this is helpful for many people in their cultivation, particularly in meritorious deeds.

Firstly, to not re-invent the wheel, here is an instructive link from Bodri (whom I do not know personally due to Master Nan having many students). His instructions seem to be correct however.

What I will discuss here are the specifics, and what one can do with the ritual. This ritual is also known as "Zhunti food offering ritual®.

The best time to cultivate the "offering of food" is at night. However, when you do it, while thinking of the unfortunate beings who can only eat when you offer food, please do it without stopping for every single day.

Why? It is the same as what a human being should do to another human - if one promises something, it is good conduct to be trustworthy and follow on with one's promises.

The main benefit of such a ritual is this: When one comes to the end of one’s life, the mind will not have any fear.

For cultivators of the path, the offering of food is the most basic and fundamental way of cultivating one's mind-ground. Rice grains do not cost anything above a few cents, this small act can be easily done! One thing to note is never to offer meat to the ghosts because it can aggravate their suffering.

When one is traveling far, or in a car, you will always be eating something! In Christianity, one "thanks God" for their food.

In Buddhism, one thinks of others who have not had the fortune of having food. Having done that, you hold up tiny food offerings with that intention, and lightly throw them outside the window - this is also good. When you are wearing cloths, you too - think of many people who are unable to afford clothing. This is the practice of stirring one's compassion and arousing bodhicitta. To the cultivator of the Buddha's way, one will think of all sentient beings, not just humans.

In the lower three realms of samsara, there are the hell denizens, the beasts, and the hungry-ghosts (Pretas).

Firstly, ghosts do not have the intermediate-bardo body. The intermediate-bardo body is what humans have before entering rebirth. However, ghosts have already entered the ghost-path, it is not a soul, there are many types we can speak of.

For example, there are some bacteria that cause humans to become profoundly sick - these are also a type within the ghost-path. There are unseen ones with form, and yet others that are formless. Ghosts are subtler than humans, they pass through hard matter very easily, such as doors and walls.

As for the hungry-ghosts, they are very unfortunate. They are in fact, much bigger than us. For example, whales in the big oceans - they have a huge body mass, but yet their throats are tiny - they are eternally unable to fill themselves up to satiety. The Buddha described Pretas as having huge bodies and needle-thin throats. These are all due to one's karma.

If you look at the drinking water in this human world, one believes it to be clear and pure. However, a deva with a deva-eye will clearly see how turbid it is. There is a subterranean stone broth called "stone-water"”, as if it was milk bleeding out from stones, which devas drink - should us humans drink one mouthful, the body will become like precious stones, not needing to eat food for a long time.

Now look at these hungry-ghosts. Even if they wish to drink water, they are unable to because it scalds their throats. This is due to their karma, and also their physiology.

There is actually a book which should be investigated when one does the Zhunti method, called the "Xianmi Yuantong Chengfo Xinyao", however this is currently not available in English. I will try to find a translation, however these copies are often difficult to translate. One of the things that this book highlights is the following: In the morning, one offers to the devas, at night one offers to the ghosts.

Apart from offering to Buddhas, one should also offer devas. It is important to give happiness to all beings. For example, if there is someone who is sick, you give him medicine and he gets better, this is allowing him to reach peace and happiness. If someone is feeling down, and you sing a nice song to set his mind at peace, this is also an offering! There is nothing that is not an offering. Therefore, the first act of Buddhism is Dana - offering - giving.

This problem of hunger is everywhere. This world looks rich but there are so many hungry people around the world. Even the richest person can become so sick that they cannot swallow a single grain. This is when people have fallen in ghost-paths.

In this zhunti food offering method that my late teacher propagated, even if one forgets or the food is insufficient, there will not be anyone who will blame you. Therefore arouse your bodhicitta, never forget beings when you eat.

Therefore as a cultivator of the way, arouse your bodhicitta! Stir up your compassion for unfortunate beings. Stir up your compassion for your own predicament. Think of the people in poverty and in unfortunate situations. Whenever you can, do something for people. Suffering - massive suffering, everywhere! If one is enlightened, there is not a single day when one does not cry. O' great Bodhisattvas and Buddhas, how great their compassion! How wonderful is the Dharmal!

Strive hard and may you attain enlightenment in this lifetime.
