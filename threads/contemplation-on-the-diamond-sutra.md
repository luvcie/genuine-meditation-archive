# Contemplation on the Diamond Sutra

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Technique` · 8 points · 2 months ago

In the Diamond Sutra, there is the verse:

> “So I say to you - This is how to contemplate our conditioned existence in this fleeting world:” “Like a tiny drop of dew, or a bubble floating in a stream; Like a flash of lightning in a summer cloud, Or a flickering lamp, an illusion, a phantom, or a dream.” “So is all conditioned existence to be seen.”

How are these svmbols related?

> "As a tiny drop of dew"

One contemplates on how conditioned existence comes and goes quickly. There is wethess, cleanness and within dew contains the conditions for drying up - this is its void nature.

> "A bubble”

A bubble is spherical but hollow. The exterior surface is the ripening of conditions and the inside is its void nature.

> "An illusion”

Phenomena can seem to be real, especially when a magician performs an illusion. Not knowing the conditions that created it, one believes it to be real. Knowing the conditions, the entire act falls apart. What the magician does is unreal on examination - this is its void nature.

> "A dream"”

When a dream ends, all that is left is some memory. The inability to grasp anything after it ends, or while it lasts - the void nature is this. The full ripening of its conditions is what came together to produce the dream.

> "A phantom"

Just like a shadow which doesn't leave us, we see it but can never catch it. Just like the reflections in a mirror, we see it but can never grasp on them. We cannot grasp them because of their void nature, but we can see them because of the ripening of their conditions.

For fuller explanations, I recommend Master Nan Huai Jin's masterpiece book - Diamond Sutra Explained, which is translated by Pia Giamassi.

Another similar one is as such:

e Past mind cannot be grasped.

e Present mind cannot be grasped.

e Future mind cannot be grasped.

These contemplations can be done alongside Hinayana meditations or after them. Either way, they build the foundation of wisdom on the realization of sunyata.
