# Procrastination

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 13 points · 1 month ago

This is strongly linked to the habit of sloth. When one strongly encourages this tendency to postpone things to the future, this will always be pushed later and later, yet later, until it becomes too late. Before one knows it, 5 years, 10 years, 20 years have passed - and one is near one's deathbed.

This too, will happen in your meditation. As mentioned before, meditation /s life. It is not a separate thing at all.

What you do in life, is what you do in meditation. Meditation is just sitting down. Thinking that somehow you sit down, cross your limbs and straighten your back will lead you to an outcome different from how you live your life is astonishingly and exceedingly ignorant.

Did the Buddha propose just 2 folds of the Noble Eightfold Path? Did Patanjali propose a half-complete Eight-limb for Yoga? Did Christianity forsake the Ten Commandments? Of course not!

What is the use of ethics in the path? Ethics are actually not ethics - when you break a precept, this is exactly what leads to the disturbance of the mind - what breaks the condition for samadhi.

When one procrastinates in life, this too, manifests in habit when the conditions are ripe in your meditation. What is meant to be done right now, must be done right now. If a farmer neglects to water his crops, his crops will wither! How can one expect fruit?

In the same way, if you neglect mindfulness at every moment, thinking - oh, I can do it later while I am sitting... when I am not doing a task... when I am doing anything else but meditating... how foolish, how wasteful!

Do not let yourself be taken by something so useless. A disciple of the Buddha will never let his mindfulness be taken

away.

You can have your eyes wide open, but yet you are blind. You can hear all the sounds of the world but yet be deaf. You can think all the thoughts of the world but yet be like a vegetable.

You are either awake or not.

There is no in-between.

Every single time you let go of the Divine Nature, it is like pouring myriads of precious stones into the ocean. Every time you let go of the primordial nature, you are dumping gallons of oil into a pure pond.

One may get off the mat and drop their mindfulness, and when they sit again, they wonder, 'why have I not gained any progress?' One does not understand, that regression has already begun when one relinquishes one's mindfulness.

Meditation is practice for life. Life is practice for meditation. They are inseparable. What excuse does one have to not hold onto mindfulness, to not dwell in phenomena, to exercise inner charity, to dwell without dwelling, to perfect the four brahmaviharas?

Are you able to be like Moggallana, who sees Mara in every form? Do not waste time, right now - one should strive.
