# All About The Spiritual Crisis: Crying, anger, itches, pain, tingling, ringing, repressed thoughts, etc.

**r/Meditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 34 points

<https://www.reddit.com/r/Meditation/comments/2ueokd/>

> **[post was deleted on reddit]**

I noticed that there were a lot of posts about having sudden crying or rage periods, where someone would just all of a sudden, in unlikely periods, burst out in intense emotion. There were also posts about having all of these repressed thoughts bursting out. Having experienced every single one of them, I decided to make a post to try and help as much as I can.

Firstly, you are NOT crazy.
Do not let these things drive you crazy. It can seem as if you are crazy. If you ask a psychologist or psychiatrist, most of them going to diagnose you as mentally ill. Let me tell you the case: If all of that happened while you were meditating, then it is a phase that you have to go through. Some people might go through the phase with different or even no symptoms, but do not blame yourself. What you have sowed in thought, it is only right that you reap that harvest. The first thing to learn here? Responsibility for everything you have planted in your mind. You must
acknowledge it.

Then there's also the weird phenomena. Ringing? "Oh you definitely have a case of tinnitus." But not all ringing sounds are tinnitus. If you go for a physical examination and it does not show up - the doctors are now confounded. Let me explain all of that in this post.

Lastly, why do we need discipline and virtue? Why do all religions talk about that? How does that really relate to meditation practice? Do you think morality is that superficially interlinked with meditation?

The purpose of this post is to spread the information that it is not true that experiencing any of these things mean that you are insane. It is a complete lack of understanding.

Believe me, when you make it through all of these phases, you will feel free. Aware. You will see through everything. You will go into deeper states of meditation, deeper than you could ever dream of (literally). Have courage, my friend.

The first phase of the crisis is Purpose Seeking.

People in this phase have this yearning in them to seek, to the point where if they cannot find the answers, they are nearly driven mad. They want to know "What is the purpose of life?" Life can seem meaningless at some point. Some of these people then take up nihilistic or existentialistic views to cure this seeking.

Let me say this: Being nihilistic or existentialistic will not bring you out of this crisis. Instead, it will either make you more depressed or more ignorant. A deeper part of you yearns for more and more - But it will never be fulfilled. It takes courage to go through this phase.

You must understand that not grasping onto a view, and just letting it go - this is the antidote to this phase. It can be unimaginably painful for some who have held their vices too tightly. Let it go. Have courage. Believe me. Let the view go. You do not need to grasp on so tightly for the comfort. You are not going insane.

The second phase of the crisis is Hypersensitivity.

This phase is marked by intense emotion or sensations. Externally, people can break down to tears or become angry out of nowhere. People can suddenly exhibit manic type of emotions or even seem as if they are bi-polar. If this happened from meditation, then you need to realize what is happening here.

Meditation reveals things about you that you do not even know lies inside of you. It digs through your memories, your past and all the hidden traumas. Even things that you thought no longer mattered, will come up to face you.

This is also the phase where all kinds of emotions, often without any thought, just comes bursting out. Meditation is revealing all of these trapped emotions that you have been suppressing for ages.

You might also experience physical sensations, such as itching all over the body or a specific area. It can be intense pain, or dizziness, or even diseases erupting out. Does meditation cause these? No, it reveals them. Should you do meditation? Absolutely
yes. Can you even imagine the damage it can cause if these diseases accumulate?

The way out of this phase is to simply have courage. You must realize that these thoughts are no longer reality. Do not cling on to the past. Do not cling on to the emotions. Let them come forth. Know that when this phase is over, you will be free from them. People can cycle around this phase for a while.

The third phase of the crisis is Intense Phenomena.

People start to hear sounds - high pitched ringing. Some feel tingling all over their body, maybe even to the point of pain. Some feel dizziness or vertigo, to the point that you have to rely on a walking stick. You might start to feel movements inside your body, such as the chakras, meridians or chi junctions.

The fourth phase of the crisis is Intense Loneliness.

At this point of time, once you have gotten past all of the previous phases, you might come to this phase where you experience a tremendous loneliness. You feel as if you are the only person capable of understanding this experience. You look around and you do not find a teacher who is relating to your profound experience.

Believe me. It is not just you or me. There are hundreds, maybe even thousands of them out there who have had the same experience through meditation. You are not alone. Rejoice in that.

Once you are free of these, you start to have a nearly indescribable experience of freedom and liberation, as if you were realized from a dark cellar after the many years of your life. You lose addictions and feel energy throughout your day.

There are going to be a few spiritual teachers who are going to tell you, "You are mad." But here's the truth. There are two reasons why a teacher will respond in that manner:

(1) They have indeed good meditation experience, but did not need to handle the knots of their psyche. In other words, they have had good virtue throughout their life and never had repressed thoughts. This does not mean that they are unaccomplished. They might be, but because of not dealing with these things, they do not understand that it is also a phase of cultivation.

(2) They are frauds with shallow meditation experience. That is the only possible reason. One can meditate for 40 years but stay at a shallow level of wisdom if they are not meditating and contemplating rightly. A good teacher is one who have penetrated into high wisdom, not a person who has meditated for many years (although it can be related, they are often not the same).

So to answer the last question: "Why do we need discipline and virtue?"

This is because our lifestyle affects our subconscious mind. This is a manifestation of what we call karma. Whatever we say, we think or act, all of these become a part of our psyche. Guilt, disappointment, anger, sadness, pain, greed, desire - All of these become embedded in your psyche without you knowing it.

Of course you need discipline and virtue!

This is the only way to stop more of these "knots" from being stored in your psyche. Want a way to incorporate virtue? Look at all the religions. Christianity's deadly seven sins. Even Yoga has its moral code and description of unwholesome states. Buddhism has its infamous Noble Eightfold Path. Can you imagine if someone murders, steals or lies - What does this person have to face inside? It is
torture
beyond hell.

This is the solution. You are not going insane or mad.

Have courage.

This heart of yours is greater than you give it credit for.
