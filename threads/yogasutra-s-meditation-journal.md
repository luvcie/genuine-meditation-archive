  Ch'an/Zen masters engaged in contemplative practice diligently. Christian mystics prayed diligently. Yogi masters engaged in the eight limbs of yoga diligently. Even western occult masters engaged in their rituals diligently. Successful masters of any religion/rite have always demonstrated extraordinary diligence. Just as a good athlete spends many hours training on their sport, a spiritual master spends their whole life residing in one's spirituality.
