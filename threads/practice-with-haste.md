# Practice with haste

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Discussion` · 7 points · 2 years ago

Where is the Deathless?

What is the Deathless?

Find the Deathless and end your suffering.

Life is short in comparison

to the numerous lifetimes

that one has suffered.

When one finds no difference in wake or sleep

When one finds no difference in here or there

When one finds no difference in this or that

All suffering has ended

There is no more Death

There is no more Mara,

Mara has been shattered.

Having seen the genuine Dharma,

Why do you falter?

Why do you delay?

Why do you prize suffering over liberation?

Living the eightfold path is the way,

There is no other liberation.

Enlightenment is finding the Deathless

it is awakening to the light of the morning star

It is not existence as you think it is.

Why do you fumble?

Why do you not practice diligently?

Enlightened ones help the ones

who knock on the door of dharma

For those who do not practice

intensely, fervently, focused,

there is no helping.

There is no answer to one

who looks outwards

for the only answer is within

So seek within

and it will be the Deathless you find.

      When one feels that one can sit forever, this is usually in equanimity in the third jhana, along with the non-dualistic experience. Awareness that can move in all directions still seems to be a bit of extrasensory perception, possibly with the third-eye. However, if you can still feel your body, it is most likely not the fourth jhana yet. In the fourth, no sensations from the body are felt. You however, might have experienced certain parts of the etheric body, which explains why you feel like you can expand and contract.

      > His advice is to sit for lona hours with a strona determination.

      That is why the Buddha added that as part of the Noble Eightfold Path. Always sit like it is the last thing you will ever do, as if you will never get another chance to meditate again, and as if you will never need to sit again.
