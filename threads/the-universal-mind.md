# The Universal Mind

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 2016-05-30

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/4lptq7/the_universal_mind/>

There is a mind that exists, but yet there is individuality. Emptiness is form, form is emptiness - but this does not mean that there is no form, there is no emptiness. Form is still form, emptiness is still emptiness.

The entire collective unconscious of all sentient beings are like a quilt with its many stitches. Every single stitch is made by a different sentient being, telling its own story in the collective unconscious. You and I have different stories to tell. In your day today, you thought about all the little things, and all of those are stories. Your thoughts, actions and speech are stories. They are embedded in the collective unconscious.

When you forgive yourself, when you appreciate, when you treat yourself kindly and with care, then one treats oneself of poison. You weave a beautiful pattern in this collective unconscious.

Every stitch is interconnected with every other stitch in the quilt. The quilt may have various patterns - You see families, tribes, beliefs, traditions, religions, attitudes, names, forms, rituals, disciplines, rites, nationalities, groups, friends, etc - But they are all ultimately interconnected.

When you go into deeper meditative states, you access the intuitive stream and the wisdom of the collective unconscious reveals its gates to you. Every single story of existence plays its lullaby into you.

It then occurs to you, then, that one must live rightly. When one treats oneself rightly, as well as others, they contribute to a beautiful resonance in all sentient beings.

When one person talks to you, you might not understand it, but it is just like one cell in your every being communicating directly to you.

Understand that the Buddha awoke to interdependent origination. All things that are compounded and transient and they constantly shift. What matters is what you do right now.
