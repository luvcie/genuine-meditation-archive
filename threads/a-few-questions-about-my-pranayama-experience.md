# A few questions about my Pranayama experience

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **Neurogence** · *(OCR)* · `Question` · 2 points · 1 month ago

I've noticed some interesting effects from my pranayama practice that I'd like to discuss. I've been doing pranayama for 10-20 minutes at a time daily.

For one, I noticed that my digestion is a lot better now. It feels like my stomach is being unclogged and I feel a lot lighter and less bloated. This I count as a positive. Second, sometimes, when I go to sleep right after my pranayama session, I seem to fall into this very deep sleep and shortly wake up after one hour. I don't feel well rested from it, but in an odd way, subjectively it feels like I was asleep for over ten hours. Fortunately I am able to go right back to sleep when this happens, but I'm always a bit concerned since I don't know if this is a positive or negative effect.

Lastly, in the beginning, I used to inhale for about 5 seconds, retain the breath for 10 seconds, and exhale through my mouth for an additional 5 seconds. It was difficult at first but now it doesn't feel challenging at all. Is this enough for long-term, continued practice? I have experimented with increasing the duration I retain my breath by up to 30 seconds. Doing this, the effects I listed above are enhanced (such as the improved digestion). It's not too challenging for me. However, I am concerned this could be damaging in some ways. Sometimes afterward I feel a bit spaced out, and although I've noticed clear benefits to my digestive system, I don't feel that my practice is helping my brain to function better. And I am a little concerned that I could be hurting my brain without knowing it, even though I have read that some people practice one breath per minute pranayama sessions for long periods every day. Does pranayama affect the mind in any way beyond slowing down thoughts? I guess I have too many expectations. I was expecting it to brighten and deepen my sense of consciousness. But sometimes it feels the opposite.

I would like to hear your thoughts on this.
