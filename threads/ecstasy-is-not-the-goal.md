# Ecstasy is not the goal

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 6 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/39tpp8/ecstasy_is_not_the_goal/>

This subreddit does tend to use jhana as a way of landmarking one's progress, but this does not mean that if one attains jhana, then one becomes fully enlightened. It is an incentive, an aid and a window that allows some light of this enlightenment to shine through.

Jhana indicates "burning". What burns? Afflictions. Habits. One could say, in a non-technical way, that karma is being burned through the "cleansing power" of the jhanas.

The jhanas provide a state for one to experience ecstasy. This ecstasy can continue even as one continues to live their own life.

But what is this purpose? The Buddha never taught that enlightenment was ecstasy. Instead, he said that it was freedom from suffering.

What suffering? There are only two kinds of suffering. We only have bodily/physical suffering and mental suffering, haven't we? When we take birth as a result of mental suffering from ignorance, it is only when we have assumed a body that we experience suffering through the six senses.

How does one say, "Birth has ended, suffering is no more"?

In meditation, the space between the physical senses and mind start to become larger. Eventually, the mind no longer senses the body. The factors of enlightenment become stronger. Eventually, they culminate and that is full enlightenment.

When there is no more suffering, it means that ignorance - giving rise to mental suffering and hence physical suffering, has ended.

Ecstasy is an incentive. It is a signpost. It tells you that yes, you are cultivating the jhanas. Just like how you would feel the heat of a bonfire increase as you walk nearer to it, ecstasy is like that.

It makes you happy, does it not? Making you happy, you tend to live life happier. You lead a life with a wholesome state, do you not? You talk kindly, think kindly and behave kindly, do you not?

The contemplative lifestyle aids liberation, but liberation also inevitably leads to the contemplative lifestyle. This contemplative lifestyle is the Noble Eightfold Path.

I wrote this post because there were some messages that seemed to have focused too much on the esoteric things such as jhana, chi, manipulation of channels - All of these are inconsequential in light of the real dharma.
