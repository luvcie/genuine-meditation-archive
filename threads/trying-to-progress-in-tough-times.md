It took me at least a few months before I could get to even half-lotus for ~30 minutes. THEN I allowed myself a pillow (earlier thought it was shameful to do so) and then another. My back may hurt now but legs rarely do in a 30 minute sit. At least in the beginning you have to be a bit stubborn and push yourself. The boundary is further than you believe.

          Also, sitting perfectly erect is important. Slouching forward puts more pressure on legs. Over time your body gets used to it, you know in general what leg positions(subtle variations) will work, and also intuitively know whether you're gonna feel pain later with this arrangement. When a meditation session was good (even in the beginning) it wasn't as if the pain would be disappear, but the session would end, I'd come out and be like "Yep, the legs hurt like hell”. You don't stop meditating just because your liver is working or your heart is pumping blood, physical pain is just something arising and falling. You're stronger than that. You're not that. Naturally the rest of the stuff comes first. What I just said applies when you're already sitting on the cushion.
