# Inconstancy of Experience in Jhanas: Bouncing and Going Back Up

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2u1mue/inconstancy_of_experience_in_jhanas_bouncing_and/>

I've received a few messages on how they've bounced up and down in jhana stages and they were asking: Is this possible? This post will address that question.

Yes, it is possible for one to experience an inconstancy of experience, especially when you have not reached the fourth jhana of equanimity. This is all related to the various degrees of grasping.

For example, you might find that you have a certain stillness of mind, bringing you to tranquility in the second Jhana. That stillness of mind may be still at that point of time, but the mad conditioning kicks in and a thought surfaces out of nowhere. This brings you back down to the first Jhana.

This cycling or unsteadiness can continue for many sessions. This is why one of the masteries of the jhana is to be able to sustain the state and factors of that particular jhana. This also comes from more wisdom, experience and practice.

It is quite impossible, in my personal experience, to remain in a single Jhana when you are new to it. For example, people might move into first Jhana and then skip right out when all the thoughts start to come back in.

Always remember the principle of simply going back to it again and again. Jhana is so much more blissful than your daily life, what is there to fret about? Don't be afraid, fade away the thoughts, be.

Ever heard of the Christian term of "Surrendering to God"? That's what it is. Letting go of the thoughts. The thoughts may come back but you should not be an inhospitable host. Let them stay, pay no attention to them and they will leave. Do not focus on them, just anchor your awareness on what matters. The factors of the jhana, the meditation object, etc.

Don't be fretful or discouraged. In fact, every time you seem to drop into the lower states, it gets much easier to get back up.

For example, you might not feel as much joy/bliss as you did the first time you reached first/second jhana if you fall from third/fourth. But this is a good thing as your wisdom has already understood that pitii itself is gross as compared to the equanimity of the third/fourth.

When you finally reach fifth samadhi and beyond, there is no longer any yo-yoing. In fact, many people in the fourth jhana are already engaged in the fifth samadhi.

Strive well.
