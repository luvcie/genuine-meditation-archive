- **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 4 points · 1 year ago

  All things a human do - it is either consciously or unconsciously. And you can choose whether to do it consciously or unconsciously.

  When you say ‘others made me this way' or 'I had no choice', you are giving away your choice and making it an unconscious process. Now see this as the case - When you raise food to your mouth to eat, you can choose whether to take that food, isn't it so? So why is it that when you want to watch shows for thrill, you cannot choose?

  It is in fact a choice to not choose.

  Now see this, especially as a human clothed in an animal body, you have the innate ability of being human. And being human is to be able to switch between consciousness and impulse. Take the ability to choose away, and you have become no different from a lower spirit. Even animals can choose whether they want to eat food or not.

  The first thing you must claim here is that you are in fact doing everything consciously. You are not being driven by impulse. Impulse is something you create in your mind as a secondary reason. You always have a choice, but it is your own choice that allows you to go along with it.

  As your life energies become more exuberant, it is natural that this energy goes to all aspects of your life, including the energies of reproduction. So it is also no surprise that a healthy, fertile male like yourself has sexual drive.

  But do not say: "I have no choice, impulses made me."

  If you choose to indulge in sexual lust, then you must be conscious that you have chosen to. This, in a certain way, is also part of the five precepts - abstaining from lying.

  If you constantly practice mindfulness, being conscious of everything that you do, then you are always in the power of choice. However, if you are unconscious, then you have no choice. You then become like a person who dreams but

  cannot control his dreams.

  So your mistake is not in having your energies flow in that manner, but what you do with it. Consciousness, or unconsciousness - mindfulness, or no-mindfulness - that is up to you.

  Mindfulness as proclaimed by the Buddha, is a weapon of the mind that can be used at any time. Cultivation is this exact mindfulness in every aspect of your life. To be more conscious, or to be less conscious like a stone? That is your choice.
