> There is not one but two people in side of me. What I mean to say is that, there is one voice that keeps chattering, laughing, judging, becoming angry, and there is one center that is absolutely calm, hidden in the background, just smiling at everything.

There is no one there but many, many "voices". It is important that you realize that there is no persona in the center. There is calm - there is nothing - and there is just a luminous awareness that shines on things and interconnects everything. Even when you do realize that you are one with everything, you must understand that you are every sentient being, every sentient being is you.

The fact that you can recognize that there is a silence - this silence is not it either. It is simply a gap between the many voices that you hear. What this is, is the absence. It is not nothing, it is not something. Something is the same as nothing, nothing is the same as something.

When you turn your eyes to look at the space between two walls, what is it that you see? You see nothing, but this nothing has a "space" to it. Similarly, in what you perceive right now, your space is still premature and conceptual. Turn into the source of perception and find out what is it that sees both something and nothing as equal.

Your vitality has risen. With the rising of yang energy, it is usually the case for a male's sexual drive to increase. In such a case, one should continuously turn away from the burning sensual desires and pay attention to the equanimity that can be found within. As the metal glows red, the blacksmith plunges the metal again and again into the coolant. However, the metal must glow before it can be changed - in the same way, your mind'’s afflictions will be burnt away from the fire of the jhanas.
