# The vital importance of not being attached to form

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 5 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3f4ckl/the_vital_importance_of_not_being_attached_to_form/>

Firstly, you create your own wisdom barriers from habits. There are people who are able to attain samadhi, but as a result of that are impeding their own progress into having real enlightenment.

In fact, I do not generally prefer that people use esoteric methods in meditation because there is a huge tendency for a person to depend on them. That is why I refrain from teaching or showing these techniques.

Hence, do not depend on any particular method. Instead, depend on your insight. There are many ways to meditate and you can go as deep if you have the right insight.

Do not be attached to the form aggregate. Do not be overly attached to the concepts of jing-chi-shen. Do not be overly attached to chakras. Do not be attached to anything that is phenomenal, not even jhana-nimittas. Let them take their course, but you should never be restricting yourself by clinging on to them.

Remember that you want enlightenment, not simply a transformation of the body. If you remain attached, not even the greatest sage can help you with it.

      Yes, when you find yourself unable to grasp - that itself, is a subtle grasping. Without dwelling in any kind of concept or mental realm, one moves along with nature. The astral body is not the end-all as it is alas, still within the realm of form.

      The five aggregates are: physical body/matter, sensuality, perception, mental structures and cognition. It is only through purification/relinquishment of these things can one see clearly.

      The higher fetters include:

      1. Craving for material existence

      2. Craving for immaterial existence

      3. Conceit

      4, Restlessness

      5. Ignorance

      When one is enlightened, the factors culminate in the following through the grounding of the traits of the four form-jhana states: Wisdom, Viriya/kundalini, Tranquility, Mindfulness/sati, Equanimity, piti (bliss), jhana absorption.

      One who masters the fourth rupa-jhana (form jhana) allows for renunciation-pleasure, which is to be pursued. One then proceeds to relinquish the lower and higher fetters.

      Perhaps the 'spark’ that you lost is devoid of energy. Examine what you lack in the seven factors and work into a stable form-jhana state as the basis.
