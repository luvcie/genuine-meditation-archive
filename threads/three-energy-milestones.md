# Three Energy Milestones

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Discussion` · 4 points · 2015-12-03

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/3vb5dk/three_energy_milestones/>

The first milestone is the opening of the heart. The solar plexus drives the current to the heart chakra. They are linked. If there is a blockage here, it is driven by the theme of selfishness - ambition - selfish desire. The way to remove this blockage is to practice generosity, gratefulness, unselfishness, charity, surrender - any form of letting go that allows the heart to bare itself and open up.

The second milestone is the opening of the throat. The sacral center drives current to the throat. If you are indulging in sexual pleasure, there is hardly any energy to fuel the throat and creativity can fall. The throat is an expression of the creativity of the soul. Artists, musicians and all kinds of expressions come from the throat. Pain - from relationships - from emotional traumas - from sexual-repression - blocks this connection. Therefore, one needs to master relationships and to master non-attachment.

The third milestone is the opening of the crown. The root fuels the crown. All forms of health deterioration or ageing - making the body rigid or chocked with health problems - are usually to do with the blockages in the root. The fear of death is what blocks this connection. One must contemplate death - accept it fully as the body's inevitable fate - the impermanent nature of the body and all forms. One must ground oneself properly, by sitting in lotus or half-lotus. One must also connect to the higher self and saturate with bliss of the jhanas.

The Buddha talked about three marks of existence:

1. impermanence (anicca)
2. dissatisfaction or suffering (dukkha)
3. non-self (anattā)

Contemplating on these three, one correctly loosens fears, traumas and attachments.

  The dharma, the Deathless does not disappear. It does not rise or fall. It is present everywhere. Having just that, you seek solace in that, you seek refuge in the dharma.

  So bare your heart. Release all trauma. Do not run away from them. Face them. Investigate its root. Get rid of them once and for all. One lifetime. Two lifetimes. Countless lifetimes. Burn the afflictions away. Ride the bliss and it will heal you.
