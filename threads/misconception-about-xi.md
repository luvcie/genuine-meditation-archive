# Misconception about Xi

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 8 points · 1 year ago

Someone recently wrote to me about having attained Xi and presented a bad misconception, so I decided to do a thread so everyone can be alerted to it.

The Xi, the breath cessation that I speak about, is very different from the slight pause between the inhalation and exhalation.

If you intentionally force the breath to stop after inhalation, it becomes a fire-based practice like tummo, forcing heat to rise through the body. If done intentionally, it becomes pranayama, as shown by the nine-wind practice here. If done improperly, it can lead to oxygen deficiency and nervous tension.

Instead, the Xi is actually more of what I call Tai Xi (Fetal Xi). This breath cessation is spontaneous and is known as the "little death". This usually comes after a very subtle outer breath known as Qi, where you no longer notice yourself breathing.

The key misinterpretation I am pointing out here is that you should not be forcing your breath to stop, but instead letting the body's physiology relax until the state where you no longer perceive the breath - and then it spontaneously stops, giving rising to a blissful experience where you no longer feel your body.

As a result of this misinterpretation, I find it important to discuss the physiological changes during deep meditation to extend the description of what I written in the Anapanasati series.

There are many kinds of physiological things that you can see when you meditate, and none of these should be regarded as any form of achievement. They are just passing phenomena that barely even scratch the surface of real cultivation.

One possible effect is the vibration of legs, or shaking. One person has written to me about this. What happens is that the energy channels in the feet are connected to the kidneys. Usually, it is the body's way of empowering the kidneys and allowing the pineal and pituitary gland to communicate with each other. There are a lot of secrets in legs, such as in martial arts, but I will not discuss them further. Simply put, this is all purification of mind, qi and energy pathways, opening up stuck or trapped energy in botht he energetic and physical body. Some might not even experience this, depending on their relative purity.

Another is body temperature fluctuation. You might feel some organs become hot, or cool, magnetic energy flows. These are all actions of purification. If you have ever touched a charger that has a high electrical resistance, you might realize that it can become hot. You might feel all kinds of energy move, especially through the spine and front.

Another is the feeling of joy or grief. You may see coloured lights. You may taste, smell, etc. One person here reported that he spoke in tongues, another had a psychic power awaken. These are all simply 'memories' that you have carried from a past life, and has been stored within your DNA.

Another common one is spontaneous movement, such as going into mudras, positions, or being pulled onto the ground by an unseen force. This is not done by an entity. As you meditate deeply, your electro-magnetic field increases - What is happening is that the field is trying to complete a loop with your surroundings.

Yet another is the opening of the celestial or deva eye, allowing people to see all kinds of colours in the forehead. Usually, one experiences pressure in this area - The reason is because blood is being trapped in that area of the head. The solution to this is to draw blood immediately away from that pressure, such as by rubbing your ears. Your cranial pressure increases from the cerebrospinal fluid trying to push out the skull-bones.

Yet another is what we call ambrosia. Sweet honey-like fluid spills from the back of the upper palate - thick, smooth, rose-like-fragrant. Should this happen, one should swallow it with a loud gulp and guide it mentally into the navel. This is all secreted because of the opening of a certain suture in the skull-bones.

As the mind starts to be exposed to the archetypes, one might see snakes or nagas (snake beings). This is simply how the mind tries to interpret these energies.

The lower chakras normally feel intense i sensation and energy refines itself in levels as it ascends. So rest your mind in the heart when you feel indescribable bliss. Then you should focus on stillness - internal light appears behind the eyes, turning bliss into emptiness.

When the 'head mind' drops into the head-center, you will see the "bright light of the heart", also known to near-death experiencers as the 'tunnel of light'. So all of these are nothing unusual, do not get caught up in them. They are really unimportant, just let go of them.

Now eventually, you then reach the 'little death'. Of course, you might not reach all of the above, but it is normally the case where people go through purification processes. Please look at the Anapansati thread to see all the various stages.

All of this phenomena, including the jhana-nimittas, such lights-flashing, sounds (music of the spheres), etc - are simply ways of the body trying to interpret all of these energy-movements. You may laugh or burst out in tears for no reason. But always let go of them and do not cling. You are simply reverting into a normal human being.

Bliss can be addictive. The true purpose of the dharma however, is to release the ego and desire, balancing the spiritual and material life together, flowing with the Tao.
