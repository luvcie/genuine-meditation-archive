# pg's meditation journal

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **philosophyguru** · *(OCR)* · `Journal` · 5 points · 2 years ago

Over the last few days, I have struggled with anger and impatience in my personal life and those experiences bled into my meditation practice with ill will and restlessness. Today I found resolution to some of those issues and the difference in the quality of my meditation was unmistakable. I was much better able to maintain my concentration on the breath and was not pulled away by sounds. My thoughts were substantially less noisy, and when I found my attention wandering it was much easier to return to the breath. Finally, I did not experience the almost painful impatience and restlessness of the last days' sessions, and in fact managed to relax deeper into the meditation for a few minutes after the concluding chime sounded.

The predominant quality of the meditative experience was heaviness: the body felt more weighty and it seemed like my attention itself had an intensity, a heaviness that had its own pull. The intensity of my awareness was not so strong as to predominate and exclude other sensation, but it felt like a difference in kind more than a difference I degree compared to the quality of my habitual consciousness.
