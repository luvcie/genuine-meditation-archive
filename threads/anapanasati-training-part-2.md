# Anapanasati Training (Part 2)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 10 points · 1 year ago

(3) Know that the breath is long, know that the breath is short.

This might seem easy, but let me ask you. How do you know whether something is long or short? It is relative. A bigger person will require more air, while a smaller person will require lesser air. Does this mean that a breath in a bigger person is longer than the breath in a smaller person?

The way to cultivate this third step, is to firstly make sure that you have mastered the first two steps.

Now, if you are beginning, you want to start at the nostrils. Only when you improve, then you will naturally know that the entire body's 18,000 pores are breathing. Even better, you will know that every single cell in your body is breathing.

Sometimes the inhale and exhale are both long.

Sometimes the exhale is long, there is a pause, then the inhale is short.

Sometimes the inhale naturally continues until the breath fills you to the soles of your feet, then even more to your toes, until your entire body is "full".

Take note that the focus of this 3rd statement is not on the breath, but the "knowing nature". This knowing is not found in your brain. This knowing is universally found in every single cell in your body.

Don't try to control your breathing, breathe naturally. Only let the "knowing nature" look at the breath. When thoughts come, don't care about them - reside in the "knowing nature".

Slowly, your breath becomes deeper and longer. Eventually, you are filled with breath and your entire body becomes upright and erect. You feel as if you cannot inhale or exhale - this is the real, authentic state of "Xi".

Do not think this is hard. If you take just half a day, you can accomplish this.

When this happens, your thoughts become single-pointed. Your body and mind immediately change. Your mind won't be dizzy and sluggish and you find it easy to concentrate.

When your thoughts stop, your breath stops. Does your breath still move? Of course it does. Take your knowing of the breath's length another step further.

In this state, it might be a very long time before you notice a movement of breath.

Your exhale might be very long, then it pauses, and then there is a very short inhale that is enough to "fill you completely" with chi.

You exhale might be short, or your inhale might be very deep, filling your body and making you lose hunger.

Hence, you should practice this way.
