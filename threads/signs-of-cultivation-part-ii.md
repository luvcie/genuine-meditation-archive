# Signs of Cultivation (Part II)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 3 points · 2 years ago

IMPORTANT: Please note that while these things are being explained, do not be mistaken to think that cultivating the body or the supernormal powers is the Path.

The reason why cultivation is essential is all just part of helping one to be able to have your mind perfectly clear. When the mind is clear and purified, it can then perceive wisdom and reach realization of enlightenment.

Expelling Yin gi (or unwholesome energy)

Yin qi makes you feel sick and is saturated in places like battlefields, graveyards and hospitals. Ghosts are composed of yin gi, and it represents evil, poison, sickness and death.

This impurity is normally what obstructs the energy channels and when "cleansing”, this energy looks like snakes or poisonous worms being squeezed out of pores. People with an opened "third eye" can see this impurity being pushed out. You may smell toxins or foul-smelling odors when your body undergoes this detoxification and cleansing.

The hardest parts to purify are normally the leg channels leading to the foot chakras, the heart chakra and throat chakra. Do not try to engage in visualization practices where you imagine circulation. That does not work, it only works when you have a single focal point of the mind.

The Heart chakra can be opened by diligently using a prayer at the heart level or the Cundi mantra while visualizing a bright flame or a mandala behind the breastbone/sternum. When it opens, there is often a sudden "pop" or shift in energy. The solidified, dirty qi spills out of the chakras and you can see different colors or textures, as well as identify the earth, wind, fire and earth qi.

By expelling these impurities, you come to the BASIC level of finally being able to understand some of the teachings that sages have taught.

Five Koshas/Sheaths

In Hinduism, there are five sheaths, and these correspond to Buddhism, where there are five aggregates that must be purified for one to gain liberation.

(1) Annamaya kosha = Form Skandha / Physical Body

This is physical matter, such as our physical body. This is known as the form aggregate/skandha. Hence, it is important to cultivate the five elements of the body.

(2) Pranamaya kosha = Sensation Skandha

This is the said etheric body that comprises feelings, emotions and sensations. In Taoism, this is normally referred to as "jing".

(3) Manakaya kosha = Conception Skandha

This still has thoughts and conceptions but do not involve the coarser thoughts and mental streams in the Annamaya or Pranamaya kosha. It is still made of qi, but less dense than the previous. This is also called the "mental body" in Hinduism.

(4) Vilnanamaya kosha = Volition Skandha

This involves the karmic forces, such as mental habits, thoughts, ideas, compulsions, prejudices, views, decisions, etc that lead to volition.

(5) Anadamaya kosha = Consciousness Skandha

This is pure awareness which supports all experiences and other koshas.

When one becomes an Arhat, one is able to transform the dense physical body in the Annamaya kosha into pure chi so that it can materialize and dematerialize at will. This comes from cultivation and mastery of the elements of the body.

Who Are You?

The mind is a group of thoughts surrounding an ego-consciousness. When you are able to find the source of this "I" or ego, then you can find out where everything is merged within original nature. This ego-consciousness is known as the 7th consciousness.

The physical body is certainly not your true self. If you are consciousness itself, you cannot possibly witness consciousness. Cut all attachments to both the mind and the body. You are not the physical nor the etheric body. There are no attributes to the real self.

In actuality, there is no form, matter, gi, consciousness, five skandhas, sensations, etc - All are an illusion from the true nature. When one is enlightened, one does not gain anything.

Calm your mind by letting go. Watch your thoughts and notice the interval between them. Inquire: "Who is experiencing this silence?"

Consciousness is natural and always rises, so you should never try to suppress it. You cannot stop consciousness either. Instead, you should detach from consciousness and find its origin.

Mantra and watching your thoughts are two of the most successful techniques throughout time. Avoid becoming attached to the physical nature and its cultivation.

Firstly, one must realize the dharmakaya original essence body, or the true self. Secondly, one must purify the skandhas to attain the sambhogakaya reward body. Third, one can then emanate countless nirmanakaya projection bodies for other sentient beings.
