# Recognition of the Fundamental Mind

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 8 points · 6 months ago

What is the source of your intention? What is the source of your speech? What is the source of your actions? From where does this arise from, and what empowers it?

When an action arises from within this source, you see the birth of the action, the ageing of the action, and the death of the action. This is the same for words and intention.

Energy is cast outwards and is eventually converted into other forms. This body itself is an emanation - and just like any apparition originating from Mind, it is an energy that arises, decays and passes away.

Throughout this process of energy being cast outwards, the original spirit, the Source, has always been there. This source is not a self, ego, personality or being, however, all of the world's ten thousand things arise from it.

To retain this energy and stop it from being cast outwards, one starts to preserve vitality. Preserving this, one does not give birth to Yin and Yang, and are outside the range of the triple realms. However, the only way to do this is to recognise the essence. This is what Chan Buddhism means by 'seeing the original face'.

This body-vehicle that you inherit is generated from inwards.

The spirit resides in the eyes in waking life and sinks to the liver in the night when it dreams. From this, it roams the various worlds during dreaming. When waking up, if one is drowsy and dull, one is clinging onto the body, this is the sign of an overpowered ‘lower soul' - in simpler terms, what is responsible for causing transmigration over multiple lifetimes.

By ceasing the outward-emission of the spirit light, this is called 'turning the light around’. Some masters will give you the direct instruction of 'seeing backwards' into the original cavity of spirit, but the more accurate understanding is the cessation of outward-leakage of the light.

By dissolving the 'lower soul’ - the 'higher soul' is made complete. Persisting in this, vitality will rise - spirit will ignite like fire - awareness will stabilise.

All lust is generated from the 'lower soul' - it looks towards death. By transforming the 'lower soul', one illuminates it completely.

Initially, one will think - it is as if there is non-existence in existence. Gradually, one will think - there is existence within non-existence.

When the light is kept within for a hundred days with no leakage, the spark of spirit arises - the Yin shines forth with brilliant Yang.

This pattern is seen throughout the entire Universe. The light of the world is firstly the Sun and the moon reflecting it. This is why the Sun is called the Sol - for it is derived from the word "Soul”. For the human being is the ‘eyes' which shines, for they are windows to the soul. This is turning around the manifesting energy of the Universe - by doing so, one ends compulsions. This light is not inside nor outside nor in-between the self. All things of the world are of this light.

By 'stopping’ the outward movement of the light, one 'turns around’. By 'seeing’ internally, one 'sees light'.

“Place the mind on one point, and everything will be done."

When the spirit is anxious and overactive, this is an over-active mind. When the spirit is steeped with torpor and sluggishness, going into oblivion, the spirit is muddled.

To fix anxiousness, one sees the direction of the mind and absorbs onto a single point.

However, to fix torpor that falls into oblivion is difficult, because the 'lower soul' has taken full control. The only way to destroy this is to tune the breath - just like how one tunes a stringed instrument.

It is undoubtedly the case where one in a sluggish mind has an extremely unrefined breath - where breath is coarse and heavy. If you can hear it with your ears, this is too rough. One will need to either do pranayama, or to reduce it to soundless breathing through calming down.

The eyes turn inwards - turning the light around. The ears turn inwards - turning the sound around. One witnesses the soundless and the formless.

Do not sit in indifference for this is a false voidness. Do not anticipate, for this races the mind. Do not tighten the body and scalp, for this stresses the breathing mechanism. Do not fall into illusions and hallucinations, for you have engaged the shadow mind - doing this will turn you into a thoughtless rock.

How does one know if one is turning the light around correctly?

As if one is taking an internal bath, there is joy, delight, satisfaction and gratitude. This is lightness. Harmony pervades the body.

When the fruit is ripe, the entire earth blooms into pure light. This is the 'substance' of mind that is luminous.

When the entire body is filled sufficiently, the red blood turns to milk-like. If one is to pierce the skin, it will exude a white paste. The physical body shines like jewels. One will not fear frost or wind.

In everyday matters, one should still tend to "turning this light inwards'. Act with purpose but without striving. Non-striving disengages forms and images - Acting with purpose prevents one from falling into dead-tree Zen, a withered emptiness. If you do this successfully for three months, there will be visitors from higher realms to prove your experience.

When gazing inward - one eventually forgets the gazing. One cannot even find one's body.

One asks - why have I not risen the Yang, the kundalini, the shakti, the divine even after years of seeking? This is because you have not consistently turned the light around for at least a hundred days without leakage.

Now this is a secret that I have not released - a hundred days does not mean a hundred days. A hundred days in the Brahma realms is equivalent to years of human-time. A hundred days can also be within one single breath. This is dependent on your merit and insight - and grace will fall upon you - a flower blossoms in its own time and place - you can only water the plant.
