# What is Kundalini and Energy?

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 5 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2uhl0j/what_is_kundalini_and_energy/>

Kundalini came from the word "Viriya" in the Pali Canon. Kundalini, in fact, means "coiled", normally represented by a snake.

Some of the symptoms of this Kundalini being risen is that of:

• Frozen in posture
• Spine elongated

• Intense pain if you resist it, almost as if you are electrocuted

• The aggressiveness of Kundalini fully opens up when in the fourth jhana, which is required to open the access to the fifth samadhi and beyond.

Just ride it out and it will get blissful. When you fight it, it becomes very unpleasant.

Also, experiencing Kundalini is where your psyche will be put to the ultimate test. Those who are unworthy will have their inner secrets, suppressions, repressions forced out and you will have to deal with them in a very ugly and rather rude manner. This is also known as the fourth stage of the Spiritual Crisis (see the subreddit).

All of us have some form of neurosis when we first start out. Although it might not seem so, meditate to this point and you will see the deep layers of your psyche - things that you never dreamed that you would be reminiscing about, griefing about, thinking about. This is why you MUST have a proper lifestyle that is catered towards merit.

However, it is not psychosis. If you are fixated on a thought, then yes, you will develop neurosis. The key to neurosis is being preoccupied with thoughts. If you let them all go, when you are devoid of a self, what can affect you? What psychosis is there?

Do not ever perform the heinous crimes. Shun immorality like you would shun things that make you extremely uncomfortable. Live life honestly, morally, upright and without regret. Follow the Noble Eightfold Path.

People think: "Oh, I do the act and nobody gets hurt. No one knows about it. Only I do." Exactly. Heaven and earth might not know what you have done, but you know it. Your subconscious does not forget that easily. Once the deed is done, you need to claim responsibility for what you have sowed. There is no running away. Nature is merciless, yet merciful.

This is exactly what people face at the point of death, where you judge yourself for your past actions - This is also known as reproductive karma. Your life flashes before your eyes in a time dilated space and you review everything that you have done in this lifetime.

Here, I quote the Buddha's own personal experience of going through his spiritual crisis, from the Maha-saccaka Sutta (mn 36):

"I thought: 'Suppose I were to become absorbed in the trance of non-breathing.' So I stopped the in-breaths & out-breaths in my nose & mouth. As I did so, there was a loud roaring of winds coming out my earholes, just like the loud roar of winds coming out of a smith's bellows... So I stopped the in-breaths & out-breaths in my nose & mouth & ears. As I did so, extreme forces sliced through my head, just as if a strong man were slicing my head open with a sharp sword... Extreme pains arose in my head, just as if a strong man were tightening a turban made of tough leather straps around my head... Extreme forces carved up my stomach cavity, just as if a butcher or his apprentice were to carve up the stomach cavity of an ox... There was an extreme burning in my body, just as if two strong men, grabbing a weaker man by the arms, were to roast & broil him over a pit of hot embers. And although tireless persistence was aroused in me, and unmuddled mindfulness established, my body was aroused & uncalm because of the painful exertion. But the painful feeling that arose in this way did not invade my mind or remain.

Do not be mistaken that Kundalini is simply energy. It is virtuous energy. It is the virtuous power that bestows life and takes it away. Its tame form are likened to that of the "chi channels" of the body, but it is definitely not the same as simple energy.

May peace take you to Dharma.
