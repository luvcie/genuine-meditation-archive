# Fruits (Phala) from Practice (Maga)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 8 months ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2v9u7x/fruits_phala_from_practice_maga/>

Some translations talk about phala-maga, as if fruits and practice are one thing - Please note that this is an erroneous translation of the sutta. The Buddha has always preached that the fruits will only be attained through practice.

What are these fruits? They are attainments. When looking at a person and determining whether one is enlightened or not, look for the fruits of attainment. You will know a tree by its fruit.

Firstly, there is no such thing as "two" paths (erroneously as "vipassana/insight meditation" or "absorption meditation). In the Dhammapada, it is stated as such in verse 372:

No jhana exists without wisdom,
No wisdom exists without jhana.

Whoever is close to enlightenment
Has both jhana and wisdom.

This is why I must stress that there is no vipassana or absorption meditation. It is only through insights that absorption deepens, and through absorption that insights are reaped. Jhana and wisdom comes hand in hand.

Hence from right absorption comes right samadhi, which also reaps the supramundane wisdom and even siddhis. The fruits that come are as shown:

1. Equanimity
2. No Fear
3. No Pain or Discomfort
4. Jhana

5. Mind-generated body (astral body/subtle body/inner yin body)

6. Clairaudience

7. Telepathy - Knowing of the mind state of others

8. Recollection of past lives

9. Clairvoyance - Being able to see the passing away or rebirth of beings

10. No mental agitation
11. Knowledge and Vision (Insight stream)

These supramundane qualities are indicative of a person who has high wisdom, leading to these fruits. The easiest to tell is through equanimity. Is this person free of addictive, angry or hedonistic behaviour? If one has one of these fruits, does this person have the rest? These are all described in various suttas by the Buddha himself.
