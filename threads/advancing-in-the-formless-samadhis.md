# Advancing in the Formless Samadhis

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advanced` · 4 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2vqq0z/advancing_in_the_formless_samadhis/>

Note: It is entirely possible to experience any of these domains of mind when you are in the form jhanas. Hence, this does not mean that you have mastered the four form jhanas, which are very important in liberation.

The proper sequence should be to master all four form jhanas as a foundation, then master the four formless jhanas. If you have not attained any of the first four, do not dabble in any of these and just work on the first four form jhanas.

(1) Arupa-Jhana of Infinite Space
Method to get to this:

1. Having reached fourth jhana, contemplate the body as "space", as if it were an empty cage or bag.

2. If done successfully, the whole of the form realm will seem as if it were infinite space.

Problems with this jhana:

1. Within this physical emptiness, there are still elements that exist within this space. For example, in space, if you were to take a sample of it, you would see elements taking birth and passing away.

2. You still have the consciousness to perceive space, no matter how infinite it can seem to be.

(2) Arupa-Jhana of Infinite Consciousness
Method to get to this:

1. Contemplate that space cannot exist within consciousness. Consciousness can't perceive itself as "spacious", it does not make sense.

Problems with this jhana:

1. Consciousness is existing to discriminate the fact that there is infinite consciousness.

2. Although you have literally no sense of the form realm, you are still within the mental nuances.

(3) Arupa-Jhana of Infinite Nothingness
Method to get to this:

1. Masters do not generally like going into this state, like the Buddha.

2. It is a subtle suppression, of pushing away.

Problems with this jhana:

1. There is very subtle effort or suppression of discriminations that is temporary. This state is unstable.

2. Do not confuse this with "no-thoughts" (No-thought samadhi is dangerous, it entrains the mind to become like a thoughtless animal).

3. Nothingness is still not emptiness. It is still a nuanced creation of the mind.

(4) Arupa-Jhana of Neither Thought nor No-thought
Method to get to this:

1. Contemplate that space cannot exist within consciousness. Consciousness can't perceive itself as "spacious", it does not make sense. Problems with this jhana:

2. Although false thoughts have stopped, there is still a very, very, very subtle perception/discriminatory ability existing.

3. In this Jhana, you are somewhat in between the duality of "infinite space" and "infinite consciousness". You can be aware of things, but yet no thoughts exist. Still, this is not liberation.

(5) Arahatta's Nirvana (Nirvana with remainder)

In this state, the Arahatta has successfully transformed the physical body so that miracles can be performed directly. In such a case, what the astral body can do, the nirmanakaya can do.

They are able to manipulate the elements of this reward body, such as fire. So if they know that their physical body is going to die, they may choose to burn their bodies by manipulating the fire element. The only reason for doing so is to encourage faith in the dharma.

However, the problem with the Arahatta's Nirvana is that although they are freed, they still come back after 84 thousand aeons. Arahatta only focus on the "empty" side of enlightenment with such intensity that they disregard the side of "existence".

This very clinging is the "remainder". But this is such a high accomplishment that many practitioners (even self-proclaimed enlightened ones) cannot even reach. Being an Arahatta is a very huge accomplishment, birthed from a lot of merit.

(6) Bodhisattva's / Buddha's Nirvana

A Buddha acknowledges both the existence and emptiness of reality. This Middle Way removes all form of clinging - to emptiness or to existence.

This is the reason why Bodhisattvas continue to come back to the Three Realms, to continue transmitting wisdom and teachings to the deluded beings. Although they are completely free of rebirth and death, they may make a choice to be reincarnated for this purpose of teaching, out of pure compassion.

Liberation is not easy. It requires effort, not just effort, but extraordinary effort. It takes huge time, huge experience. Huge is an underestimation. It must be extraordinary.

Hope this helps some of the advanced practitioners.
