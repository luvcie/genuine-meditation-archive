  While it is so, a being who has never seen one's own nature will continue in ignorance, taking birth continuously without fail.

  When a rope is heavily tangled, you may not see the existence of the rope. But when the rope becomes more untangled, one may see the rope, and realize that one has never been separate from the rope.

  Firstly, you must see that there is a path. This is the third Noble Truth.

  Then, you awaken to the path.

  Lastly, you realize that there is nothing attained, simply because you can only laugh about the Tac and directly experience it - and there is no entity to receive any Tao.
