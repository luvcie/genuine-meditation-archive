# Understanding Worries

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **elnoxvie** · *(OCR)* · `Discussion` · 7 points · 2 years ago

Worries is a kind of diseases. It starts out of nowhere (The nowhere here refers to the worries that just randomly pop ups when things happened or when the mind made up of some stories. although i said nowhere, but it's actually from complex programming of mind obviously), taking our mind from reality and pushing us to insanity. What good is worrying, When the only certainty is uncertainty itself ?

One has to understand the nature of certainty of uncertainty to go beyond.

Unfortunately, the normal mind is weak to comprehend this (Only In Jhana, the mind is strong enough to make this understanding permanent). Therefore unable to let go immediately.

If that is the case than one has to examine the worries, experiment with it.

1. Out of 100 worries, how many is true? 2. Out of 100 worries, how many time have I wasted to speculate the outcome which is just another uncertainty?

Hopefully the next time worries arrived, one has already discerned the nature of worrying and go beyond the reached of worries. (One programmed one self to ignore worries by understanding the nature of worries is actually uncertainty itself).

And this is what one should built to live a worry less life.

Sometimes, i get some ideas or thoughts so i will just post it here to seek critics, advise or discussion and do hope what helps me, helps the other fellow dharma seekers.

  Just to add an excerpt from a sutta to fuel this topic:

  > Upaadaaparitassanaa Sutta (SN 22.7) At Saavatthii the Blessed One said:] "Monks, I will explain to you grasping and worrying, and also not grasping and not worrying... Here, monks, the uninstructed worldling, with no regard for Noble Ones,[1] unskilled and untrained in the Dhamma of the Noble Ones,... of those who are worthy[2]... regards body as the self, the self as having body, body as being in the self, or the self as being in the body. Change occurs to this man's body, and it becomes different. Because of this change and alteration in his body, his consciousness is preoccupied with bodily change. Due to this preoccupation with bodily change, worried thoughts arise and persist, laying a firm hold on his mind. Through this mental obsession he becomes fearful and distressed, and being full of desire[3] and attachment he is worried. He regards feeling as the self,... change occurs to his feeling... he is worried. [Similarly with 'perception,' 'the mental formations' and 'consciousness']. In this way, monks, grasping and worrying arise. And how, monks, do not grasping and not worrying arise? "Here, monks, the well-instructed Ariyan disciple, who has regard for the Noble Ones, is skilled and trained in the Dhamma of the Noble Ones,... of those who are worthy, does not regard body as the self, the self as having body, body as being in the self, or the self as being in the body. Change occurs to this man's body, and it becomes different, but despite this change and alteration in his body, his consciousness is not preoccupied with bodily change... Not being full of desire and attachment, he is not worried. [Similarly with 'feeling,’ 'perception,’ 'the mental formations' and 'consciousness']. In this way, monks, grasping and worrying do not arise."
