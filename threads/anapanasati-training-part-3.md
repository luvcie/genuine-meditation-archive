# Anapanasati Training (Part 3)

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 7 points · 1 year ago

(4) Know the breath in the whole body.

When you were doing the length of the breath, you already knew the breath in the whole body, where your entire body is breathing. When you reach this stage, you no longer care about the breathing in your nose. At this stage, you notice that your entire body’s cells, everywhere is breathing. You are filled with chi and your chi channels are all open.

It is extremely important to be able to notice that the entire body is breathing.

Here, you no longer talk about counting breaths or following breaths anymore. At this time, breath and thoughts have combined into one.

Don’t be fooled by what “Buddhists” teach at this point – If you get fooled that this is delusion, or any kind of teaching like this, you are done for.

If you can do these 3 steps, your body will maintain health and have a long life – your mind will be clear and your work will be smooth. Even if you age, or get sickness, the aged cells will start to be renewed. In Indian Yoga, it is said that your three channels of left, middle and right, are changing – so is every cell in your body.

If you have any delusions, don’t block the “knowing nature”, simply throw them away. Simply turn your mind towards the breath.

This is also known as in Taoism as “the three flowers reach the peak, the five chi move to the unmoveable”. The three flowers are the brain’s channels opening. The five chi are metal, wood, water, fire and earth – referring to the five organ-system in the body.

Don’t think that whatever I say here is easy to obtain! Even though the way I say it sounds theoretical and easy to understand, you will only understand it through practice. You will only know through cultivation. Stop theorizing and mentally trying to understand. Go and practice! It is only through many years of practice, struggling through cultivation, that you will know.
