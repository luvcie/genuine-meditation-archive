# Drop Everything

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 4 points · 2 years ago

Drop it completely!

Attainment is not the Tao.

Attainment is not prajna wisdom.

Attainment disappears. Prajna does not.

Drop emptiness. Drop the realms of experience. Drop this or that. Drop the idle thoughts.

The moment you perceive a state, that state is already gone. What else is there to cling on to?

Remember - attachment leads to suffering.

Even the idea of "dropping" - drop it!

The three seals of Dharma are:

1. No mind - emptiness

2. No form

3. No intention

A Chan master once said, "Can you make a mirror by polishing a brick?"

In the same way, can you make a Buddha by meditating?

Leave your idle and wandering thoughts.

Drop it completely!

The more you push them away, the more they grow. The more you grasp at dust floating in the air, the more you disturb the surrounding dust.

First stop is the mind. Second stop is your posture.

With discipline, only no proper samadhi and wisdom arise.

Stop playing with your thoughts.

Who are you?

Who is meditating?

Who are you when you were born?

Who are you when you die?

Who woke up this morning?

Who will go to sleep?

Attainment strokes the ego - it flares it up - pride ensnares you into the realm of Mara.

Drop it!

What use is all the knowledge you wish to have? What use is your logic when you die? What use is all the time you spend on meditation when you die? What use is your attainment?

This is the method of the old Chan masters - also known as a "hua tou".

A hua tou for one to contemplate on - one must have huge doubt when contemplating on this.

Drop your thoughts.

Day in, day out, contemplate the hua tou.

Seek within, not the outside.

Walk without being aware of walking; Talk without being aware of talking. Drop it completely!

Clean mind. Dust away, dust away.

Drop even the "dust away"!

No one will enlighten you.

It is only through your own wisdom.

Don't think "I want to get jhana" - "I want to get samadhi” - "Where is this going” - "Am I feeling bliss" - "Am I feeling joy" - Drop it completely!

Right now, your present thought has already flown away into the past. How can you grasp anything?

You think you see the present. But it has already gone away!

You say, I feel something but I am unsure what it is. That's because you are grasping, and it has already gone away!

Su Tung Po says: "The sound of the stream speaks into the whole universe. The form of the mountains is nothing but the form of pure dharma.”

Do not indulge in lip-service dharma.

Let it flow from the Tao.

The Surangama Sutra says: "The shadow of differentiation of things still remains even in inner quiet"

Drop the physical shell! Let your mind out through the crown! Not this body!

Boil a salty water bath and the water is purified. Where is your pure nature?

The moment you expect samadhi, you are no longer in samadhi.

When you place your legs in the crossed position... That's IT.

“The false nature spontaneously rests; When it rests, there is exactly Bodhi" -Surangama Sutra

Disregard the self and the self exists on its own.

Forget the self.

Does a flower need to know it is a flower?

Does a tree know itself as a tree?

Forget self for others. Fill up the entire universe - be one with heaven - the entire of space and the universe. Be one with Earth.

But when you think of it - drop it. It is no longer it!

Abide nowhere!

No form of a person, self, being or life. No ego, no personality, nothing. No nothing.

Do you get it yet?

Where is the common point where the five aggregates appear? Where do they disappear?

Who are you?
