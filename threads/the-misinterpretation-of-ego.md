# The Misinterpretation of Ego

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 10 points · 10 days ago

Friend, when you say "I am meditating"”, do not think that this "I" you refer yourself to is an Ego. This is the myth being perpetuated by many people outside who do not understand what this is.

The Ego is the function of the Manas, the 7th consciousness. The Manas operates together with four afflictions:

1. self-delusion - the source of all afflictions, taking the wrong things as the self.

2. self-view - view as a composite self.

3. self-conceit - generates contempt for others.

4. self-love - craving and addiction to the erroneous self.

Let me ask you then - if this I that you refer yourself to does not exist, how can you start any meditative action? How can this I negate anything that is impermanent and has no self-nature?

When you say I, it is the pure I. The question is understanding this I.

Is this I the body, the feelings, the thoughts or the mental objects/dhammas? No, this I is not them. These are the 'small self'.

The universe is one Big Self. It is also called God, Allah, Tao, Atma, energy, or buddha-nature. This Self is just one entire body, with everything encompassed within.

When Buddha said anatta, did he mean that there was no Self, no I? No, he merely said that there was no I to be found in these four foundations of mindfulness - No self-nature in body, no self-nature in feelings, no self-nature in thoughts, and no self-nature in dhammas.

At no point had the Buddha ever said there was no Big-Self, no I!

What is cultivation? Cultivation is removing the illusion of this small self. By removing this, you realize - oh, it has always been the Big Self, the universe.

As this Big Self, there is no small-self here or no small-self there. There is no Paramananda or Tom or Henry. As names, there they are. As composite-phenomena, there they are. But there is actually no Paramananda or Tom or Henry there. All the time, there has always only been the Big Self.

The four immeasurables all come from the Big Self. It comes from your I. When you shed the layers of illusion of the small-self, it naturally comes forth, because the You that you are, the I that you call yourself, is that Big Self. Ignorance is not realizing that, ignorance is taking the wrong things as the self!

If you think that the I does not exist, then you will face countless troubles in your cultivation. You will fall into a nihilistic view.

On the other hand, you should not immediately think that you see through it all, and that you are eternal. This is the false view of eternalism. You are still taking the wrong things as the self, hence you are still not the Big Self, and this is why you need to shed these layers of illusion.

Why then, did the Buddha say in the Diamond Sutra, that you should not cling onto a notion of an ego, personality, self and a life? Because if you have truly shed the illusion of a small self, you will have no more 'notions' of such things, and will act utterly as the Big Self.

Strive well, and may you be enlightened in this lifetime.

Awakening is realizing that you are the True-Self, and that the small-self is not the True-Self. Liberation is the cessation of these afflictions that obscure the True-Self, the ripening of prajna, of wisdom.
