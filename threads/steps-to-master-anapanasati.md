  From body... heaviness... aching... pain... cold... hot... itchiness... fatigue... lightness... relaxation... blissfulness... even neutral feelings...     - **refugeinbuddha** · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · 1 point · 2 years ago

      > What is 'compounded things' though?? Well I am personally not that sure... but my opinion is that it is something to do with the conditioning of mind...

      The five aggregates are the compounded things.

      Material form (rupa) is the first aggregate and is usually explained as a combination of the primordial elements of fire, earth, water and air. Things cannot exist on their own. They come together for a period of time and then fall away. A sandcastle is built on the beach by sand, but eventually it is washed away by the incoming tide. This is the body, including sense organs.

      Feeling (vedana) is the sensory phenomena derived from sense organs. Perception (sanna) is how this sensory phenomena is interpreted. Mental structures (sankhara) are thinking patterns, habits and mainly beliefs, down to the deepest rooted ones

      Cognition (vinnana) is the awareness that arises with sensory phenomena. This is related to the fetters such as narcissism, ill-will, doubt, clinging to rules and rituals and sensual lust.
