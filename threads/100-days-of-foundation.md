# 100 Days of Foundation

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 8 points · 1 month ago

To some familiar with Taoist alchemy, they speak of 100 days to 'lay the foundation'.

What exactly do 100 days mean? Is it literally 100 days? Here is another puzzle for one to solve, said by the Buddha:

> O monks, let alone seven years. Should any person practice these four foundations of mindfulness in this manner for six years... five years... four years... three years... two years... one year, then one of these two fruits may be expected by him: highest knowledge here and now, or if some remainder of clinging is yet present, the state of non-returning. O monks, let alone a year. Should any person practice these four foundations of mindfulness in this manner for seven months... six months... five months... four months... three months... two months... a month... half a month, then one of these two fruits may be expected by him: highest knowledge here and now, or if some remainder of clinging is yet present, the state of non-returning. O monks, let alone half a month. Should any person practice these four foundations of mindfulness in this manner for a week, then one of these two fruits may be expected by him: highest knowledge here and now, or if some remainder of clinging is yet present, the state of non-returning.

How do Zen monks attain the state of awakening in 7 days? How did the famous monk threatened by the emperor to awaken or be killed - awaken in 14 days?

Now this is the big question isn't it?

> He is the shepherd who left behind the ninety-nine sheep which had not strayed and went in search of that one which was lost. He rejoiced when he had found it. For ninety-nine is a number of the left hand, which holds it. The moment he finds the one, however, the whole number is transferred to the right hand. Thus it is with him who lacks the one, that is, the entire right hand which attracts that in which it is deficient, seizes it from the left side and transfers it to the right. In this way, then, the number becomes one hundred. This number signifies the Father.

From The Gospel of Truth.

The word 'Xi' in chinese is composed of two smaller word-particles that mean: "Self" and "Heart-mind". Combined, the word 'Xi' means the 'true breath’, the embryonic breath, that gives rise to the yuan-chi (original chi).

This true breath takes an entire lifetime to sustain. Therefore, a true breath lasts through an entire 100 days. What does time matter to primordial energy? These 100 days are here to tell you that you are to constantly keep the original spirit with you - you are to strive and never lose this energy, this shakti, this energy of Mother Nature.

You must be able to understand the Surangama Sutra and turn the light around, along with the hearing.

What is light? When one knows without discriminating, when one sees without discrimination, this is called 'light'. The moment one discriminates, defines, names, labels, conceptualizes, realizes, thinks - when one moves an additional step, this is no longer ‘'light’.

This light turns into discrimination, and it is lost in thoughts, which cannot be found. When thoughts stop, the mind shines bright with light - why? Because the discriminative mind has stopped! It is as if the realms have lit up with a brilliant sun, this is because thoughts have not obscured this.

What are objects?

They are the dust that drop on the Dharma-mirror. They are external things, material things. As long as the seeking is not turned inward, away from external things, you take external things as the self, and you lose yourself in mis-identification of your nature with things that are not you.

If the mind's discrimination is not stopped, the thoughts cannot empty and the elixir cannot form.

If you see emptiness as empty, this is still discrimination. Only when one is empty, but does not see it as empty - then we can say that the mind is truly empty. This is the secret of the Heart Sutra.

The stimulation of the senses, following outward from the seven apertures of the body - this is Fire (yang). The withdrawal from these seven apertures is the Water principle (yin). If one is able to withdraw, unaffected by stimuli without discrimination, sitting in deep samadhi, then this is intersection of yin and yang.

When one gathers this energy for a period of time, it will eventually fill the heart with light. To be free of "dust" is to empty the heart completely.

The ending of discrimination is the seeing of basic-reality. The Water-Cleansing pearl, the original nature, will appear.

Like thunder, the endless afflictions vanish.

What does it mean to sit in front of the Wall for nine years? Nine signifies the Yang principle, how would anyone understand this without developing wisdom?

The original nature, the Higher Self, sends down the great Yang. Only when one is purely Yang, can the original nature return.

Effort is needed to turn the light around - use the Xi to stabilize in the Central Chamber - eventually, slowly, one attains transmutation and communion with the original nature. Quiet mind until it is forgotten, and the energy will congeal, thus does the Philosopher’'s Stone produce the Life-giving Elixir.

There is so much given in here. If you do not practice, it is a huge pity.

Be like lead - firm, steadfast, unwavering in practice. Lead can then be turned to gold.

After turning the light around, even the method is thrown away - as Buddha said, upon reaching the shore with the Dharma, throw away the Dharma-raft.

The alchemical process that happens after this can only be guided by an enlightened master - this has always been the oral tradition kept by lineages. As such, I am no one's teacher, because I cannot guide you as such - you must find a master, you must accumulate the Work and the merit, then the teacher will find you.

Lao-Tze sits on his mule. The animal does its thing in daily life, but the mind is in Tao. One's face will shine with radiance.

I may have even done a disservice if you retain these words intellectually without any practice. If that is your purpose, destroy these words and burn them in the fire. What is the use of conceptualizing a path that you will never walk?

If there is anything that you must know, you must stop before discriminating. That is the primary instruction.
