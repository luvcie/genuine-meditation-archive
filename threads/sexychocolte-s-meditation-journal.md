  > Also every time i meditate I get pulses of medium level tingling throughout my body. It feels pretty good but it also can get sort of annoying, any idea what it could be?

  It's a jhana-nimitta, so it's a good sign. Jhana-nimitta is the sign of absorption, so it means you're getting better at following the breath and gross distraction is decreasing significantly. It is only annoying because you need to let bliss arise. I suggest you (re)-read this post by refuge. Good luck!

  Tightness in body/neck/wherever can be helped through physical exercise: especially Yoga asanas in my experience are helpful for physical pains/tightness, but also running, walking, etc.

  > For a while now there has been this pervasive tightness in my neck, head and face when I try to concentrate the on the breath or anything in general.

  Observing the tightness is the first step. Calming physical agitations is the second. On the out-breath, consciously relax those areas. It helps to drop the shoulders and retract backwards.

  > Also every time i meditate I get pulses of medium level tingling throughout my body.

  That is the physical jhana-nimitta starting to arise. For now, focus on steadying the mental state through the breath. When the breath is subtle and unobservable as it gradually slows, then find where the bliss is, and switch the focus to it.
