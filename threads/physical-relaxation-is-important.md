# Physical relaxation is important

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 9 points · 1 year ago

Body and mind are linked. Stress on the body can affect mind state, and vice versa. Therefore, if you want to go into deep states, physical relaxation first by doing a body scan from head to toe, relaxing all bodily agitations along with the exhale will allow the body to become calm. Now while the body is relaxed, you want to make sure your posture can hold by itself without any straining of muscles or tightness.

Next, realise that you are merely observing. Let things come as they come, go as they go. If you find yourself straining if you focus on the breath, use the inward and outward contraction/expansion of the body along with the breath to note the rising/falling, then go back to the breath.

If you do it correctly, one can enter the cessation of breath within minutes. There is no need to wait for hours if one does it rightly. Immediately you can enter xi and extreme stillness. However, this really depends on your mental state, on whether the mind is relatively free of thoughts or not. For a beginner, the moment you sit down and before the thoughts start to come in, that moment is it.

Normally, the body will start to slowly and sippingly take in air in a slow manner, such that you cannot exhale. Then it fills the lungs like full bags and the body's yang chi rises and tries to clear the way of blockages. Do not panic - simply let go and see them as phenomena. If you can do this well, the states of itchiness or heat or various other effects will just pass on by. Simply let go.
