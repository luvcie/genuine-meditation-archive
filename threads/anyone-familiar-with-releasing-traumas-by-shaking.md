# Anyone familiar with releasing traumas by shaking?

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **Ebtn** · *(OCR)* · `Question` · 3 points · 10 months ago

I'm asking because I could use any input. It has been my greatest tool for letting go so far. It has also worked for physiological sensations like eye pain and headaches caued by anxiety.

The popular name is TRE (Trauma release exercises). The method is to make yourself feel like shaking and then let your body shake away the emotion. My way of causing the shaking is to resist the emotion I want to let go, become aware of the the shape of the feeling in my body, feel the most overwhelming part I can handle and then let my body shake. I make sure to stay in the feeling with my awareness and not jump into thoughts.

I would love your input.
