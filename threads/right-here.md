# Right Here

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advice` · 9 points · 1 year ago

If you know just this body alone, you know the universe. Why? Because the microcosm holds the macrocosm. If anything is affected in one cell, it affects something else in the macrocosm.

The Ouroboros is a snake eating its own tail. What people think of it is that they have to go an entire journey, then come back to the head again when they reach the tail.

Why do such a long path? Why not just drop everything right now?

There are so many errant paths out there that lead nowhere.

Right here is where the head and tail is. The circle of Zen shows you that one brush stroke starts and ends at the same point.

If you can just surrender, right here and right now, you will see it.

Nature moving by itself is perfect in its own way. Letting the immune system work by itself, it restores the body back to perfect function. We simply need to get out of the way - stop eating bad food, stop eating bad mental food, etc.

A huge part of cultivation is the purification of the mind. This is what the Buddha calls perfect, right virtue.

Do not become entangled with "lower virtues". Thinking that things can only be done in a single way but not understanding why it is done is too, foolish.

What is perfect virtue?

Instead of that, we can talk more about what is imperfect virtue. Taking advantage of others, expecting compensation from others from your deeds, being dishonest, blaming others for their karmic repercussions, wanting fame, wanting power, efc.

The first component that the Buddha taught was sila - discipline.

It is not so simple. Sila, itself, can bring you nearly all the way.

Being humble, living within the heart, knowing that you don't know is important. The ego is important for directing it in various ways to accomplish various tasks, but if one does not realize the immense potential of being a full human being, this chance of being a human being will be squandered.

If you build the rainbow body and fly off to the constellations, you still need to be able to teach beings how to become human beings! The highest wisdom is right here in front of you. You need to be able to balance your material and spiritual life!

Don't be selectively virtuous. Virtue is universal, regardless of whatever you do. It does not matter whether an enemy has raised a gun to your head. A true, accomplished disciple of the Buddha will never give in. Nor will aversion stir in

one.

Don't grasp for power over nature or others. Don't believe yourself to be superior to others. That is all the work of an ego. Rinse them away. You are nothing!

It is your clouded mind that blocks you from experiencing the truth. Make the right choices, and it will move you to right fruit.

My dear dharma friends, the masters have never lied. But don't even regard them as "superior beings", except for that they have mastered their own limitations and awakened. Learn from their teachings and they will be honoured.

Ask yourself - what can you truly take from this life when you die? Whatever you do, the truth cannot be hidden from yourself.

Positive virtue is already half of the battle won - your mind and heart connects by an energetic channel, allowing the child light to merge with the empty mind.

So do not just treat meditation as a sitting sport. The entire Noble Eightfold Path must be practiced.
