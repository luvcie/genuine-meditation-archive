# Sweet saliva running down the back of my throat

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **analphaguy** · *(OCR)* · `Question` · 6 points · 2 years ago

I haven't posted much, but I was wondering if Paramananda or any other advanced cultivators could shed some light on this:

I meditate regularly to second-third jhana (using either the Breath or Body sati meditations here). At this point sensory data would become like thought objects, so while i was mindful of their arising, I am still aware of them. At this point I realized there was this cool liquid flowing down the back of the throat.

I googled and research talked about it being "sweet nectar" or ambroxia. This usually goes along with ecstasy.

> At this point I realized there was this cool liquid flowing down the back of the throat.

Yes, this is usually when the bliss has reached a very intense level throughout the whole body. Some yogi say that this is a over-spilling of the cerebrospinal fluid to the back of the throat. This is normally accompanied by the tongue moving spontaneously backwards past the soft palate (which retracts) to the nasal cavity. Therefore, it is not saliva, nor mucus, nor sinus discharge.

This is often called the 'nectar of the gods' or amrta in Sanskrit. Food of the gods seems a little too fantastic a name, just like many old lores that come from a misunderstanding of the things that happen. However, it is not the liquid that confers bliss, but the state of the entire nervous system that resulted in that spilling. This is usually the after-effects of a good meditation session. We can also call this sweet-tasting sensation a jhana-nimitta, a gustatory sign. It can sometimes be accompanied by a fragrant smell, which is the olfactory sign.

Great work. More has to be developed, so strive well.
