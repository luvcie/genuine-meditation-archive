# Siddhis (Supernatural Abilities) Aroused by Deep Samadhi

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advanced` · 6 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2ttxhx/siddhis_supernatural_abilities_aroused_by_deep/>

You might have often seen that in the texts of many sages and prophets, they have repeatedly performed miracles that were quite difficult to believe, especially in modern society.

The Buddha called these abilities Siddhis, which are attainments from spiritual cultivation. The following list is compiled from many sources, including Hinduism and grouped accordingly. Personal experience is also added.

(A) The below group of Siddhis are all what one experiences when you have generated the astral body from will (mind-generated body). In the astral body, the body is much more volatile as compared to the physical counterpart and hence its properties can be more easily transformed.

1. Contracting the body to an atom / Expanding the body to extremely large sizes

2. Becoming infinitely heavy / Becoming almost weightless

3. Having access to all places / Teleporting to wherever thought goes

4. Assuming any form desired
5. Entering the bodies of others

6. Witnessing and participating in the pastimes of gods

7. Walking on water
8. Passing through walls
9. Going to as far as the Brahma realms

(B) This group of Siddhis are possible to be used in the physical body, although it has a full-scale counterpart in the astral body.

1. Knowing the past, present and future. (Clairvoyance)

2. Knowing the minds of others (Clairaudience)

3. Seeing or hearing things at extreme distances (Remote Viewing)

4. Generating an astral body from mind

5. Experiencing low-level siddhis as jhana-nimitta

6. Insight stream (Intuition and insight)

7. Feeling synchronicities and guidances (Intuitive Guidance)

8. Energy manipulation

(C) This group of Siddhis are birthed as a result from reaching fourth Jhana and attaining the factor of equanimity (neither pain nor pleasure).

1. Undisturbed by hunger, thirst, cravings.

2. Undisturbed by pain from lack of mystic knowledge.

3. Undisturbed by pain from clinging to materialistic gains.

4. Undisturbed by pain from karmic ripening or 'fate'.

(D) This last group of Siddhis are possible but yet are side-paths. People who indulge in the following are fools as they have side-tracked from the path of enlightenment.

1. Realizing what one desires
2. Absolute rulership, subjugating charisma
3. Tolerance of heat, cold and extreme dualities

4. Knowing influences of fire, sun, water, poison, etc.

5. Resistance against conquer
6. Deliberate death

7. Extending lifespans to near-immortality (still mortal)

Please be aware that all of these abilities are all within one's grasp, but never should be the main purpose of going into deep spiritual advancement. One should only look at cultivation for one single purpose: Enlightenment, liberation from the endless suffering.

This post is only to verify phenomena that advanced practitioners have experienced and are confused about.
