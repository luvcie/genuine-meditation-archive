# The Immaterial Domains, Galaxies and Outer-Space

**r/genuinemeditation** · by **refugeinbuddha** · *(OCR)* · *परमानन्द Paramananda* · `Advanced` · 3 points · 2 years ago

<https://www.reddit.com/r/genuinemeditation/comments/2ttibq/the_immaterial_domains_galaxies_and_outerspace/>

Note: This topic might be too advanced for some of the basic practitioners. I must advice you that the foundations of enlightenment is in the first four Jhanas, so please do not be distracted. If you wish to read this, then treat it as a way to clear your doubts about the Buddhist teachings.

The fifth samadhi, the first immaterial absorption, "Realm/Domain of Infinite Space", is usually experienced when one develops a mind-generated body (body from will).

The ancient people liked to gaze at the skies, savouring its divinity. There is actually much truth in that. The stars, were what they took to be devas or angels.

How did the word 'angel' come about? It came from the word 'angle', hence the degree of perspective of each star indicates that a particular star is of a certain angle. This was then indicated as the word 'angel'. Where is this heaven then? It is of course, space, also known in many mysticisms as "akasha". In other words, there is no difference between heaven and outer-space.

In fact, many ancient spiritual practitioners have talked about our own Milky Way galaxy, likening it to that of a cloud hanging in the middle of the dense blackness of space. In fact, meditations like these were taught even in the mysticism of early Christianity.

One interesting fact to note is that while the various planets and moons seem to be uninhabitable as deemed by "modern" science, there were millions of beings, often that of astral nature. While they do not have physical bodies, they do have astral bodies. For each world, there was also a particular deity or demigod who was in charge of governance in the world.

To move into the sixth samadhi, "Realm/Domain of infinite consciousness", one then needs to recede from these beings and view the full expanse of all beings. When doing so, you are suddenly able to perceive the many "consciousnesses" living their samsara cycles on planets, stars, etc.

To move into seventh samadhi, "Realm/Domain of nothingness", even these stars, planets, all turn into fine points of light radiating expansive love. At this point, these "things" turn into "nothing".

To move yet even further into eight samadhi, "Realm/Domain of neither perception nor non-perception", one lets go of even that experience, and merges one's consciousness with the whole mass of light and love. This is the very point that I am exploring right now. Cutting the fetter, I am then able to go into nibbana.

The way to enlightenment, is becoming one with this mass of light, merging with this very fabric of this universe, and hence merging into the very fabric of your physical body, transforming it.

In a way, you can say that once enlightened, every single cell of that enlightened being's body can represent the point of everyone's consciousness in the whole galaxy of the Milky Way.

So strive on my friend.

Know that while you are being enlightened, there is really no enlightenment - Just a realization of the truth, resulting in the acquisition of the three bodies of Dharmakaya, Sambhogaya and Nirmanakaya.

This is also why the Diamond Sutra, Subhuti replied:

"No, lord. There is no such thing as Perfect Enlightenment to obtain. If a Perfectly Enlightened Buddha were to say to himself, 'I am enlightened' he would be admitting there is an individual person, a separate self and personality, and would therefore not be a Perfectly Enlightened Buddha."

I hope this post has inspired you, which I hope it does.

Also, remember that knowledge is not wisdom. One needs to goes through the experience to truly understand what it really is. Hearing the words of an adventurer is not the same as experiencing that knowledge for yourself.

That is the meaning of being enlightened.

Now do you understand why it is so important to develop the four jhanas, before the higher four samadhis?

Enlightenment is truly right here.
